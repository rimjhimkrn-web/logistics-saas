This Legal Notice applies to the public KRIM Logistics website and related digital information made available by KRIM. It should be read together with the Terms & Conditions, Privacy Notice, Cookie Notice, Refund & Cancellation Policy and Shipping & Delivery Policy.

1. Business identity

KRIM Logistics is the business name used for the logistics technology, coordination and related services described on this website. KRIM currently operates as a sole proprietorship. A trade name is not a separate incorporated company or limited-liability entity.

The exact contracting party, proprietor details, address, tax registration particulars and authorised signatory must be stated in the applicable customer contract, partner agreement, tax invoice or other legally required document. Do not rely on a website brand name as a substitute for those particulars.

2. Website operator and contact

For general website, service and legal enquiries, use the KRIM Contact page. Formal notices should clearly identify the sender, relevant shipment or transaction reference (if any), the issue and the remedy or response requested.

Where a law requires additional operator, grievance or statutory particulars to be displayed, KRIM must publish the verified details and keep them current. This notice does not invent or replace any such statutory designation.

3. Scope of this website

This website provides information about KRIM's platform concept, intended workflows, service categories, network participation and available digital tools. Content may include product descriptions, educational material, indicative process explanations and public announcements.

Features, services, routes, locations and transport modes may be under development, subject to partner onboarding, legal approvals or operational availability. A page describing a capability does not by itself establish that the capability is active or available for booking.

4. Accuracy and updates

KRIM aims to keep website information accurate and current, but operational details, product features, market coverage, partner availability, timelines and policies may change. Information is provided subject to correction, update and availability.

Before relying on a price, service scope, transit estimate, eligibility statement or operating commitment, obtain a current written quotation or shipment-specific confirmation that identifies the responsible contracting and performing parties.

5. Quotes, bookings and transactions

A website enquiry, account registration, quote request, partner bid, automated estimate or draft booking is not by itself an accepted shipment or a promise that transport capacity is reserved. A shipment proceeds only after the relevant parties, service scope, price, documents, legal checks and acceptance requirements have been confirmed through the applicable workflow.

Customer-facing terms, the Refund & Cancellation Policy, Shipping & Delivery Policy and shipment-specific work order govern an actual transaction, subject to mandatory law. If website content conflicts with a signed contract or mandatory legal requirement, the applicable contract and law take precedence to the extent legally permitted.

6. Platform and independent service providers

KRIM may provide digital tools to help customers discover, request, compare or coordinate logistics services and to help independent providers present capabilities or respond to opportunities. The exact role of KRIM and each provider must be disclosed for the relevant transaction.

Partner labels, marketplace descriptions or platform terminology do not determine legal status. They do not remove any licence, registration, consumer, carrier, tax, safety or contractual obligation that applies to KRIM or a service provider based on actual activities.

Operational and legal gate: Before enabling paid marketplace-style bookings, KRIM must obtain written advice on the applicability of the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 and the Carriage by Road Act, 2007, including the business-model and registration implications for a sole proprietorship. A partner-led structure alone does not settle those questions.

Those details must be verified against the actual business and displayed in the form and location required by applicable law before any regulated marketplace activity is enabled.

A private residential address must not be replaced with a fictitious address or an address used without lawful authority. Publication and launch gate: This notice is not a substitute for mandatory operator disclosures. A general contact page alone may not satisfy those requirements.

14. E-commerce compliance readiness

The Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 may apply depending on the actual digital business model and activities. The 2021 amendment substituted Rule 4(1), including the specified company or qualifying foreign-company/office/branch/agency requirement for an e-commerce entity. KRIM's present sole-proprietorship structure creates a material eligibility question if its activities fall within that regime. The legal classification and available lawful structure must be confirmed by an Indian lawyer before marketplace-style paid bookings or transactions are launched.

The Department of Consumer Affairs has announced the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) (Amendment) Rules, 2026, effective 1 January 2027. The Government's published summary describes additional requirements concerning complaint records, search-result integrity, sponsored-listing disclosures, price-reduction transparency, dark-pattern self-audits, marketplace seller/product information, consumer information and bundled fees. KRIM must assess the actual notified text and prepare applicable controls before that effective date; this page does not certify compliance.

Where applicable, grievance processes must meet the statutory acknowledgement and redress timelines and display the required officer particulars. KRIM must not imply that an officer has been appointed until a named, authorised person and working contact channel have actually been designated.

Do not activate marketplace checkout solely because the website or payment gateway works. Legal form, platform classification, operator disclosures, grievance handling, consumer terms, payment collection and partner contracts must be assessed together.

15. Road carriage regulation

The Carriage by Road Act, 2007 regulates common carriers. Sections 3 and 4 address the prohibition on carrying on the business of a common carrier without the required certificate of registration and the application process. Whether KRIM's activities—such as accepting bookings, contracting for carriage, arranging or undertaking transport, acting as an agent or broker, or issuing transport documents—fall within the statutory definition depends on the actual facts and legal interpretation.

A customer-facing technology platform or partner-led operating model does not automatically remove regulatory obligations. Before KRIM accepts or arranges paid road-freight work, obtain written advice from an Indian transport-law professional on the definition, registration, documentation, liability, state/central authority and the precise roles of KRIM and each carrier.
