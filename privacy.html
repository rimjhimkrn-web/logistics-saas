Privacy & information handling

Your information deserves clear handling.

This notice explains the types of personal, account, enquiry and shipment-related information KRIM Logistics may process, why it may be needed, when it may be shared and how you can contact us about it. We aim to collect information relevant to the service or request.

India-first operationsPurpose-led information useUpdated 4 November 2026
Implementation and legal review note: This is an operational privacy notice, not legal advice or a certification of compliance. Before relying on it as final, KRIM must verify live data flows, processors, retention periods, the designated privacy contact and any cross-border transfers. The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and Rules, 2025 have phased commencement; provisions applicable to a particular activity must be checked against current Gazette notifications and commencement dates.

Read this notice with any specific notice shown when you submit an enquiry, create an account, request a quote, make a payment or use a particular KRIM workflow. A specific notice may explain a particular data use in more detail.

1. Scope and who operates KRIM

This notice applies to the KRIM Logistics website, digital platform, customer and partner workflows, enquiry channels and related logistics coordination activities operated under the KRIM Logistics brand. KRIM’s commercial focus is India-first; international workflows are subject to country, route, partner and regulatory validation.

“KRIM”, “we”, “us” or “our” means the business operating under the KRIM Logistics trade name in India. A carrier, warehouse, customs broker, payment provider or other supplier may have separate privacy responsibilities when it independently determines how it uses information.

Operator disclosure: The exact legal-operator details and designated privacy/grievance contact must be confirmed and published before this notice is treated as final. Do not send identity documents or sensitive information through a general enquiry unless specifically requested through an appropriate channel.

3. How and why information may be used

Depending on the interaction and applicable law, information may be used to respond to enquiries; assess shipment requirements, route feasibility and partner capability; administer accounts and access; coordinate accepted services; initiate or reconcile payments; provide updates and support; protect the platform and investigate misuse; maintain audit, tax and accounting records; and improve platform reliability and workflows.

We do not treat a general enquiry as permission for unrelated marketing. Where consent is the appropriate basis for optional processing or communications, it should be requested separately and may be withdrawn through the stated channel.

4. When information may be shared

KRIM does not publish customer shipment details as public information. Relevant information may be disclosed for the requested workflow, a necessary business purpose or a legal requirement. Recipient categories may include:

  • Operating partners: carriers, fleet operators, warehouses and other suppliers who need relevant details to assess or perform an accepted task.
  • Technology providers: hosting, database, authentication, security, communication and platform-support services.
  • Payment providers: services handling payment instructions, confirmations, refunds or disputes.
  • Professional advisers: legal, accounting, audit or insurance advisers where needed.
  • Authorities: government, law-enforcement, courts or regulators where required by applicable law.

We aim to limit disclosure to what is relevant. A service partner may have its own legal and contractual responsibilities for information it receives. Where a provider processes information on KRIM’s behalf, its contract, access scope, security arrangements and processing location must be verified and documented. A verified live-provider list is not yet published in this notice.

Partner visibility: A carrier or other partner should receive only operational details needed for an opportunity or assignment made available to it. Do not assume every prospective partner can view every customer or shipment record.

5. Payments and financial information

Where online payment is offered, an external payment provider may handle the payment flow. KRIM may receive transaction identifiers, payment status, amount, currency and reconciliation information. The provider may collect payment credentials under its own terms and privacy notice.

Never send card numbers, UPI PINs, banking passwords or one-time passwords to KRIM through a form, email or chat. Payment status remains subject to reconciliation with the relevant order or transaction.

6. Storage locations and international transfers

Technology providers may store or process information in data centres in India or other countries, depending on their configuration. International shipment coordination may also require limited details to be shared with a service provider abroad for a movement separately validated and accepted.

KRIM must confirm actual hosting regions, provider locations and applicable transfer safeguards before international data flows are enabled. A global-ready architecture does not mean every international route or country is currently commercially available.

7. How long information is retained

Retention depends on the information type, purpose, account or shipment status, security needs and applicable legal or accounting requirements. KRIM’s intended approach is to retain information only as long as reasonably necessary, then securely delete, anonymise or otherwise dispose of it where appropriate.

Specific periods for enquiries, inactive accounts, shipment records, payment records, security logs and partner documents must be set in an internal retention schedule and checked against actual database and provider settings. Legal, tax, fraud-prevention or dispute requirements may require longer retention.

8. Security and access controls

KRIM aims to use proportionate technical and organisational safeguards, which may include access permissions, authentication controls, logging, secure connections, backups, rate limits and provider security features. Access should be limited according to role and business need.

No website or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Controls must be checked against the live platform and connected providers; this notice is not proof that every safeguard is enabled or independently tested. If you believe your account or information has been accessed improperly, contact KRIM promptly. Never include passwords or authentication codes in your report.

9. Your choices and privacy requests

Depending on applicable law and the commencement of relevant provisions, you may have rights or choices relating to access, correction, updating, erasure, consent withdrawal or grievance redressal. Some records may need to be retained where a legal obligation or necessary operational reason applies.

  • Access or correction: identify the account, enquiry or record and explain what should be reviewed.
  • Deletion: request deletion where available and no lawful retention exception applies.
  • Consent withdrawal: use the channel stated for the relevant consent-based processing.
  • Grievance: describe the concern and the interaction so it can be reviewed.

KRIM may need to verify identity or authority and ask for clarification. The designated privacy/grievance contact and response procedure must be formally confirmed and published before public launch.

10. Cookies, logs and device information

The site may use essential browser storage, session technologies, security logs and similar technical data to operate features, remember preferences, protect accounts and diagnose errors. Third-party services linked from or embedded in the site may use their own technologies.

KRIM must audit the live site for analytics, advertising pixels, embedded media and other non-essential trackers before publishing a complete cookie inventory or promising granular cookie controls. Browser settings can manage cookies, although blocking essential storage may affect functionality.

11. Children

KRIM’s logistics and business workflows are intended for people who can lawfully use the relevant service. We do not knowingly seek children’s personal information through general marketing or enquiry forms. If you believe a child’s information was submitted improperly, contact us for review.

12. Information across KRIM’s nine user types

KRIM’s platform is designed for nine distinct user types. The examples below describe possible operational context, not a promise that every workspace or feature is currently enabled.

  • Customer / Shipper: contact details, shipment instructions, quotes, documents and transaction references.
  • Logistics Partner / Carrier: organisation, service area, capacity, verification and accepted assignment information.
  • Driver: assignment details, relevant contact and delivery instructions, milestones and proof-of-delivery records.
  • Warehouse Operator: facility, handling, inventory and inbound/outbound coordination records.
  • Customs & Trade: shipment and trade documents needed for a validated coordination task.
  • Finance: invoice, payment, refund, reconciliation and settlement records relevant to a transaction.
  • Operations Control Tower: shipment milestones, exceptions and coordination records required to manage operations.
  • Enterprise: organisation, authorised user, shipment and workflow information within its permitted scope.
  • Platform Administrator: restricted internal access for platform administration, subject to separate authorisation and audit controls; not a public account or support route.

Role labels alone do not grant access. Actual access must be enforced by authenticated identity, organisation membership, server-side permissions and applicable data policies. Do not assume a partner or other user can see every shipment or customer record.

13. Reporting a suspected privacy or security issue

If you suspect that your account, shipment information or other personal information has been exposed or accessed improperly, contact KRIM through the Contact page and select “Privacy or data request”. Describe the affected workflow and approximate time, but do not include passwords, one-time codes, payment credentials or unnecessary identity documents.

KRIM must assess the report, preserve relevant records, restrict access where appropriate and determine any notification or reporting duties under the law applicable at the time. This notice does not promise a particular response time or confirm that a specific incident-response process has been independently tested.

14. Changes and how to contact KRIM

We may revise this notice when workflows, providers, legal requirements or data practices change. The date at the top indicates the latest revision. Material changes should be communicated through an appropriate website or account notice where required.

For a privacy question or request, use the KRIM Contact page and select “Privacy or data request”. Include enough context to identify the interaction. Do not send passwords, OTPs, payment credentials or unnecessary identity documents.

Required before final publication: complete a live data-flow and vendor audit; confirm legal operator name and service address; designate a privacy/grievance contact; set retention periods; verify hosting and transfer locations; and ensure consent and request-handling records match the backend. This page alone does not prove those controls are implemented.

Need to ask about your information?

Use the privacy enquiry type on the KRIM Contact page. Share only the details needed to identify your request.
