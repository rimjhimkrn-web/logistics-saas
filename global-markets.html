Market readiness

A country is not a route.
A route is not a booking.

KRIM separates platform design, market configuration, partner validation and operational activation. This keeps customer promises aligned with what can genuinely be delivered.

STAGE 01

Designed

The platform can support a market or capability conceptually. No local service availability is implied.

STAGE 02

Configured

Country settings, workflows, currencies, languages and applicable requirements are entered and reviewed.

STAGE 03

Partner validated

Provider credentials, route coverage, capacity, commercial terms and responsibilities are checked.

STAGE 04 · GATED

Operational activation

Not yet commercially active. Execution, verified providers, pricing, payment, customer support, exception handling and delivery evidence must pass controlled validation before bookings are enabled.

i
KRIM's availability rule

A route is presented as bookable only when the required transport legs, providers, pricing, regulatory checks and customer-support ownership are confirmed. Otherwise, the customer submits an enquiry for review—not a confirmed booking.

  • Domestic customer support and exception ownership

International markets

Connect cross-border origin, transit and destination capabilities through qualified forwarders, customs specialists and local operators.

  • Country-specific provider onboarding
  • Trade-lane and customs requirements
  • Local payment, tax and support readiness
  • End-to-end validation before market activation

Global platform foundations

Six foundations for every market.

A global logistics operating system needs more than a country selector. It needs consistent core records with explicit local configuration.

01 / GEOGRAPHY

Countries, regions & lanes

Separate a country's configuration from enabled locations and from a supported origin-to-destination service lane.

02 / LOCAL EXPERIENCE

Language, time & addresses

Local language, time zones, date formats, address structures, phone formats and customer communications.

03 / TRANSPORT

Multimodal execution

Road, rail, ocean, air and warehousing with mode-specific milestones, equipment, documents and hand-offs.

04 / FINANCE

Currency & settlement

Explicit quote, invoice, charge and settlement currencies with traceable FX source, timestamp, fees and rounding.

05 / COMPLIANCE

Trade & local obligations

Customs, permits, restricted goods, sanctions, export controls, tax, privacy, retention and insurance checks.

06 / GOVERNANCE

Security & accountability

Tenant isolation, least-privilege access, partner boundaries, auditable changes and incident handling.
