Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Cancellation Before Shipment Assignment

A customer may request cancellation before a shipment has been assigned to a logistics partner. Any eligible refund will be processed according to the applicable payment and refund terms.

2. Cancellation After Partner Assignment

Once a logistics partner has been assigned, cancellation may be subject to applicable operational charges, transportation commitments or other costs already incurred.

3. Cancellation After Pickup

Once goods have been picked up by the logistics partner, cancellation may not be available. Any applicable refund will be determined after considering transportation costs and services already provided.

4. Refund Eligibility

Refunds may be available when a service is cancelled before the service is substantially performed, subject to applicable charges and the circumstances of the shipment.

5. Failed or Cancelled Shipments

If a shipment cannot be completed because of circumstances outside our reasonable control, we may review the case and determine any eligible refund or alternative arrangement.

6. Payment Processing Charges

Where permitted by applicable law and payment-provider rules, certain payment processing or transaction charges may not be refundable.

7. Refund Processing Time

Approved refunds will be initiated through the applicable payment method or payment provider. The time required for the refund to appear in the customer's account may depend on the payment provider or bank.

8. Damaged or Missing Goods

Claims relating to damaged, lost or missing goods may require separate investigation and supporting documentation. Such claims are not automatically treated as payment cancellation requests.

9. Fraudulent or Unauthorized Transactions

Transactions suspected to be fraudulent or unauthorized may be investigated and may be subject to applicable payment-provider and legal procedures.

10. How to Request a Cancellation or Refund

Customers should contact us with their shipment tracking number, registered contact information and reason for the cancellation or refund request.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cancellation & Refund Policy from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page.

12. Contact Us

For cancellation or refund questions, please contact us using the contact information provided on our website.
