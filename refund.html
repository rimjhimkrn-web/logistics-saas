Customer care & payment terms

Fair decisions. Clear resolutions.

This policy explains how KRIM reviews quote withdrawals, shipment changes, cancellations, payment issues and refund requests. The outcome depends on the shipment stage, the confirmed booking terms, the party that collected the payment and applicable law.

The booking confirmation must state who contracts to provide the logistics service, who receives each payment, the applicable cancellation schedule, and who is responsible for deciding and issuing each refund.

1. Scope and core principles

This policy applies to cancellation and refund requests relating to KRIM platform workflows and any shipment service arranged through KRIM, to the extent KRIM is responsible for that part of the transaction. A service provider may have separate terms, but those terms must be made available before the customer accepts the booking.

  • We assess each request using the booking record, shipment stage, service-provider evidence, payment status and applicable law.
  • We do not promise a refund merely because a request is submitted, and we do not reject a valid request solely because a shipment has progressed.
  • Any amount retained or deducted must have a lawful, disclosed and supportable basis.
  • Mandatory consumer rights and remedies prevail over any inconsistent term in this policy.

2. Quote requests and unaccepted offers

Submitting a shipment enquiry, requesting a quote or receiving an indicative estimate does not by itself create a transport booking or payment obligation. A customer may withdraw an unaccepted request without a shipment cancellation charge. If a payment was collected in error before a booking was accepted, contact KRIM so the transaction can be checked and any amount due can be returned through the appropriate payment route.

A partner bid, draft quote, price estimate or availability indication is not a confirmed booking unless the platform or authorised contracting party issues an explicit acceptance/booking confirmation.

3. Customer cancellation requests

Customers should submit a cancellation request as soon as possible using the KRIM Contact page and include the shipment or booking reference. KRIM will record the time of the request and check whether the provider has accepted the work, allocated resources, dispatched a vehicle, collected cargo or begun another billable service.

The cancellation terms shown in the accepted booking confirmation govern any disclosed, lawful cancellation charge. If no applicable cancellation charge was disclosed before acceptance, KRIM will not invent one after the event. A charge must reflect a valid contractual or legal basis and be supported by relevant records; it cannot be a disguised penalty or an undisclosed fee.

4. Review by shipment stage

Before provider acceptance

Normally the request can be withdrawn without a provider service charge. Any captured payment that is not due must be reviewed for return.

After provider acceptance, before dispatch

We check documented commitments or costs already incurred and the cancellation schedule accepted at booking. Only lawful, previously disclosed amounts may be considered.

After dispatch or pickup

Transport, vehicle deployment, loading, return, storage or other completed services may affect the amount recoverable. The provider must supply the applicable booking terms and supporting records.

During transit or after delivery

A cancellation may no longer be operationally possible. A service failure, loss, damage, delay or other complaint is reviewed separately under the booking, provider liability terms, insurance and mandatory law.

These are review stages, not automatic outcomes or fixed fee bands. The exact service stage and applicable charges must be evidenced for the particular shipment.

5. Partner cancellation or service failure

If a service provider cancels, cannot perform, materially changes the agreed service or fails to attend, KRIM will notify the customer when reasonably practicable and review available alternatives, such as a replacement provider, revised schedule or cancellation. The customer is not required to accept a materially different service without agreement.

Where the customer is entitled to a refund because the provider did not perform the paid service, the responsible contracting/payment-collecting party must arrange the refund in accordance with the booking terms and applicable law. KRIM will coordinate and share relevant transaction records where the service was arranged through its platform. This does not make KRIM the refund debtor for funds it did not collect or control, nor does it remove any liability KRIM may have under its actual role or applicable law.

6. When a refund may be due

Depending on the facts and applicable law, a full or partial refund may be considered where:

  • a payment was duplicated, collected in error or exceeded the amount properly due;
  • a booking was not accepted or the paid service was not provided;
  • the responsible provider cancels or cannot perform and no agreed alternative is accepted;
  • a material service failure gives rise to a refund or price adjustment under the booking or applicable law;
  • a customer cancellation qualifies under the disclosed booking schedule; or
  • a refund is otherwise required by law, a payment-network rule or a binding decision.

Each case is assessed individually. Where only part of a service or payment is refundable, the decision should identify the amount, the payment component being returned and the basis for any amount retained. A refund decision does not replace any separate cargo-loss, damage, delay, insurance or statutory claim process.

7. Payment failures, pending transactions and duplicate debits

If your bank account is debited but the KRIM booking screen does not show a successful payment, do not assume that a booking is confirmed. Share the payment date, amount, payment reference and booking/order reference so the payment status can be reconciled with the payment provider.

  • Failed or declined payment: If the payment was not captured, there may be no refund for KRIM to initiate; your bank or payment provider can confirm any temporary debit or reversal.
  • Pending payment: We will check the gateway status before taking a second payment or confirming the booking. Avoid paying again until the first attempt is reconciled.
  • Duplicate payment: Once duplicate capture is verified, the excess amount will be reviewed for refund to the original payment source.
  • Successful payment but no booking: We will investigate whether the order was created, accepted or failed and identify the party responsible for returning any amount not due.

8. Refund method, status and processing time

Where the booking receipt identifies Razorpay as the payment provider, an approved refund may be submitted through the supported gateway workflow against the original payment, subject to the payment provider, bank, payment method and transaction status. The payment collector—not the gateway acting on its own—must approve and initiate the refund. The payment provider does not decide whether a shipment cancellation qualifies.

Initiated / pending

The refund request has been submitted or accepted for processing, but the bank or payment network may still be processing it. This is not proof that the money has reached your account.

Processed / failed / reversed

The final gateway or bank status must be checked. If a refund fails or is reversed, the responsible payment-collecting party must investigate and communicate the next step rather than treating it as paid back.

There is no single processing time that applies to every payment method, bank or transaction. Any timeframe displayed at checkout, in the payment-provider receipt or in a refund confirmation is an estimate unless the applicable law requires otherwise. The current gateway status and the bank or payment network's processing information take precedence. A refund can remain pending before its final status is available. KRIM will not describe a refund as completed until the relevant final status is confirmed, and will not promise a fixed refund date that it cannot verify.

If the refund is marked processed but has not appeared, contact your bank/payment provider with the refund reference or bank trace number (such as an ARN, RRN or UTR) supplied, if available. If the refund remains pending beyond the provider's stated estimate, contact KRIM with the booking and payment references so the responsible party can trace it.

Refunds will normally be returned to the original source; a different destination will be used only where the payment provider supports it and the alternative is lawful and properly verified. KRIM will not ask for passwords, OTPs, card PINs or full card credentials to process a refund.

9. Platform fees, freight charges, taxes and deductions

The booking confirmation and invoice/receipt must distinguish, where applicable, the freight or provider service amount, KRIM's separately agreed platform or coordination fee, taxes, insurance premium and any other disclosed charge. It must also identify the entity collecting each amount and the entity issuing the corresponding invoice or receipt.

A refund of one component does not automatically determine the treatment of every other component. Any retained fee, payment-processing cost, cancellation amount, tax adjustment or third-party cost must be lawful, disclosed before acceptance where required, supported by the relevant contract and transaction records, and handled consistently with applicable tax and payment rules. No blanket non-refundable label applies to amounts that must legally be returned.

Where GST invoices or credit notes are required, the responsible registered supplier must issue the correct document and make the corresponding tax adjustment in accordance with applicable GST law. Customers should retain the original invoice and refund confirmation.

10. Lost, damaged, missing or delayed cargo

Loss, damage, shortage, tampering, delay or non-delivery may require a separate service claim rather than a simple payment cancellation. Notify KRIM and the contracting provider promptly, preserve packaging and cargo evidence where safe, and provide shipment documents, photographs, delivery remarks, proof of value and other reasonably requested information.

The responsible carrier, warehouse operator, insurer or other provider and the applicable claim procedure must be identified in the shipment documents. Claim notice periods, liability limits, exclusions and insurance coverage depend on the actual contract and mandatory transport law. This page does not shorten a statutory deadline or guarantee insurance cover. KRIM will route the claim to the responsible party where it is within KRIM's agreed role.

11. International and cross-border shipments

For any future international movement, the booking-specific documents must disclose before payment the applicable customs duties, import/export taxes, brokerage, inspection, storage, demurrage, return or abandonment costs, restricted-goods requirements, refund currency and exchange-rate treatment, and the party responsible for each item. Such amounts may be imposed by authorities or third parties and may not be recoverable after they are incurred.

International services are offered only where the route, provider, legal permissions, payment method and customs arrangements have been validated and expressly confirmed. A country or currency shown on the platform does not establish that a shipment can be booked there.

12. How to request a cancellation or refund

Use the KRIM Contact page and select the relevant enquiry channel. Include the following information, but do not send sensitive payment credentials:

  • shipment, quote, order or booking reference;
  • name and contact details used for the booking;
  • payment date, amount, payment method and gateway/payment reference (if available);
  • the cancellation or refund reason and when the request was first made;
  • relevant booking confirmation, invoice, receipts, provider messages or cargo evidence.

KRIM may request additional information reasonably needed to verify the booking, prevent fraud or route the request. Do not send OTPs, passwords, card PINs or complete card/bank credentials.

13. Review, escalation and statutory rights

We will acknowledge and review requests through the contact channel provided, using the booking and payment records available. Where another provider or payment collector is responsible, we will identify or route the request to that party where appropriate and explain the next step. Any grievance contact, response time or escalation process required by law must be published and maintained in the final operational version of this policy.

If you disagree with the outcome, reply with the case reference and explain which booking term, payment record or service event you believe has been overlooked. You may also use any consumer forum, bank/payment-provider dispute process or other remedy available under applicable law. Nothing in this policy restricts those rights or imposes a waiver as a condition of receiving a refund.

14. Policy changes and contact

KRIM may update this policy to reflect changes in its platform, payment arrangements, service model or legal requirements. The version applicable to an accepted booking will be assessed together with the booking documents and mandatory law; a later website update does not silently rewrite an existing contract.

For a cancellation, refund or payment query, contact KRIM through the Contact page and select the relevant payment, refund or shipment-support enquiry type. Read the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Notice for related information. The Terms & Conditions explain KRIM's platform role and the need for each accepted booking to identify its actual contracting and payment-collecting parties.

Operational launch gate: Before accepting paid bookings, KRIM must verify its legal eligibility and regulatory position, publish the actual legal operator and grievance contact, define who collects and refunds each payment component, align provider contracts and cancellation schedules, confirm Razorpay refund permissions/balance handling and webhook status reconciliation, test full and partial refunds plus duplicate/pending/failed payments, and ensure the customer-facing checkout matches this policy. This page is a policy disclosure; it does not prove that those backend controls are live or that any particular refund has been tested.
