KRIM LOGISTICS · PARTNER-DIRECT MODEL

Cancellation & refund guidance

Interim customer guidance for KRIM's initial partner-direct marketplace model.

Freight services are contracted and paid directly to the selected logistics partner.
If you paid a partner, contact that partner to request cancellation, adjustment or refund under your direct contract and applicable law. The partner is responsible for decisions and payments relating to money it collected. KRIM does not receive, hold or refund partner-collected freight payments.

How KRIM can assist

KRIM can receive a complaint about the platform experience, help identify the relevant shipment or partner contact, and route the issue through its support process. This does not guarantee a freight refund or replace your rights against the partner.

KRIM fees

No KRIM referral, coordination or other fee should be assumed from a partner freight quotation. If a separately payable KRIM service is introduced in future, its scope, price and cancellation/refund terms must be disclosed before the customer agrees or pays.

This is interim operational guidance, not a final contract or legal advice. The policy must be reviewed by KRIM's legal and tax advisers before commercial launch. Mandatory consumer rights remain unaffected.
