Visibility with context · Updates with accountability

Know what is confirmed. Understand what comes next.

This policy explains how shipment tracking references, status events, carrier updates, estimated milestones and delivery records may be presented through KRIM Logistics.

KRIM aims to make shipment progress easier to understand by presenting available operational events and records. Tracking is an information service, not a guarantee of continuous monitoring, a particular route, a delivery time or the physical condition of cargo. The accepted quote, booking, work order, provider terms and applicable law govern the actual service.

1. Scope and visibility principles

This policy applies when a KRIM customer, authorised partner or other permitted user views shipment status through a KRIM website, account, notification or support channel. The exact tracking features available may differ by route, transport mode, provider, booking type and shipment stage.

KRIM's global-ready technology architecture does not mean that live tracking is available worldwide or on every shipment. Service coverage and visibility are enabled only where a provider and the relevant data connection are actually available.

2. Shipment references and lookup

A shipment reference identifies a record within the applicable KRIM or provider workflow. Enter it exactly as issued and use only the official tracking route communicated for that booking. Some carriers may issue a separate carrier reference, consignment note number, bill of lading number or airway bill number.

Keep references confidential. Do not post them publicly or share screenshots that expose names, addresses, phone numbers, commercial details or other personal information. A reference alone may not prove that the person using it is authorised to access all shipment details.

3. Sources of tracking information

Shipment events may be received from a carrier, driver, warehouse, terminal, customs or other service provider; entered by an authorised operations user; or generated by a connected system. The source and level of verification can differ between events.

Where provider data is not connected or an event has not been supplied, the KRIM interface may show limited information or no new update. KRIM does not independently observe every physical movement. A displayed event should not be interpreted as independently verified unless it is expressly identified as such.

4. Understanding status labels and milestones

Status labels are operational summaries. Depending on the service, events may include booking received, booking confirmed, pickup scheduled, picked up, received at facility, departed, arrived at facility, out for delivery, delivery attempted, delivered, exception, cancelled or returned.

Not every status applies to every mode or shipment. A status can be delayed, corrected, duplicated or supplied retrospectively. A milestone such as “departed” or “arrived” may refer to a facility or transport leg, not the final destination. The event timestamp, time zone and source should be considered where displayed.

5. Update frequency and notifications

Updates are event-based unless a particular service expressly provides a different monitoring frequency. There may be gaps while cargo is moving between facilities, outside network coverage, awaiting a provider scan or during system outages. A lack of a new event does not by itself establish that a shipment is stationary, lost or delayed.

SMS, email, messaging-app or in-account notifications may be offered only where the relevant channel is configured and operational. Delivery of a notification can be affected by network providers, device settings, incorrect contact details, spam filtering or third-party outages. Do not rely on a notification as the only means of meeting a deadline or taking urgent action.

6. GPS and location visibility

Precise vehicle or parcel location is shown only where the relevant provider, device, permissions and data feed support it and the feature is enabled for the shipment. Some shipments may show only milestone events, facility scans or manually reported updates.

Location points may be approximate, delayed, intermittent or unavailable. They may represent a vehicle, facility, scan point or last reported position rather than the exact current position of an individual package. KRIM does not promise continuous GPS tracking unless that capability is expressly confirmed in the accepted service description.

7. Estimated arrival and delivery windows

Any estimated pickup, transit or delivery time is indicative unless the accepted booking expressly states a guaranteed service and its conditions. Estimates may be based on provider information, route assumptions, historical or operational data and may change.

Weather, congestion, capacity, breakdowns, security checks, customs, regulatory inspection, inaccurate shipment details, access restrictions and other events can affect timing. A displayed estimate is not a promise or proof that delivery will occur at that time. Refer to the Shipping & Delivery Policy and the accepted booking terms.

8. Delays, exceptions and status changes

An exception may indicate a delivery attempt, address issue, missing document, customs hold, operational disruption, safety concern, damaged packaging, capacity constraint or other event requiring attention. The detail available may depend on what the provider reports.

Where an action is requested, follow the instruction shown in the shipment record or contact KRIM using the official support channel. Provide the shipment reference and relevant facts. Do not assume that a status change automatically resolves an exception or that a revised estimate has been confirmed by every party.

9. Delivered status and proof of delivery

A “delivered” event may be based on a carrier scan, provider confirmation, recipient acknowledgement or other record, depending on the service. Electronic proof of delivery (ePOD), signature, recipient name, photograph, delivery timestamp or location evidence is available only where the provider captures it and the booking supports its disclosure.

A delivered status does not always mean that a recipient personally accepted the goods, that all packages were received, or that contents were inspected and found undamaged. If the delivery record appears incorrect or a package is missing, notify KRIM promptly and preserve the packaging and evidence. Follow the Shipment Claims & Damage Reporting Policy.

10. Access, authorised users and sharing

Tracking details may be visible to the customer who booked the shipment and other users authorised for that organisation or shipment, subject to role permissions and applicable law. Partners may receive information needed to perform their assigned work. Access may be restricted, suspended or corrected where there is a security, privacy or operational reason.

Customers are responsible for maintaining accurate contact details and controlling access to their accounts and references. Do not share login credentials, one-time passwords, authentication codes or private tracking links with unauthorised persons. Report suspected unauthorised access immediately.

11. Data accuracy, corrections and limitations

Tracking information is presented from available records and may contain errors, omissions, inconsistent time zones, duplicate events or delayed updates. KRIM may correct or annotate a record when reliable information becomes available. Corrections may not instantly propagate to every provider or notification channel.

Tracking data should not be used as the sole basis for safety-critical decisions, customs declarations, insurance decisions, inventory release, financial settlement or legal proof unless the relevant record is expressly suitable and verified for that purpose. Request supporting documents where required.

12. Missing, delayed or incorrect tracking updates

If a shipment reference does not work, first check spelling, spaces, reference type and whether tracking has been activated for the booked service. If an event is missing, allow for provider scan and data-transfer delays, then contact KRIM with the reference, last known status and the specific issue.

If the tracking page reports no record, this does not necessarily mean that no booking exists. The booking may be pending acceptance, associated with a different carrier reference, not yet synchronised or unavailable through the public lookup. Do not submit sensitive documents through an unverified link.

13. Privacy and security of tracking data

Shipment tracking can involve personal data, contact details, location information, commercial records and delivery evidence. KRIM handles such information as described in its Privacy Notice, subject to applicable law and the actual systems and providers used for the shipment.

Access should be limited to legitimate operational and support purposes. Do not use tracking data to stalk, harass, profile or contact drivers, recipients or other individuals outside the shipment purpose. Report suspected data exposure through the official support channel.

14. Loss, damage, shortage and claims

Tracking is not a claims decision and does not determine legal responsibility. If cargo is lost, damaged, tampered with or short-delivered, notify the relevant carrier/provider and KRIM promptly, preserve packaging, take photographs and retain delivery or exception records.

Claims are assessed under the accepted contract, carrier terms, insurance arrangements and applicable law. No claim deadline, compensation amount or insurance cover should be assumed from a tracking status. See the Shipment Claims & Damage Reporting Policy for reporting guidance.

15. Records, retention and service availability

Tracking events and supporting records may be retained for operational, customer-support, security, legal, accounting and dispute-resolution purposes, subject to the retention practices described in the Privacy Notice and applicable law. The period may vary by record type, provider and legal requirement.

Tracking interfaces may be unavailable or limited due to maintenance, connectivity, provider integrations, system incidents or force majeure events. KRIM may change features, labels or supported providers, while respecting applicable contractual and legal obligations.
