Frequently asked questions

Subscription and payment clarity.

Can I subscribe and immediately get transport providers?

No subscription guarantees a provider, quote, load or route. KRIM confirms actual service availability route by route. Basic shipment enquiries remain free.

When will paid plans and payment be available?

After the software features, plan entitlements, recurring billing, refunds, tax treatment and payment-provider eligibility are verified. Paid checkout is not enabled at this time.

Will the free evaluation automatically become paid?

No. The 30-day evaluation is invitation-only, starts after written confirmation, requires no payment card and does not automatically convert to a paid plan.

Can an operator use KRIM with its own customers?

That is the intended SaaS use case. Access is provided only to the features confirmed as ready for that pilot or contract.
