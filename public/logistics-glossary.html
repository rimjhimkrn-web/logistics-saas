Key Supply Chain Definitions

Essential terminology for enterprise shippers and carrier partners.

ePOD (Electronic Proof of Delivery)

Cryptographically verified digital sign-offs and delivery receipts captured at destination terminal handoffs, instantly triggering escrow fund disbursements via Razorpay.

Row Level Security (RLS)

PostgreSQL database security feature implemented in Supabase to guarantee absolute tenant isolation, ensuring enterprise shipping ledgers never cross organizational boundaries.

Demurrage & Detention

Institutional penalty fees levied for exceeding free time allowances during container loading, unloading, or terminal storage periods, automatically audited by KRIM OS.

Multi-Modal Freight

The seamless integration of two or more transport modes (road, rail, ocean, air) under a single consolidated consignment contract and unified tracking telemetry stream.
