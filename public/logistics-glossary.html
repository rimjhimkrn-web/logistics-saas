A–Z Trade & Freight Dictionary

Global Logistics & Supply Chain Glossary

Essential definitions, Incoterms® 2020 abbreviations, maritime shipping acronyms, and customs clearance terminology for freight forwarders and shippers.

A B C D F H I L O T W

A

3 Terms
AWB (Air Waybill) Air Freight

A non-negotiable transport document issued by an air carrier that acts as a receipt of goods and evidence of the contract of carriage.

AEO (Authorized Economic Operator) Customs

A certified status granted by customs authorities to businesses that satisfy strict compliance, security, and financial solvency criteria.

AOG (Aircraft On Ground) Express Logistics

A high-priority emergency logistics classification indicating a grounded aircraft requiring immediate replacement spare parts.

B

2 Terms
B/L (Bill of Lading) Ocean Freight

A legal document issued by a freight carrier to a shipper detailing the type, quantity, and destination of cargo being transported.

Bonded Warehouse Warehousing

A secure facility licensed by customs where imported goods can be stored, manipulated, or undergo processing without paying customs duties.

C

2 Terms
CIF (Cost, Insurance & Freight) Incoterms

Incoterm where the seller pays ocean freight, insurance, and costs to the destination port, but buyer assumes risk once cargo is loaded.

Cross-Docking Contract Logistics

A logistics practice of unloading inbound materials directly onto outbound transportation with little or no interim storage time.

D

2 Terms
DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) Incoterms

Incoterm where the seller takes maximum responsibility, paying for transport, import duties, customs clearance, and taxes to the buyer’s door.

Demurrage & Detention Maritime

Demurrage is charged when container equipment remains inside the terminal past free time. Detention applies when containers are kept outside the port beyond allowable days.

F

2 Terms
FCL (Full Container Load) Ocean Shipping

An ocean freight booking where an entire shipping container (20ft, 40ft, 40ft HC) is dedicated exclusively to a single consignee's cargo.

FTL (Full Truckload) Road Freight

A ground freight shipment mode where an entire semi-trailer or dry van is contracted for a single shipper’s freight destination.
