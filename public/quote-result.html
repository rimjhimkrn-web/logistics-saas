Payment protection

Three controls keep the transaction connected to the shipment.

01Exact quote bindingThe selected quote ID is passed to the server. KRIM checks the accepted quote and shipment together.
02Verified provider responseOpening or closing Razorpay does not mark a shipment as paid.
03No blind retryAn unknown result must be reconciled before another charge is attempted.
