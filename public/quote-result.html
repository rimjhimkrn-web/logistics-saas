Pay your partner directly.

KRIM connects customers with logistics partners. Your selected partner is responsible for confirming the final service scope, contract, freight amount, payment beneficiary and receipt. KRIM does not collect or hold freight payments in this launch model.

What happens next?

01 · ReviewCheck the accepted quotation and the partner's service details.
02 · ConfirmContact the selected partner to confirm availability, scope, final price and terms.
03 · Pay directlyPay only the partner under your direct agreement after independently checking beneficiary details.
04 · Keep recordsObtain the partner's invoice or receipt and retain your agreement and payment proof.
KRIM freight checkout is disabled.
No KRIM freight payment order will be created on this page. Do not transfer freight money to KRIM. Verify the partner's legal identity, bank beneficiary and service terms independently before payment.

Need help?

For shipment-platform access, tracking or a complaint about your KRIM experience, contact KRIM support. For a refund of freight paid directly to a logistics partner, contact that contracted partner first. KRIM can help route a complaint but does not promise or issue refunds for money collected by the partner.
