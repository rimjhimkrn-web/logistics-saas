Before you pay

Know what this checkout will and will not do.

Quote-linked checkoutThe payment amount, currency, quote ID, shipment ID, customer organization and partner offer are checked by the backend. Browser values are not treated as payment authority.
A successful provider window alone does not mean payment is complete. KRIM must verify the Razorpay signature, order, captured amount, currency and shipment linkage, then save the financial transaction. International payment methods are subject to Razorpay account approval and market availability; no global coverage is assumed.
