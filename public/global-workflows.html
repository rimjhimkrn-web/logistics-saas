Connected shipment lifecycle

The intended end-to-end operating sequence across the workspaces.

Request & quote
Customer submits shipment details; eligible pricing is returned.
Accept & pay
Quote acceptance and verified payment status are recorded.
Assign & execute
Authorized partner, driver and warehouse update milestones.
Track & resolve
Exceptions, documents, delivery evidence and customer updates.
Invoice
Applicable charges and invoices are reconciled against records.
Settle
Partner settlement and platform commission follow controlled rules.
Audit
Important actions retain accountable records and permission checks.
Improve
Service metrics and feedback inform quality and reliability.
