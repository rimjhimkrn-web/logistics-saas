How the complaint process should work

What makes a useful complaint?

  • Dates, time zone and sequence of events.
  • Relevant shipment or partner reference.
  • Expected service compared with actual outcome.
  • Clear description of business or customer impact.
  • Evidence references, without unnecessary sensitive data.
  • A reasonable requested outcome.

Important safeguards

  • Keep original records and evidence unchanged.
  • Share information only with authorized recipients.
  • Do not use complaints to disclose credentials or secrets.
  • Insurance and contractual claim deadlines may apply separately.

Complaints & claims — know the difference

Some matters require a separate contractual or insurance process.

CP

Service complaint

Concern about communication, coordination, conduct or process quality.

SC

Shipment claim

Loss, damage or shortage may require a separate evidence and claim procedure.

IN

Insurance claim

Notify the relevant insurer or broker under the applicable policy and deadlines.

CM

Commercial dispute

Invoice, payment or settlement disagreements may follow contractual review terms.

Frequently asked questions

Key points before raising a concern.

Does preparing this draft submit a formal complaint?

No. The draft remains in the browser. A live submission requires an authenticated, authorized support workflow and a confirmed receipt or ticket reference.

How quickly will KRIM resolve my complaint?

No response or resolution deadline is promised by this preview. Applicable acknowledgement and resolution targets must be confirmed in the relevant partner agreement or published support policy.

Can I request compensation here?

You can state the outcome you are seeking, but this does not establish entitlement or approve compensation. Any decision depends on evidence, applicable contracts, policies and authorized review.

What if the issue involves cargo loss, damage or an insurance policy?

Preserve evidence and follow the relevant carrier, contractual and insurer notification requirements. Separate claim deadlines may apply; a general service complaint does not replace a formal claim.

Can I complain about an urgent safety or security incident?

Use the verified urgent operational or security escalation channel immediately. Do not rely on this draft page for emergency response.

Need to contact KRIM?

Use the official contact page for an actual enquiry. Keep your complaint draft for reference.
