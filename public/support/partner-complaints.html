Evidence & fairness guidelines

Help the review stay accurate, secure and balanced.

Keep original records

Preserve original documents, timestamps, tracking events and correspondence. Do not alter evidence or remove relevant context.

Protect sensitive information

Do not include passwords, OTPs, API keys, full bank details, full card numbers or unrelated personal information.

Separate facts from assumptions

Describe what you directly observed, what records show and what remains uncertain. Avoid stating allegations as proven findings.

Frequently asked questions

Important points before raising a concern.

Does preparing this form formally submit my complaint?

No. It creates a local text summary only. A production submission channel must be explicitly provided and should issue a reference or acknowledgement after a successful submission.

Does every complaint qualify for compensation?

No. Any compensation, refund or claim depends on the relevant contract, evidence, applicable law, insurance terms and review by the responsible party. Selecting a requested outcome does not create an entitlement.

What if the concern involves an immediate safety or security risk?

Do not rely on this preview. Use the verified urgent operational or security escalation channel and contact emergency services where appropriate.

What if the issue concerns cargo damage or loss?

Preserve evidence and notify the responsible carrier, warehouse or insurer promptly through the applicable contractual process. Claim notification deadlines may apply; do not wait for a general complaint review.

How long will review take?

Response times depend on the issue, evidence, responsible parties and applicable partner arrangements. No specific service-level deadline is promised on this page.
