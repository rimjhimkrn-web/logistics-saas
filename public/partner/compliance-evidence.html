Compliance evidence submission

Submit current documents for independent KRIM review. Uploads are stored in a private evidence bucket and linked to your verified business organisation. A submission is not an approval, certification, commercial activation or permission to accept shipments.

Important: These four baseline categories are an operational review framework, not a universal legal checklist. Requirements vary by country, entity type and actual service. Four baseline compliance categories

For each category, upload one clear PDF, JPEG or PNG (maximum 10 MB), or provide a reasoned non-applicability declaration. Do not upload original documents you cannot lawfully share.

1. Business registration & legal identity

Certificate or registry extract showing legal entity name, registration number, issuing authority and current status.

2. Tax identity & invoicing

Applicable tax registration or an explanation of the lawful basis for non-registration in this jurisdiction.

3. Operating authority & service permissions

Relevant permits, licences, registrations or service authority for the actual logistics activities and territories.

4. Insurance & liability

Relevant cargo, carrier, warehouse, motor, public or professional liability cover, or a reasoned explanation of the current position.
