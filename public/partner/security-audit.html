Security posture checklist

Practical controls to review before production partner operations.

GOVERNANCE

01 · Identity & session

Use an individual account, sign out on shared devices, protect recovery email and enable MFA when required by your organization.

02 · Organization isolation

Access must come from active organization membership and database policies—not from a hidden UI element or a guessed organization ID.

03 · Least privilege

Give teammates only the role and permissions they need. This page does not grant or change roles.

04 · Secrets & integrations

Never place service-role keys, API secrets or private credentials in browser code, screenshots or CSV exports.

05 · Evidence retention

Preserve approved audit evidence and supporting documents under your company retention and legal requirements.

06 · Incident response

Record the time, affected workflow and request reference; notify your designated security owner. Do not retry suspicious actions blindly.
