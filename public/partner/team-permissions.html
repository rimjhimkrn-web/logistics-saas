Partner administration · Organization access

Team roles and permissions

Team access must be granted to verified organization members through an authenticated, permission-controlled workflow. This page is guidance only; it does not create accounts, issue credentials or change anyone's access.

Self-service changes not enabled here

Access control status

Do not enter a teammate's personal details or email address on this page. No invitation or permission change will be made here. A team invitation should only be issued after the organization owner or an authorized administrator confirms the member's identity and the minimum permissions required.

Operational roles

Dispatch, shipment updates, driver coordination and service evidence should be limited to staff who need those functions for an assigned operational responsibility.

Finance and sensitive records

Financial access must be separately authorized and limited to records actually available to the organization. KRIM does not provide escrow payouts or partner-fund custody through this page.

Owner and administrator

Organization owners should periodically review active memberships, remove access when a person leaves, and retain an audit trail of authorized changes.

Need an access review?

Contact KRIM with your organization reference and the type of access change required. Do not send passwords, OTPs, identity documents or full banking details in an ordinary message.
