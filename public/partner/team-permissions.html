8 profiles
Governance

Organisation owner

Accountability for the organisation's KRIM relationship and authorised administration.

Typical responsibilities
  • Review organisation profile and status.
  • Nominate authorised administrators.
  • Approve access-review requests.
Protect owner-level actions with strong authentication.
Administration

Organisation administrator

Coordinates approved team access and routine organisation administration.

Typical responsibilities
  • Maintain approved team records.
  • Escalate role or membership changes.
  • Keep access aligned to job duties.
Grant only permissions explicitly authorised by KRIM controls.
Operations

Operations coordinator

Coordinates day-to-day work across approved shipments and services.

Typical responsibilities
  • Review assigned operational work.
  • Coordinate status and exception follow-up.
  • Escalate safety or service issues.
Access should be limited to assigned operational scope.
Dispatch

Dispatch coordinator

Supports dispatch preparation and authorised driver or vehicle coordination.

Typical responsibilities
  • Coordinate approved dispatch details.
  • Check assignment readiness.
  • Escalate changes to authorised operations staff.
No authority to self-approve unverified fleet or service capability.
Field operations

Driver / field operator

Performs assigned field tasks using their own authorised identity.

Typical responsibilities
  • View assigned work instructions.
  • Submit permitted milestone evidence.
  • Report delivery or safety exceptions.
Never share another person's login or OTP.
Finance

Finance team member

Handles approved financial records relevant to the organisation.

Typical responsibilities
  • Review permitted invoices and statements.
  • Reconcile approved commercial records.
  • Raise discrepancies through authorised channels.
Financial access should be separately limited and audited.
Compliance

Compliance & evidence lead

Coordinates evidence readiness and internal document follow-up.

Typical responsibilities
  • Track evidence requests and expiry dates.
  • Submit current supporting documents.
  • Escalate status mismatches for review.
Submission is not the same as independent verification or approval.
Read only

Reporting viewer

Reviews information needed for reporting without operational control.

Typical responsibilities
  • View approved reports and summaries.
  • Identify trends or data-quality issues.
  • Refer required changes to authorised staff.
Read-only access should not expose unrelated personal or financial data.

Four checks before access is granted

Use these checks during an internal review or support request.

01

Confirm identity

Verify the person through the organisation's approved process. Never share credentials or OTPs.

02

Confirm membership

Check active membership in the correct legal organisation, not just a familiar email address.

03

Limit permissions

Choose only the access required for the person's current duties and approved scope.

04

Review regularly

Revoke or adjust access when responsibilities change or a person leaves the organisation.
