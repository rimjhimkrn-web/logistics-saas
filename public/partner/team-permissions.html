Role responsibility guide

Illustrative responsibility boundaries; exact permissions depend on KRIM's configured role-to-permission mappings.

O

Organisation owner

Accountability and oversight

Responsible for organisation-level oversight and ensuring that access is granted only to authorised people.

  • Review active team membership.
  • Nominate authorised administrators where supported.
  • Escalate ownership or identity changes for review.
Highest organisation responsibility
A

Organisation administrator

Team coordination

Supports approved administration within the scope assigned by the organisation owner and KRIM configuration.

  • Maintain team records where authorised.
  • Coordinate access requests and periodic reviews.
  • Cannot override platform-level controls.
Delegated administration
D

Dispatch & operations

Operational execution

Handles permitted shipment coordination and operational updates for the organisation's assigned work.

  • View relevant assigned shipment information.
  • Record authorised milestones and exceptions.
  • No automatic access to finance or identity administration.
Operational scope
F

Finance

Commercial record handling

Access to financial records should be separately scoped and limited to the organisation's authorised financial responsibilities.

  • Review permitted invoices and settlement records.
  • Protect bank and tax information.
  • No implied authority to approve operational or compliance evidence.
Sensitive financial scope
C

Compliance & documentation

Evidence coordination

Coordinates required organisation and service evidence without treating submission as approval.

  • Track document status and expiry.
  • Submit evidence through approved secure workflows.
  • Cannot self-certify KRIM verification or statutory licensing.
Evidence workflow
V

Driver / field personnel

Assigned field activity

Should receive only the minimum information needed for assigned transport or delivery activity.

  • Access assigned work and relevant instructions.
  • Submit permitted pickup or delivery evidence.
  • No access to organisation-wide administration by default.
Restricted field scope

Four controls to keep access safe

Apply these practices whenever team responsibilities change.

Least privilege

Start with the smallest access scope needed. Add permissions only when a documented responsibility requires them.

Separation of duties

Avoid concentrating sensitive finance, compliance review and operational authority in one account without a clear business need.

Identity protection

Never share passwords, OTPs or sessions. Use each person's own authenticated account and required MFA controls.

Recommended access review cycle

Review after onboarding, role changes and staff departures, and periodically thereafter.
