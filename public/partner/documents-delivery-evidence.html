01 · Customs & trade

Trade documentation remains jurisdiction-aware

Commercial invoices, packing lists, transport documents, permits and customs records must follow the applicable lane and jurisdiction requirements.

02 · Delivery proof

Keep chain of custody auditable

Receiver identity, timestamps, uploaded evidence and verification outcomes should remain tied to the relevant shipment and authorized user.

03 · Secure access

Metadata is not a file permission

Private storage objects should be accessed through authenticated, time-limited or policy-checked mechanisms—not public URLs or raw storage paths.
