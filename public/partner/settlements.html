Partner finance · current launch model

Customer freight payments go directly to the contracting provider.

KRIM connects customers with logistics partners. The partner that accepts the service contract performs the logistics service, issues its own invoice and receives the freight payment directly from the customer.

KRIM does not currently collect freight charges, hold escrow, split freight funds or execute partner payouts. This page is an operating-policy explanation, not a settlement ledger. No payout amount or settlement status is created here.

Partner responsibilities

  • Agree service scope, rate, payment terms and invoice directly with the customer.
  • Provide payment instructions in the partner's verified legal/business name.
  • Record receipt, outstanding balance, refunds and disputes in the partner's own accounting system.
  • Pay drivers, subcontractors and employees under the partner's own agreements.
  • Keep transport, tax and insurance records required for the service and jurisdiction.

Separate KRIM fees

If a referral, marketing or coordination fee is agreed with KRIM, it must be documented separately from the customer's freight payment and invoiced separately by KRIM. No automatic 10/90 split or freight deduction is active.

Tax treatment and invoicing requirements depend on the actual service and must be confirmed by a qualified Indian tax adviser before charging.

Do not assume escrow or payout protection

KRIM does not provide escrow, trust custody, split settlement, partner payout timing or automatic bank-account verification under the current launch model. A KRIM shipment status is not proof that the partner has received money.

Need help?

Use the authenticated partner workspace for operational tasks. For a commercial or support issue, create a secure support ticket and include only the relevant shipment or contract reference. Never send passwords, OTPs or full banking credentials.
