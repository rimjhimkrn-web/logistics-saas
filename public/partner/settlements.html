Partner finance · payment guidance

Clear payment responsibilities. Direct partner settlement.

KRIM connects customers with logistics partners. Under the current launch model, the customer pays the logistics provider they contract with directly. KRIM does not hold freight funds or operate partner payouts.

How the payment flow works

Three distinct steps keep the commercial relationship clear.

The customer and selected logistics partner agree the service scope, price, invoice terms and payment due date directly.

Commercial responsibilities

Each party maintains its own records and obligations.

Partner responsibilities

The logistics provider is responsible for its service contract and freight collection.

  • Confirm scope, rates, taxes and payment terms with the customer.
  • Issue invoices and receipts under the correct business identity.
  • Reconcile incoming payments, outstanding balances, refunds and disputes.
  • Pay drivers, subcontractors and suppliers under separate agreements.
  • Maintain applicable transport, tax, insurance and service records.
Separate commercial arrangement

KRIM platform fees

If a referral, marketing or coordination fee is agreed with KRIM, it is a separate commercial obligation.

  • Document the fee, service scope and payment terms separately.
  • KRIM issues its own invoice where legally required.
  • Do not deduct a KRIM fee from customer freight funds by assumption.
  • No automatic 10/90 split or freight-fund settlement is active under this model.

Partner reconciliation checklist

Use this checklist in your own finance system before marking an invoice as settled.

Before payment

  • Match invoice to the signed or accepted service agreement.
  • Confirm invoice number, legal entity, tax details and currency.
  • Verify payment instructions through a trusted partner contact.

After payment

  • Match the received amount to the bank or payment-provider record.
  • Record payment date, reference and any shortfall or excess.
  • Issue a receipt or credit note where applicable and retain evidence.

Frequently asked questions

Key clarifications about the current KRIM payment model.

Does KRIM receive the customer's freight payment?

No. The customer pays the logistics partner that contracts to provide the service. KRIM does not collect or hold freight funds under the current launch model.

Does KRIM automatically deduct a commission from freight?

No. Any KRIM referral, marketing or coordination fee must be separately agreed and documented. No automatic freight deduction or 10/90 split is active.

Does a shipment marked delivered mean the invoice is paid?

No. Operational completion and financial settlement are different events. Payment must be confirmed using the partner's own invoice and bank or payment-provider records.

Does this page provide escrow or payout protection?

No. This page does not provide escrow, custody of funds, split settlement, partner payout timing or bank-account verification.

Are KRIM fees automatically exempt from GST?

No such assumption should be made. Tax registration, invoicing and tax treatment depend on the actual service and applicable rules. Obtain advice from a qualified Indian tax professional before charging fees.
