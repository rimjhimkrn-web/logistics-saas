Operational guardrails

Capacity is not a dispatch assignment. Resource availability does not reserve or award shipment work by itself.

Allocation controlBind resources to a confirmed job only through the authorized assignment workflow.
Safety & complianceCheck licence, equipment suitability, maintenance, permits and cargo restrictions before dispatch.
Capacity integrityAvailability, reserved capacity, utilized capacity and contractual commitments are distinct measures.
Data governanceOnly backend-authorized organization records are shown. Missing data is not replaced with demonstrations.
