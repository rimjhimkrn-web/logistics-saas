Partner-reported
Capacity details are partner-reported and require administrator review. Until approved, the resource remains pending review and its availability is blocked from booking. Capacity registration requires the organisation's partner.manage permission. Only approved vehicle resources using vehicle-count or weight-capacity units currently participate in shipment-leg reservations. Warehouse slots, volume, containers and other equipment types remain registered records until their dedicated booking controls are implemented. Registration does not guarantee a shipment or create a booking. Do not enter customer personal data.

Operational guardrails

Capacity is not a dispatch assignment. Approved vehicle or weight capacity is reserved only against an accepted assignment and a matching scheduled window.

Allocation controlBind resources to a confirmed job only through the authorized assignment workflow.
Safety & complianceCheck licence, equipment suitability, maintenance, permits and cargo restrictions before dispatch.
Capacity integrityAvailability, reserved capacity, utilized capacity and contractual commitments are distinct measures.
Reservation scopeCurrent automated shipment reservations support verified vehicle resources using vehicle counts and weight units (kg / metric tonnes). Warehouse slots, volume and equipment-specific reservations are not yet enabled.
Data governanceOnly backend-authorized organization records are shown. Missing data is not replaced with demonstrations.
