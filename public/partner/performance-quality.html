Metric definitions

Consistent and auditable methodology

• On-time delivery compares actual delivery against the agreed delivery window.

• Milestone completeness measures required events with valid evidence.

• Exception incidence is not automatic proof of partner responsibility.

• Quality scores require a defined, verified assessment source.
