Give each person only the access they need

Operational roles should be separate from organization administration, finance approval and platform-wide privileged access.

02 · Membership lifecycle

Keep team access current

Review joiners, role changes, leavers and suspended access through approved and auditable processes.

03 · Accountability

Record sensitive changes

Invitations, role changes, removals and permission changes should require explicit authorization and auditable events.
