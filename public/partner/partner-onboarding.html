The onboarding journey

Four clear stages. One accountable process.

Indicative workflow only. Timelines and requirements depend on partner type, service category, jurisdiction and verification outcomes.

01

Share your profile

Tell KRIM who you are, where you operate and which logistics services your organisation can provide.

Partner interest
02

Provide supporting evidence

Where applicable, provide business identity, licences, insurance, fleet or facility details and authorised contacts.

Evidence review
03

Capability assessment

Service categories, geographic coverage, operating capacity and relevant compliance information are assessed.

Due diligence
04

Approval and activation

Only approved capabilities may be activated for relevant opportunities, subject to route and service confirmation.

Prepare for review

What may be needed?

Exact requirements are confirmed during review. Do not upload sensitive documents through this staging page.

Registered logistics businesses

For carriers, freight forwarders, warehouse operators and other registered service providers.

✓Registered business name and legal identity details
✓Authorised representative and business contact details
✓Service categories, operating locations and coverage
✓Relevant licences, insurance and safety evidence, where applicable

Individual service partners

For eligible independent professionals and individual service providers.

✓Identity and contact information
✓Relevant skills, experience and service category
✓Operating location and availability information
✓Required permits or credentials, where applicable
i
Important: partner interest is not partner approval.

Submitting interest does not guarantee onboarding, assignments, route coverage, rates, customer demand or a commercial relationship. KRIM confirms partner status and capabilities through its review process.
