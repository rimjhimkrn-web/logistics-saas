Next stage: service capability evidence

Your business verification packet is only an application receipt. KRIM must first complete independent identity and compliance review. Once the organization is verified and compliant, use Services & Capabilities to submit separate evidence for each transport mode and service. Each capability remains inactive until an independent reviewer approves it; country, corridor, service-area and capacity gates still apply.
