Financial definitions

Keep each state distinct

• Gross is not the same as net payable.

• An approved payable is not proof of transfer.

• A settlement reference is not a bank confirmation by itself.

• Amounts in different currencies must not be added without an authorized FX conversion.

01 · Commercial integrity

Preserve the accepted commercial terms

Partner payable records should reconcile to the awarded assignment and approved pricing—not an independently recalculated estimate.

02 · Reconciliation

Investigate discrepancies instead of hiding them

Differences in invoice, payable, settlement or provider reference should remain visible for an authorized finance workflow.

03 · Global finance

Respect currency and jurisdiction

Store and report each record in its authoritative currency. Taxes, FX, payout methods and compliance depend on the applicable country and provider.
