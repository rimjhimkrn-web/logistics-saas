Support request preparation

Organize the information before contacting the KRIM support team.

01

Operational issue

For shipment milestones, pickup delays, delivery exceptions, assignment questions or partner workspace access.

  • Include the shipment or assignment reference.
  • Describe the last confirmed event and its time zone.
  • State the operational impact and next deadline.
02

Finance or document issue

For invoice references, settlement questions, document review or proof-of-delivery concerns.

  • Include a safe invoice or document reference.
  • State the amount/currency only where appropriate and authorized.
  • Do not send full bank details, passwords or access tokens.

Support response principles

Clear expectations without promising unverified service levels.

Prioritize by impact

  • Safety, security and major service disruption require urgent escalation through the approved channel.
  • Time-critical shipment exceptions should include the next operational deadline.
  • Routine questions should include complete references to reduce back-and-forth.

Keep communication secure

  • Use verified KRIM contact routes and known partner contacts.
  • Never share passwords, OTPs, API secrets or full payment credentials.
  • Share only the personal or shipment data necessary to resolve the issue.

Frequently asked questions

Common partner communications questions.

Will changing the preferences here update my account?

No. The preference helper is a local interface preview. Production preferences must be saved through an authenticated workflow with appropriate permissions and an auditable record.

Does KRIM guarantee 24/7 support?

Support hours and response targets depend on the applicable partner agreement and service arrangement. Do not assume continuous coverage unless it has been expressly confirmed.

Can I submit a support ticket from this page?

No. The support summary tool only prepares text in your browser. Use the official KRIM contact channel or an enabled authenticated support workflow to submit a real request.

What should I do if I suspect an account compromise?

Use a verified urgent support or security contact route, protect the affected account and credentials, and do not post secrets or access tokens in a ticket.

Ready to contact KRIM?

Use the official contact channel for a real enquiry. The draft helper above does not send your information.
