01 · Delivery certainty

Track the state, not just the send

Queued, sent and delivered are different states. Provider callbacks and delivery events are necessary to confirm final delivery.

02 · Global operations

Respect locale and time zones

Notification preferences, local quiet hours, language and fallback channels should be applied by the authorized communication service.

03 · Controlled recovery

Retry safely and idempotently

Review failure reasons and previous attempts before retrying to avoid duplicate alerts and repeated customer contact.
