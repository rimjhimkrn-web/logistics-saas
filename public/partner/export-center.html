Security and organization activity records require a verified requester, a valid organization relationship and an authorized review before any disclosure.

How a records request is handled

  1. Identify the recordSpecify the organization, record category, relevant shipment or reference, and date range.
  2. Verify authorityKRIM reviews requester identity, organization membership and permitted access scope.
  3. Confirm the outcomeThe support team confirms whether the record can be provided and the approved delivery method.

Security reminder: never send passwords, one-time passcodes, full card details, bank login credentials or unrestricted document links in a support request.
