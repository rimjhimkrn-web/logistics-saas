Partner offboarding guidance

Partner account closure requires a documented review of organization access, open operational commitments and any contractual obligations. This page explains the considerations; it does not submit or approve an offboarding request.

Important: This page does not lock bidding, cancel shipments, close an account, move funds or initiate any settlement. KRIM does not hold partner freight funds or provide an escrow payout through this workflow. Any financial obligations must be reconciled directly between the contracting parties and reviewed under their agreements.
Preparation checklist

Operational close-out

  • Review active and assigned shipments with the relevant customer and service provider.
  • Resolve outstanding delivery evidence, claims, incidents and service exceptions.
  • Confirm that authorized team members know the proposed access-closure date.
  • Retain copies of records that your organization is legally entitled to keep.
Contract and access review

Organization and commercial review

  • Review the applicable partner agreement and notice requirements.
  • Reconcile invoices and balances directly with the relevant contracting parties.
  • Identify organization data and documents that require an authorized export request.
  • Ask KRIM to review access removal only after the required checks are complete.

Request an offboarding review

Contact KRIM with your organization name, registered account email and requested closure date. Do not include bank account numbers, passwords, OTPs or identity documents in an ordinary contact message. KRIM will confirm the applicable verification steps before any account-level action.
