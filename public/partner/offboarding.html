Important operating principles

Account closure must not bypass obligations or erase required evidence.

Customer and shipment continuity

  • Complete or formally hand over open shipments.
  • Keep customers informed through authorized channels.
  • Resolve delivery evidence and active exceptions before closure where possible.

Finance and commercial separation

  • Customers and logistics partners reconcile freight charges directly under their contract.
  • KRIM does not hold partner freight funds or guarantee escrow settlement through this page.
  • Separately agreed KRIM fees and other obligations should be reviewed under the relevant agreement.

Data and retention

  • Provide only records the organization is authorized to receive.
  • Retain information required by law, contract or dispute preservation duties.
  • Deletion requests require a separate privacy and retention assessment.

Access and integrations

  • Review staff accounts, API keys, webhooks and connected systems.
  • Revoke credentials in a coordinated, auditable manner.
  • Do not disable access until the authorized reviewer confirms the plan, except for urgent security containment.

Frequently asked questions

What to expect from a controlled partner exit.

Does this page close my KRIM partner account?

No. It provides preparation guidance and local draft tools only. Account closure requires an authorized request, identity and organization verification, operational review and an approved administrative action.

Will my active shipments be cancelled automatically?

No. Active shipments and customer commitments need a separate operational handover or completion plan. A partner account closure must not silently cancel service obligations.

Will KRIM pay or settle outstanding freight amounts?

This page does not initiate payments or settlements. Freight payments and balances are handled by the customer and logistics partner under their direct commercial agreement. Any separate KRIM fee is governed by its applicable agreement.

Can my organization's data be deleted immediately?

Not necessarily. Records may need to be retained for legal, regulatory, contractual, accounting, security or dispute-related reasons. Any deletion request must be assessed under the applicable retention and privacy rules.

What if I need urgent access removal for security reasons?

Use the verified KRIM security contact route immediately. An authorized security process may need to contain access before the ordinary offboarding sequence is complete.

Ready to request a formal review?

Send your prepared request through an official KRIM contact route. No request is submitted by this preview.
