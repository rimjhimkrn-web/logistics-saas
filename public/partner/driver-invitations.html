CONTROLLED CARRIER NETWORK · DRIVER ACCESS

Invite drivers to your carrier organization.

Issue a single-use, seven-day invitation to a named email address. The driver must sign in with that same address, accept the invitation and submit genuine licence evidence. KRIM keeps driver verification pending until an authorized reviewer completes the review.

Eligibility: only an active, verified, compliant registered carrier with an approved transport capability can issue invitations. An invitation does not approve a driver, licence, vehicle or shipment assignment.

Create driver invitation

The invitation token is shown once. Copy and send it using a trusted channel. Do not post it publicly.

Driver onboarding controls

The secure invitation workflow enforces the following controls:

  • Carrier organization and transport capability rechecked at issue and acceptance.
  • Token stored as a one-way SHA-256 hash; expires after seven days.
  • One-time acceptance bound to the invited email address.
  • Driver profiles explicitly marked development/test remain ineligible for commercial assignment.
  • Driver membership is added to this carrier organization.
  • Driver verification is reset to pending and requires evidence review.
  • Invitation creation, acceptance and revocation are audit logged.
