Booking confirmation, backed by evidence.

KRIM confirms an operational booking only when the authenticated customer, accepted quotation, verified payment, partner assignment and operational readiness records agree. A successful browser redirect or payment popup is never enough. This page checks the existing server records and will not manufacture a booking confirmation.
