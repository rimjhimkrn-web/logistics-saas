KRIM LOGISTICS · CUSTOMER JOURNEY

Confirm the shipment directly with your partner.

KRIM connects customers with logistics partners. The selected partner confirms final availability, service scope, shipment execution arrangements, contract, freight price and payment instructions directly with you.

Partner-direct launch model
KRIM does not collect or verify freight payments in this model. A KRIM payment popup, payment order or browser redirect is not required to proceed. Pay freight only to the logistics partner under your direct agreement, after independently verifying the beneficiary and terms.

Before treating your booking as confirmed

01 · Accepted quotationCheck that the quotation is valid and accepted by the relevant partner.
02 · Partner confirmationObtain direct confirmation of route availability, service scope, timing and responsibilities.
03 · Contract & paymentAgree terms and pay the partner directly. Obtain the partner's invoice or receipt.
04 · Keep evidenceRetain the accepted quote, partner confirmation, contract, invoice and payment proof.

Need assistance?

For KRIM account, platform or tracking issues, contact KRIM support. For freight payment confirmation, cancellation or refund, contact the logistics partner you contracted with. KRIM can route a platform complaint but cannot promise or issue refunds for partner-collected freight.
