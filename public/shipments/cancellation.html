Shipment cancellation and disruption

This legacy page is not connected to KRIM's authenticated cancellation workflow. It cannot cancel a shipment, notify a carrier, release capacity, or update an audit record.

No action has been taken.
Do not use this page to represent a shipment as cancelled or released. For an already dispatched or in-transit shipment, use a controlled operational exception/disruption process; do not attempt a generic status cancellation.

Why this control is paused

KRIM must verify the shipment, partner assignment, capacity, driver, warehouse stock, dispatch authorization, custody and notification state together before it can confirm a cancellation. The complete cross-resource cancellation workflow has not yet been production-verified.
