Records Governance & Cryptographic Erasure Schedule

Data Retention & Record Archival Policy

Effective Date: October 1, 2026. Statutory retention periods, automated cold-storage archiving schedules, and NIST 800-88 compliant data destruction protocols across KRIM OS database clusters.

01. Statutory Retention Schedules

To comply with international maritime law, customs audit mandates (US CBP, EU Customs Union), and global tax compliance frameworks, operational records are maintained according to strict timelines:

Bills of Lading & Customs Filings 7 Years Retention

Mandated by international maritime and customs tax laws.

Real-Time Telemetry & Raw GPS Feeds 90 Days Active Storage

Aggregated into historical transit benchmarks prior to raw purge.

02. Automated Cold-Storage Archiving

After 365 days of inactivity, live shipment records are automatically compressed, encrypted with AES-256 keys, and moved to immutable, write-once-read-many (WORM) cold storage vaults.

03. Cryptographic Data Erasure (NIST 800-88)

Upon expiration of statutory retention windows or formal receipt of a valid GDPR/CCPA erasure directive, data is cryptographically rendered unrecoverable using NIST 800-88 compliant multi-pass key destruction.
