Freight Booking Cancellation & Refund Policy

Effective Date: October 1, 2026. Rules governing cargo cancellation lead times, carrier equipment booking fees, spot rate deposit forfeitures, and automated billing credits.

01. Booking Cancellation Lead Windows

Cancellation requests must be submitted directly through the KRIM OS Portal prior to cargo gate-in or air waybill tender. Cancellations executed 72 hours prior to vessel estimated time of departure (ETD) or flight departure incur no penalty fee. Cancellations within 72 hours are subject to ocean/air carrier cancellation surcharges.

02. Non-Refundable Surcharges & Customs Outlays

Customs filing fees (ACE, ISF, ICS2), government inspection advances, and carrier space reservation lock-in fees are non-refundable once data transmission to border authorities has been initiated.

03. Automated Account Credit Disbursements

Approved refunds are disbursed automatically as account credits toward future shipments or refunded to the original payment method within 5–10 business days following finance desk audit approval.
