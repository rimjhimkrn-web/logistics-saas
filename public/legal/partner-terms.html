Carrier & Logistics Partner Network Terms

Effective Date: October 1, 2026. Legal conditions governing Ocean Lines, Air Freight Carriers, Trucking Fleets, Customs Brokers, and NVOCC Partners integrated into KRIM OS.

01. Partner Network Qualification

All carriers, drayage operators, and NVOCC partners connecting to KRIM OS must maintain active operating licenses (e.g., FMC, IATA, DOT, C-TPAT certification), mandatory commercial liability insurance, and valid environmental safety compliance rating.

02. Telemetry Sync & EDI Messaging Standards

Logistics Partners agree to transmit sub-minute tracking updates, milestone events, and electronic documentation (EDI 214, EDI 315, e-AWB status) into the KRIM OS event stream with a minimum guaranteed uptime of 99.5%.

03. Automated Settlement & Rate Filing

Rate matrix submissions, bunker adjustment factors (BAF), and terminal handling fees filed by Partners via the Partner Portal become binding upon system validation. Financial settlements are processed automatically upon electronic Proof of Delivery (e-POD) verification.
