Financial Framework & Settlement Rules

Payment Policy & Multi-Currency Billing Framework

Effective Date: October 1, 2026. Official terms governing freight invoicing, automated clearinghouse (ACH) payments, credit card surcharges, customs duty disbursements, and invoice dispute procedures.

01. Invoicing & Multi-Currency Settlement

Invoices are generated automatically upon cargo dispatch or booking lock-in and made available in the Billing Portal. All payments must be settled in the invoiced currency (USD, EUR, SGD, GBP, HKD) via wire transfer, SEPA, ACH, or corporate card.

02. Customs Duties & Government Fee Disbursements

Customs duties, import VAT, and government inspection fees advanced by KRIM OS on behalf of Shipper are subject to a 2% disbursement fee and are payable immediately upon invoice generation regardless of general freight credit terms.

03. Invoice Dispute Window

Any billing discrepancies or fee disputes must be submitted via the Billing Portal within 14 calendar days of invoice date. Undisputed portions of invoices must be paid according to standard account terms.
