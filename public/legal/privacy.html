Data Protection & Information Security Standard

Global Privacy Policy & Telemetry Governance

Effective Date: October 1, 2026 | Last Updated: October 2026. Governing how KRIM System Technologies Ltd. encrypts, processes, and safeguards corporate telemetry, container manifest data, and portal credentials globally.

01. Scope & Operational Context

This Global Privacy Policy applies to all data collected, transmitted, or processed through the **KRIM OS** logistics operating system surface, public APIs, container tracking telemetry engine, customs clearance gateway, and connected mobile interfaces. As an enterprise logistics system, KRIM OS operates under strict ISO/IEC 27001 information security controls and SOC 2 Type II trust principles.

02. Telemetry & Data Ingestion

In operating a global freight and logistics engine, KRIM OS ingests and processes the following telemetry and operational data categories:

  • Shipment & Manifest Telemetry: Bills of Lading (B/L), Air Waybills (AWB), container numbers, commercial invoices, HS commodity codes, and origin/destination coordinates.
  • Carrier & IoT Sensor Data: Live AIS satellite coordinates, reefer temperature/humidity logs, GPS truck positioning, and port gate timestamp events.
  • User & Access Credentials: Enterprise single sign-on (SSO) metadata, API authentication keys, corporate email identifiers, role-based access control (RBAC) logs, and session tokens.
  • Automated Customs Filings: Importer Security Filings (ISF), US CBP ACE transmission records, EU ICS2 safety data, and tax identification numbers.

03. Encryption Standards & Retention

All data in transit across the KRIM OS mesh is encrypted using **TLS 1.3** protocols with modern cipher suites. Data at rest in primary database clusters and secondary backup vaults is protected via **AES-256** zero-knowledge volume encryption.

Retention Mandate:

Operational shipment records are retained for 7 years to satisfy international maritime law, customs audit mandates, and tax compliance frameworks before automated cryptographic erasure.

04. Global Data Rights (GDPR / CCPA / APPI)

Regardless of jurisdiction, KRIM OS extends comprehensive data privacy rights to registered portal users and corporate representatives:

Right to Audit & Access Request a machine-readable export of all stored personal and corporate metadata.
Right to Rectification Update account credentials, notification settings, and company dispatch details instantaneously.
