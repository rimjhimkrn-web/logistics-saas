ESIGN Act & EU eIDAS Electronic Record Validity

Electronic Records & Digital Signatures Agreement

Effective Date: October 1, 2026. Governing the legal validity of electronic signatures, digital Bill of Lading execution, electronic Proof of Delivery (e-POD), and automated transactional notifications across KRIM OS.

01. Consent to Electronic Records & Signatures

By executing freight contracts, accepting spot quotes, or confirming booking dispatches on KRIM OS, your enterprise explicitly consents to conduct business electronically. Electronic signatures applied through the portal carry the same legal weight as wet-ink physical signatures under the US ESIGN Act, UETA, and EU eIDAS regulation.

02. Digital Bill of Lading & e-POD Enforceability

Electronic Bills of Lading (e-BL), Sea Waybills, e-Air Waybills, and digital Proof of Delivery signatures captured on mobile dispatch terminals are cryptographic, tamper-evident records legally binding upon all shippers, consignees, and carriers.

03. Transactional Email & Webhook Notices

Important contractual notices, customs hold alerts, freight invoices, and system status updates are delivered via electronic mail to registered account addresses or transmitted directly to enterprise webhook endpoints.
