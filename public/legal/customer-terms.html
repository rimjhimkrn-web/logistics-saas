Customer Agreement & Freight Booking SLA

Effective Date: October 1, 2026. Conditions governing commercial shippers, cargo declarations, payment terms, demurrage responsibilities, and portal account operations.

01. Scope & Shipper Account Registration

This Customer Agreement governs all freight bookings, rate guarantees, and transport services executed by registered commercial entities ("Shippers" or "Customers") through the KRIM OS platform. Customers are responsible for maintaining secure corporate portal credentials and updating company tax registration information.

02. Cargo Declaration & Hazardous Material Warranty

Shipper warrants that all cargo weight, dimension, commodity description, and HS codes submitted through the portal are 100% accurate. Shipper accepts full legal and financial responsibility for undeclared hazardous cargo (DG) or weight misdeclarations under SOLAS VGM (Verified Gross Mass) rules.

03. Payment Obligations & Credit Terms

Freight charges, ocean surcharges, and customs duties are payable according to the credit terms assigned to Shipper's account (e.g., Pre-payment, Net 14, Net 30). Unpaid balances accrue interest at 1.5% per month or the maximum statutory rate allowed by law.

04. Demurrage, Detention & Port Storage

Shipper is solely liable for all demurrage, detention, and terminal storage charges incurred due to delayed container return, customs holds, or late pickup at discharge terminals beyond allotted carrier free time.
