Trade Sanctions & Contraband Zero-Tolerance

Prohibited Items & Sanctioned Goods Policy

Effective Date: October 1, 2026. Comprehensive listing of contraband items, restricted commodities, embargoed destinations, and OFAC/EU/UN trade compliance rules.

01. Absolute Contraband & Prohibited Commodities

The following categories of cargo are strictly banned from transit or booking through any service operating on the KRIM OS network under any circumstances:

  • Illegal Narcotics & Controlled Substances: Illicit drugs, synthetic narcotics, or precursor chemicals governed by international drug control treaties.
  • Counterfeit & Pirated Goods: Trademark-infringing merchandise, pirated electronics, or forged currencies and negotiable instruments.
  • Endangered Species & Wildlife: Flora and fauna protected under CITES treaties (ivory, shark fins, exotic timber).
  • Unlicensed Munitions & Weapons: Firearms, military ordnance, explosives, or military-grade surveillance hardware lacking export permits.

02. Sanctioned Countries & Embargo Compliance

KRIM OS automatically screens booking manifests against US OFAC Specially Designated Nationals (SDN), EU Sanctions Map, and UN Security Council lists. Cargo originating from or destined for embargoed jurisdictions is automatically blocked.

03. Misdeclaration Penalties & Escalation

Attempting to ship prohibited goods or misdeclaring restricted commodities results in immediate account termination, cargo seizure, and direct reporting to federal customs enforcement authorities.
