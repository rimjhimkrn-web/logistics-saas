Cargo Claim Submission & Loss Settlement Protocol

Effective Date: October 1, 2026. Standard operating procedures for reporting cargo loss, physical damage, shortage, and marine insurance claims under international transport conventions.

01. Mandatory Statutory Notice Deadlines

Notice of cargo loss or physical damage must be noted on the Proof of Delivery (e-POD) at time of delivery or submitted in writing via the Claims Portal within strict statutory limits:

  • Apparent Ocean Damage: Immediate notation on delivery receipt or within 3 days for non-apparent damage.
  • Air Cargo Shortage/Damage: Written notice required within 14 calendar days of cargo receipt (Montreal Convention).
  • Road Freight Damage: Written notice within 7 business days under CMR standards.

02. Required Evidentiary Documentation

Claim filings must include high-resolution digital photographs of damaged cargo and broken container seals, original commercial invoice, packing list, formal survey report (for claims exceeding $5,000 USD), and signed delivery receipt noting exceptions.

03. Claims Portal Processing SLA

Fully documented claims submitted through the Claims Desk are acknowledged within 24 hours and undergo carrier subrogation audit within 30 business days.
