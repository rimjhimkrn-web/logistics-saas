Cookie Policy & Local Data Storage

Effective Date: October 1, 2026. Explaining how KRIM OS utilizes cookies, local storage objects, and session tokens to secure platform operations, maintain active portal sessions, and optimize rate query performance.

01. Essential Operational Cookies & Tokens

KRIM OS utilizes strictly necessary cookies and browser local storage to manage user authentication, maintain active JWT portal sessions, store localized currency preferences, and enforce security rate-limiting. These functional tokens cannot be disabled without compromising platform stability.

02. Telemetry & Performance Analytics

With user consent, first-party performance cookies collect anonymized edge-node latency metrics, rate engine search volume distribution, and navigation paths to optimize global CDN routing and system responsiveness.

03. Managing Consent & Preferences

Users can adjust non-essential cookie preferences at any time through the Portal Settings interface or via browser-level privacy controls. KRIM OS respects standard Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals sent by client browsers.
