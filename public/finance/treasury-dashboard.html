Current operating model

Customers contract with and pay their selected logistics partner directly. KRIM does not collect, hold, escrow, transfer or settle partner freight funds. Any future KRIM fee or financial feature requires separate signed terms, legal and tax review, verified bank/provider setup and tested accounting controls before activation.

Historical payment-provider reconciliation and authorised internal accounting records remain separate from customer billing and partner payout services.
