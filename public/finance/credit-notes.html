KRIM LOGISTICS · PARTNER-DIRECT LAUNCH CONTROLS

Credit Notes & Invoice Adjustments

KRIM does not issue credit notes or adjust partner freight invoices. Invoice corrections and refunds are handled by the contracting logistics partner.

Not an active finance, bank, compliance or payout workflow.
All former sample keys, access scopes, bank names, balances, transfer references, counterparties, forecast values, compliance statuses, credit notes, payout amounts, expense totals and demo buttons on this page are retired. Nothing here creates or changes a bank connection, API credential, invoice, credit note, payment, reconciliation, compliance decision or accounting entry.

Current operating model

Customers contract with and pay their selected logistics partner directly. The partner confirms freight pricing, invoice or receipt, payment beneficiary, terms and any correction or refund. KRIM does not collect, hold, escrow, transfer or settle partner freight funds.

Historical payment-provider reconciliation and authorised internal accounting records remain separate from customer billing and partner payout services. Any future KRIM fee or financial feature requires signed terms, legal and tax review, verified banking/provider setup and tested accounting controls before activation.
