Partner-direct launch

Customers contract with and pay the logistics partner directly. The partner issues its freight invoice or receipt and handles any freight invoice correction under the direct contract and applicable law. KRIM must not issue a partner freight tax invoice.

Any future KRIM referral or coordination fee requires separate signed partner terms, confirmed GST treatment, applicable registration/threshold review, correct invoice and accounting design, and tested controls before collection or recognition.

This status page is operational guidance, not a tax opinion or proof that KRIM is exempt from GST registration. The exact model must be reviewed by a qualified Indian CA before paid commercial transactions.
