Safety invariant

No blind fallback

If a provider result is uncertain, KRIM must reconcile the original order first. A timeout or closed checkout is not proof that no charge occurred.

  • Never auto-charge another provider
  • Idempotency before retry
  • Verified state before shipment progression

Unified payment lifecycle

Provider-specific events must resolve into one canonical KRIM transaction state before they affect shipment, invoices, revenue or partner settlement.

01 · ELIGIBILITYCountry, currency, method, account approval and quote checked.
02 · ORDERServer creates/reuses an order bound to one quote and shipment.
03 · VERIFYSignature, provider state, amount, currency and references validated.
04 · LEDGERIdempotent financial record, fees and refund states reconciled.
05 · OPERATIONSShipment and partner payout advance only under explicit rules.
