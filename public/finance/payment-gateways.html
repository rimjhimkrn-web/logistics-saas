KRIM centralizes payment order creation, provider verification, webhook reconciliation and financial records. Razorpay is the only enabled checkout provider today. New provider adapters remain disabled until commercial eligibility, country support, settlement and reconciliation controls have been validated.

Enabled integration

Razorpay

Current customer checkout route. KRIM creates orders on the server and checks the accepted quote, shipment, partner assignment, amount and currency before payment.

  • INR quote-bound checkout
  • Server-side order creation
  • Signature and provider-status verification
  • Idempotent financial transaction recording

International card acceptance

Availability depends on merchant account approval, account settings, customer country, currency, card network and provider rules. KRIM does not assume these capabilities are enabled.

Future adapter slot

Regional payment providers

Provider B/C are architectural extension points—not connected payment services. Add only when customer demand, local methods, legal eligibility and operational support justify integration.

  • No automatic second-provider charge
  • Shared payment-order lifecycle
  • Provider-specific verification and webhooks

Global rollout gates

KRIM must not advertise a payment method as available merely because a provider supports it in general. Market and method eligibility should be evaluated against the merchant's approved account, the transaction's currency, the customer's location, regulatory constraints, fees and settlement options.
