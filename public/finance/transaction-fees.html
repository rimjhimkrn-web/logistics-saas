Current operating model

Customers contract with and pay the selected logistics partner directly. That partner confirms the freight service, final price, invoice or receipt, payment beneficiary, terms and any freight cancellation or refund. KRIM does not collect, hold, escrow, transfer or settle partner freight funds.

Historical provider records and authorised internal reconciliation remain separate from customer checkout and partner payout services. Any future KRIM fee or financial workflow requires separate signed terms, legal and tax review, verified provider/bank arrangements and tested accounting controls before activation.
