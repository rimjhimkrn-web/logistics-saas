Payment policy · Market-aware design

Payment methods customers can trust.

KRIM presents only methods that are actually enabled for the transaction. Availability depends on Razorpay merchant approval, country, currency, customer payment instrument and provider configuration.

UPI and Indian payment instruments

Potentially available for eligible INR transactions when enabled on KRIM's Razorpay account. The checkout must use provider-returned options; KRIM does not promise a specific method until verified.

Eligibility check required

Indian debit and credit cards

May be offered when enabled by the merchant account and supported for the transaction. Payment completion is confirmed server-side, not from the browser's success screen alone.

Account dependent

International cards and currencies

International acceptance is subject to provider approval, supported countries, account settings, currency and settlement rules. The current KRIM checkout is INR-only until broader currency handling is implemented and tested.

Not yet verified

International bank transfer and local wallets

Future market-specific options require a validated collection and reconciliation workflow. They are not represented as connected checkout methods today.

Future capability
Single payment safety rule: if a payment result is unclear, KRIM must reconcile the original order before allowing a retry. It must never automatically charge a customer through another provider because the first provider appears to have failed.
