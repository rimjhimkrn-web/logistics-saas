Current operating model

Customers contract with and pay their selected logistics partner directly. KRIM does not collect, hold, escrow, transfer or settle partner freight funds. Partner freight invoices, receipts, cancellations and refunds are handled by the contracting partner under the direct agreement.

This page is not a live financial workflow, bank connection, payment instruction, invoice, payout approval or proof of funds. Any future KRIM fee or financial service requires separate legal/tax review, signed terms, verified banking/provider setup and tested accounting controls before activation.
