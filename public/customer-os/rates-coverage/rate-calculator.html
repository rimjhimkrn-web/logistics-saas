Before relying on a rate

  • Verify the issuing partner, rate source and exact version.
  • Match lane, cargo, service mode, equipment and shipment dates.
  • Check minimums, surcharges, exclusions, free time and validity.
  • Confirm transaction currency, tax basis and payment terms.

Commercial boundaries

  • This is a planning estimate, not a binding offer or booking.
  • Partners issue and fulfil logistics services and their freight invoices.
  • Any KRIM coordination or referral fee must be separately identified and agreed.
  • Do not assume partner capacity or route availability from a calculation.
