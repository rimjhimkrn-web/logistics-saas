Currency integrity: A display conversion does not change the currency stated on a quote, contract, invoice, payment or settlement. Always use the rate source and effective time required by the relevant agreement and accounting rules.

Manual FX conversion

Enter a positive rate: 1 unit of the source currency equals the entered number of target-currency units. No exchange-rate data is fetched or stored.

Use three-letter currency codes such as INR, USD, EUR, AED or GBP. The code is validated for format; this page does not verify the rate provider or rate authenticity.

How to use the result

  • Confirm the source and effective date of the rate before relying on the estimate.
  • Check whether bank fees, spreads, transfer charges or contractual rounding rules apply.
  • Keep the original transaction amount and currency visible alongside any converted display value.
  • Use the appropriate authorized accounting or tax rate for statutory reporting.

What this tool does not do

  • It does not fetch live market rates or guarantee an exchange rate.
  • It does not execute currency exchange, transfer funds or calculate bank fees.
  • It does not alter quote, contract, invoice, payment or settlement records.
  • It does not determine tax treatment or accounting conversion rules.

Global operating standard

KRIM connects customers with independently verified logistics partners. A currency selection does not establish service coverage, partner capacity or a payable price. Freight charges remain subject to the applicable customer–provider agreement and its stated transaction currency.
