Price Audit

Understand the evidence required to review a quote calculation, trace changes and explain why a final amount differs from an earlier estimate.

← Return to workspaceReview quote details
STAGING REVIEW MODE · Illustrative guidance only. No live quote, contract, rate, FX feed, coverage decision or shipment location is loaded. No persistent writes are available on this page.

A price audit must be based on authorized records and immutable calculation evidence. This guide does not access customer financial records.

Audit evidence

  • Original request attributes and shipment scope
  • Quote ID, exact version, issuing partner and timestamps
  • Line-item amounts, charge basis and ISO 4217 currency
  • Surcharges, exclusions, tax basis and rounding rules
  • Approvals, amendments, acceptance and relevant notifications

Review sequence

  • Match the quote to the correct shipment and customer organization.
  • Recalculate each line using the recorded basis and precision.
  • Compare versions and identify material changes.
  • Verify authorized approvals and the effective terms.
  • Record findings, reviewer identity and follow-up actions.

Integrity controls

  • Preserve original records; corrections create traceable amendments.
  • Restrict access by organization and role.
  • Use UTC event timestamps and display them in the saved timezone.
  • Never silently substitute an FX rate or change transaction currency.

Global operating standard

KRIM connects customers with independently verified logistics partners. Catalogue presence, currency selection or a displayed country does not establish operational eligibility. Market, corridor, partner capability, capacity, approved pricing, compliance and fulfilment must each be verified before a service is offered.
