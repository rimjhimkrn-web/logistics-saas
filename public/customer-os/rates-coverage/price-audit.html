Evidence checklist

Review the underlying source records before drawing a conclusion.

  • Correct customer organization, shipment scope and quote reference.
  • Exact quote version, issuer and validity timestamp.
  • Line-item amounts, units, quantities and charge basis.
  • Included charges, surcharges, exclusions and tax treatment.
  • Transaction currency, FX source if contractually relevant, and rounding.
  • Approval, amendment, acceptance and notification history.

Important boundaries

  • Do not replace the original quote or overwrite its history.
  • Keep provider freight charges separate from any KRIM partner fee.
  • Do not infer a tax or accounting rate from a display conversion.
  • Escalate unexplained differences to the authorized quote issuer.

Recommended review sequence

01 · Match

Confirm the customer, shipment, quote reference, version and validity.

02 · Recalculate

Rebuild each line from its recorded quantity, rate basis and applicable charges.

03 · Explain

Identify each variance and verify the supporting approval or amendment.

04 · Record securely

Send findings through the authorized customer or support workflow for traceable review.

Global operating standard

KRIM connects customers with independently verified logistics partners. A price comparison does not establish route coverage, partner capacity, a confirmed booking or a payable price. Freight terms remain subject to the applicable customer–provider agreement. KRIM’s separately agreed partner marketing, referral or coordination fee is distinct from provider freight charges.
