Shipper Rate Calculator

Prepare a shipment-specific planning estimate using the commercial inputs supplied by your logistics partner. Keep shipment context and rate assumptions visible before requesting or reviewing a formal quote.

Request a partner quoteView quotes
This is a local planning tool, not a rate search or quote engine. KRIM does not retrieve live partner pricing here. Use only a rate supplied by the issuing partner; route details do not automatically establish service coverage, capacity or availability.

Describe the shipment, then enter the partner's unit rate and any fixed charges. The calculator applies the arithmetic shown below and does not save or transmit your inputs.

Shipment and rate inputs

Shipment context is for your own reference only; it is not checked against a live partner network.

Formula: (billable quantity × partner-entered unit rate) + fixed charges + additional charges = gross subtotal. Discount is deducted from gross subtotal; the entered tax assumption is then applied to the discounted amount. Confirm the actual partner's charge basis and tax treatment; they may differ.

Shipper checks before acceptance

  • Confirm billable quantity and unit, including any volumetric or chargeable-weight rule.
  • Match origin, destination, mode, cargo and shipment dates to the partner offer.
  • Check minimum charges, surcharges, exclusions, validity and service conditions.
  • Verify currency, tax basis, payment milestones and cancellation terms.

Who provides the service?

  • Logistics partners issue freight rates, provide the service and issue freight invoices.
  • KRIM connects customers with logistics partners through its platform.
  • Any separately agreed KRIM coordination or referral fee must be clearly distinguished from partner freight charges.
  • Only a valid partner-issued offer and confirmed operational acceptance establish the commercial commitment.
