Price Audit — Rates

A practical control framework for checking rate provenance, applicability, version history and calculation accuracy before a logistics offer is relied upon.

View quote workspaceRate calculator
This page provides audit guidance only. It does not load a live rate card, issue a quote, approve a price or change a shipment. Confirm all commercial terms with the issuing logistics partner before acceptance.

A rate is usable only when its source, scope, effective period, charge basis and issuing party can be verified. A displayed amount alone is not proof of an available service or binding offer.

Rate provenance

Record where the rate came from and who is accountable for it.

  • Issuing partner and authorized source document
  • Rate card, contract or quote reference and version
  • Published or received timestamp and effective dates
  • Currency, market, lane and service scope
  • Named owner for clarification and amendments

Applicability checks

Confirm that the exact shipment fits the commercial conditions.

  • Origin, destination, route and service mode
  • Cargo type, dimensions, weight and handling class
  • Equipment, capacity and operational constraints
  • Minimum charge, rate unit and billable quantity
  • Validity, exclusions, surcharges and free-time terms

Calculation integrity

Reperform the calculation from retained source values.

  • Preserve unrounded line-level source amounts
  • Verify units, conversion factors and charge basis
  • Check rounding precision and calculation order
  • Separate taxes, duties and third-party pass-throughs
  • Reconcile subtotal, adjustments and final total

Governance and audit trail

Keep every decision traceable and attributable.

  • Retain original rate and every revised version
  • Record reviewer, review time and evidence references
  • Require independent approval where policy demands
  • Log overrides with reason and authorisation
  • Notify affected users when a material rate changes

Commercial responsibility

Logistics partners issue and fulfil their services and remain responsible for their freight rates and freight invoices. KRIM provides the platform connecting customers and partners. Any separately agreed KRIM coordination or referral fee must be identified distinctly from partner freight charges. Do not treat a reference rate or audit result as a booking confirmation, capacity commitment or guaranteed price.
