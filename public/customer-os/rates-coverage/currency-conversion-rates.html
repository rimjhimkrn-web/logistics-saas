Record rate provenance

  • Provider and exact rate product.
  • Base and quote currencies with explicit direction.
  • Publication or retrieval timestamp and timezone.
  • Rate type, validity window and contractual eligibility.
  • Spread, transfer fee, intermediary charges and rounding rule.

Spreads, fees and rounding

  • A reference rate may differ from an executable provider rate.
  • Percentage spreads and fixed transfer fees are separate costs.
  • Intermediary and receiving-bank charges may be additional.
  • Rounding precision can change the final payable amount.
  • Confirm final charges with the provider before committing funds.

KRIM quote and settlement integrity

  • A display conversion does not change contractual quote currency.
  • Partner freight charges remain subject to the logistics provider's issued offer and terms.
  • Payment and settlement currencies must be explicitly confirmed.
  • KRIM's separately agreed referral or coordination fee is distinct from partner freight charges.
  • Use applicable statutory and accounting FX rules for tax and books.

Before accepting a converted amount

Confirm the provider's final executable rate, validity, fees, payment cut-off, beneficiary details and settlement currency. An indicative conversion is not proof that a payment can be made or that a logistics service is available.
