This manifest is an architecture and readiness guide, not proof that every connector, market, payment rail or notification channel is active. A listed integration must pass its own end-to-end tests before being represented as operational.

Integration readiness controls

Core platform and identity

  • Supabase-backed identity, organization membership, role permissions and row-level security protect customer records.
  • Public staging previews must not require production authentication or read/write customer data.
  • Production access remains authenticated and server-authorized; frontend checks are not substitutes for RLS or backend validation.
  • Every integration must preserve organization scope, audit events and idempotency.

Payments and finance

  • KRIM's current launch model is partner-direct freight payment; KRIM does not hold freight funds or settle partner payouts.
  • Historical KRIM-collected payments and separately invoiced KRIM service fees remain distinct from provider freight invoices.
  • Razorpay-related code presence does not mean customer freight checkout is enabled under the current model.
  • Currency, tax, refunds, credit notes and reconciliation must follow the actual legal supplier and jurisdiction.

Notifications and external channels

  • Essential account, quote, booking, payment, shipment, exception, ePOD, invoice, refund and support notifications are mandatory ecosystem requirements.
  • In-app, email and push delivery need event triggers, permission/consent handling where legally required, receipts, retries and monitoring.
  • The latest audit found no demonstrated notification outbox/delivery records or end-to-end customer receipt test.
  • Do not claim email, Telegram or push delivery is live until real delivery and failure/retry tests pass.

Global activation gates

  • A country, language, currency or timezone catalogue does not activate an operating market or corridor.
  • Confirm verified partner capability, capacity, approved rate, compliance and payment-provider eligibility per shipment.
  • Use ISO 4217 currency, UTC event storage, IANA timezones, canonical units and country-specific address/legal fields.
  • Professional translation, RTL presentation and localized tax logic must be verified for each supported locale.
Global readiness rule. Store event timestamps in UTC and display them in the saved IANA timezone. Use ISO 4217 transaction currencies, canonical stored measurement units, country-specific address and legal fields, locale-aware dates/numbers and RTL only where supported. Never infer live market, corridor, partner, rate, capacity, compliance or payment eligibility from a catalogue or display preference. Essential service and security notifications remain mandatory, with delivery status and retry handling; marketing preferences are separate.
