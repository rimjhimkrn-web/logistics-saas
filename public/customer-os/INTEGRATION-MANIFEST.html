Integration Manifest

One transparent reference for how KRIM's customer workflows connect to identity, shipment operations, partner services, documents, finance and communications—along with the controls required before an integration is treated as production-ready.

Customer ecosystemOpen shipment workspace
Readiness principle: a page, API, database object, configured secret or provider account is not by itself proof of a working integration. Each capability must pass authorization, failure, retry, audit and end-to-end delivery tests before being represented as live.

This manifest describes integration boundaries and acceptance requirements. It is not a live monitoring console and does not expose credentials, customer records, webhook payloads, provider secrets or private operational data.

Integration domains

Core platform

Identity, organization and permissions

Supabase Auth and organization-scoped data access underpin protected customer workflows.

  • Authenticate the user and verify current organization membership for every protected operation.
  • Enforce role and permission checks server-side; browser visibility is never authorization.
  • Apply row-level security and least-privilege access to exposed data and storage.
  • Use idempotency for retryable create/accept/transition operations and retain audit history.
  • Never expose service-role credentials or privileged keys in browser code.
Shipment operations

Shipment and tracking lifecycle

Customer shipment creation, status, milestones and tracking must remain linked to authorized organizations and partner-supplied events.

  • Use canonical shipment identifiers and server-controlled state transitions.
  • Record event source, event time, ingestion time and timezone context.
  • Do not fabricate GPS positions, milestones, ETAs, capacity or delivery completion.
  • Partner operational events must be validated before they change customer-visible status.
  • Duplicate, delayed and out-of-order webhook events need deterministic handling.
Quotes and pricing

Partner offers and customer decisions

Quote records must preserve the issuing partner, exact version, price basis, validity and acceptance history.

  • Separate partner-issued freight charges from any separately agreed KRIM fee.
  • Check partner verification, current capability, compliance and shipment eligibility.
  • Reject expired or superseded versions; preserve immutable decision references.
  • Internal approval, customer quote acceptance and shipment booking are separate events.
  • Never present a manually entered estimate as a live partner rate.
Documents and evidence

POD and ePOD vault

Delivery evidence is sensitive operational data and requires stronger controls than a public document link.

  • Restrict access to authenticated users with active organization and shipment authorization.
  • Only independently verified proof may be represented as verified.
  • Issue short-lived signed links (60 seconds where the configured vault flow supports it); never expose permanent object URLs.
  • Log document view/download, verification decisions, denials and expiry events.
  • Retain document versions and follow applicable retention/legal-hold requirements.
Finance boundary

Payments, invoices and settlement

The initial operating model keeps partner freight collection separate from KRIM's own revenue.

  • Customer contracts with and pays the logistics partner directly for freight services.
  • KRIM's separately agreed referral/marketing/coordination fee is distinct from provider freight charges.
  • Do not route, hold or settle partner freight funds through KRIM unless a separately approved legal and payment model is implemented.
  • Razorpay code or keys do not mean customer freight checkout or partner split settlement is active.
  • Invoices, taxes, refunds and credit notes must identify the actual supplier and legal basis.
End-to-end validation required

Notifications and messaging

Essential service notifications are a mandatory part of the customer operating system, not an optional marketing preference.

  • Trigger in-app/email/SMS/push events for security, account, quote, booking, payment, shipment, exception, POD, invoice, refund and support changes as applicable.
  • Track queued, sent, delivered, failed and retried states with correlation IDs.
  • Retry transient failures safely; alert operations when critical messages remain undelivered.
  • Respect consent and legal requirements for channels while keeping essential service notices distinct from marketing.
  • Telegram, Resend and WhatsApp delivery must not be called live until real send, receipt, failure and retry tests pass.

Globalization and interoperability

Canonical data standards

  • Persist event timestamps in UTC; display using the user's saved IANA timezone.
  • Use ISO 4217 currency codes and currency-specific precision for every amount.
  • Store canonical measurement units and label converted display values.
  • Use country-aware addresses, postal formats, phone numbers, tax identifiers and legal entity fields.
  • Version API contracts and maintain stable identifiers across systems.

Market activation controls

  • Country and currency catalogues are not proof of active operations.
  • Enable a corridor only after partner capability, capacity, compliance and service terms are verified.
  • Apply jurisdiction-specific tax, customs, restricted-goods and data-retention rules.
  • Validate translation, date/number formatting, accessibility and RTL behavior per locale.
  • Show customers only service options that are currently confirmed for their shipment.
