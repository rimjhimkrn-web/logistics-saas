Customer Spot Approvals

Review and approve a specific, time-limited spot quote only after its terms, provider identity and operational eligibility have been verified.

STAGING REVIEW MODE · Public, read-only preview. No customer case records, claims, financial balances, partner scores or analytics are loaded. No persistent writes or workflow submissions are available here.

No live spot quotes are loaded in staging. An approval applies only to the exact quote version shown and does not by itself confirm a booking, pickup or transport execution.

Workflow controls

Before approval

  • Verify origin, destination, transport mode, cargo details and requested dates.
  • Confirm partner identity, capability, capacity and route/corridor eligibility.
  • Review charge basis, accessorials, exclusions, validity deadline and ISO 4217 currency.
  • Check applicable customs, restricted-goods, insurance and regulatory requirements.

Approval integrity

  • Bind approval to an immutable quote version and exact customer organization.
  • Reject expired, withdrawn or superseded offers and require a new quote version.
  • Capture approver identity, authority, timestamp and any required approval reason.
  • An approved quote remains separate from booking confirmation and payment confirmation.

Global display rules

  • Render dates in the saved IANA timezone while preserving UTC source timestamps.
  • Display amounts with explicit currency codes and correct currency precision.
  • Convert weights and dimensions only for display and label the units.
  • A selected country, language or currency does not establish corridor availability.
Global readiness rule. Store event timestamps in UTC and display them in the saved IANA timezone. Use ISO 4217 transaction currencies, canonical stored measurement units, country-specific address and legal fields, locale-aware dates/numbers and RTL only where supported. Never infer live market, corridor, partner, rate, capacity, compliance or payment eligibility from a catalogue or display preference. Essential service and security notifications remain mandatory, with delivery status and retry handling; marketing preferences are separate.
