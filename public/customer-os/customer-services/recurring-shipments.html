How repeat shipment works

  • Select a previous shipment from your authorized organization.
  • Review and update origin, destination, cargo, dimensions, weight, dates and service requirements.
  • Request current pricing and confirm partner eligibility and capacity again.
  • Submit a new booking request only after reviewing the new draft.

Scheduling transparency

This workspace does not silently schedule future dispatches or create recurring jobs. Automated schedules, approval rules and dispatch automation require a separately enabled, tested and authorized scheduling service.

Essential operational notifications remain separate from marketing preferences. New booking, quote, pickup, delay and delivery events must generate their required customer notifications.
