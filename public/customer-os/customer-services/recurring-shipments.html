Fresh confirmation every time

A previous shipment is a reference—not a standing booking, guaranteed rate or reserved capacity.

No automatic dispatch is created. Each repeat movement is a new shipment request. Any future-date scheduling or recurring dispatch arrangement must be separately agreed and confirmed with the logistics service partner.
