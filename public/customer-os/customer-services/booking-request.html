One shipment request.
A world of logistics possibilities.

Create a shipment request for domestic or cross-border freight. Choose origin and destination across countries, describe the cargo, and state your preferred movement. KRIM must separately validate service coverage, cargo eligibility, compliance, partner capacity and an approved price before a shipment can be booked.

Booking integrity safeguard · submission currently paused
The production booking RPC has a documented conflict with mandatory shipment corridor controls. Booking remains disabled until the atomic mode-aware backend fix is tested and approved. Notifications remain mandatory for release: the booking-created event must create an in-app record with history, delivery state and retry handling; essential service/security alerts cannot be disabled, and marketing preferences stay separate. Customer email / push delivery is not yet verified end-to-end, so no delivery is claimed here.

Search worldwide for cities, towns, airports, seaports, terminals and addresses. Select a suggested result for each endpoint. These results identify geography only; they do not confirm an active market, a freight lane, customs clearance or carrier coverage.
02 / Route type
Choose the route arrangement that best describes the request. KRIM records it as a preference; actual route legs and handoffs must be confirmed.
03 / Transport network preference
Your preference is saved on the shipment record. KRIM must still confirm geographic feasibility, customs or trade requirements, available partners, capacity and pricing before accepting a booking.
04 / Service expectation
This selection is passed to the quote workflow. It does not guarantee a mode, carrier, serviceability, transit time, or price; those require backend confirmation.

Describe the goods accurately. Restricted, dangerous or regulated cargo may require additional compliance review.
