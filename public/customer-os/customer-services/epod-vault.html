ePOD Vault

A secure recordkeeping destination for electronic proof of delivery. Access should be limited to independently verified delivery documents for shipments your organization is authorized to view.

View shipmentsPOD download guidance
Electronic proof of delivery is evidence of a recorded delivery event—not a guarantee that every shipment has a document. A file should appear only after it has been uploaded, reviewed and independently verified under the applicable workflow.

Use the controls below to understand how an ePOD record should be identified, verified, accessed and retained. No document, shipment record or download link is displayed on this public page.

What belongs in the vault

Only delivery evidence linked to a known shipment and an authorized customer organization.

  • Shipment reference and delivery event identifier
  • Document type, upload time and source
  • Verification state and independent reviewer record
  • Recipient acknowledgement or exception evidence, where applicable
  • Document integrity metadata and retention classification

Access protection

Document access must be enforced server-side, not by hiding links in the browser.

  • Require an authenticated user and active organization membership
  • Verify shipment-to-organization authorization on every request
  • Expose only independently verified proof of delivery
  • Issue short-lived signed access links; never publish permanent public file URLs
  • Record access outcomes and deny unauthorized or expired requests

Verification and exceptions

Preserve the distinction between a submitted file and verified delivery evidence.

  • Check shipment reference and document association
  • Review legibility, completeness and required acknowledgements
  • Route missing, damaged or conflicting evidence for review
  • Keep reviewer identity and decision timestamps
  • Do not allow the uploader to approve their own evidence where independent review is required

Retention and audit

Maintain a defensible record without exposing documents beyond their authorized purpose.

  • Apply the organization's retention and legal-hold rules
  • Keep document version and replacement history
  • Log download, denial, expiry and verification events
  • Restrict exports and onward sharing
  • Use secure deletion only under the applicable retention policy

Recommended document lifecycle

01 · RECEIVEDocument submittedPartner or authorized workflow supplies evidence linked to the shipment.
02 · REVIEWValidate evidenceCheck shipment association, completeness and required proof details.
03 · VERIFYIndependent decisionAuthorized reviewer confirms evidence; unresolved records remain unverified.
04 · ACCESSTime-limited retrievalAuthorized organization users receive short-lived, auditable access.
Important: An uploaded, pending-review, rejected or expired document must not be presented as verified proof of delivery. If a document is unavailable, contact the responsible logistics partner or customer support rather than relying on an unverified copy.

Service notifications

Essential shipment and document events—such as proof submitted, verification completed, verification rejected, document access failure or an expiring retrieval link—should be delivered through the configured service-notification channels. These operational notifications are distinct from optional marketing communications.
