How the current model works

Freight services are supplied by the selected logistics partner. KRIM connects the parties and provides the digital workflow.

01

Service & invoice

The customer confirms service terms with the logistics provider. The provider issues its freight invoice and defines applicable payment terms.

Provider-issued document
02

Direct customer payment

The customer pays the provider directly using the payment method and account details confirmed by that provider.

Customer → provider
03

Separate KRIM fee, if applicable

Any agreed KRIM referral, marketing or coordination fee is separate, documented independently and invoiced by the appropriate party.

Separate commercial arrangement

Settlement responsibilities

Each party remains responsible for the obligations attached to its own service, invoice and funds.

Logistics provider

The provider supplies the transport or logistics service and manages its own freight billing.

  • Issues freight invoices and receipts.
  • Confirms receipt of customer payment.
  • Handles provider-side refunds, adjustments and subcontractor payouts.

Customer / shipper

The customer reviews the provider's commercial terms and pays the provider as agreed.

  • Checks invoice issuer, amount, currency and due date.
  • Uses payment details independently verified with the provider.
  • Retains payment confirmation and raises discrepancies promptly.

KRIM Logistics

KRIM provides the platform connection and workflow, without taking custody of partner freight funds in this model.

  • Does not operate freight escrow or partner payout settlement here.
  • Does not confirm provider payment unless supported by verified evidence.
  • Documents any separate KRIM fee under its own applicable terms.

Records & reconciliation

Keep each financial record tied to the correct legal entity and transaction.

  • Match invoice, payment reference and receipt.
  • Keep freight charges separate from KRIM service fees.
  • Resolve provider invoice disputes with the issuing provider.
i
Important payment safeguard

Before sending funds, verify the invoice issuer, beneficiary name, bank or payment details and reference directly with the logistics provider through a trusted channel. Never rely only on a message or changed bank details received unexpectedly.

Future payment and settlement capabilities

Any future KRIM collection, escrow, split-payment or partner-payout feature would require separate legal, tax, regulatory, payment-provider and security review before activation. No balance, payout, transfer or settlement instruction is created by this page. Tax treatment depends on the actual arrangement and applicable law; this page is not tax or legal advice.
