Freight payment & settlement

KRIM's initial operating model is partner-direct. KRIM does not collect freight funds or settle freight payments between customers and logistics partners.

Pay the contracted logistics partner directly.
Your selected partner confirms the final freight amount, invoice, beneficiary, payment method and payment terms. Obtain the partner's receipt and keep your payment evidence.

What KRIM records

KRIM may display historical payment records for reconciliation. Those records do not mean KRIM currently offers freight checkout, escrow, wallet balances or partner payouts. Any future KRIM fee would be separately described and legally reviewed before activation.
