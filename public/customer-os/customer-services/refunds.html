KRIM connects customers with logistics service partners. Under the partner-direct payment model, freight charges are paid by the customer to the service provider. Refund responsibility follows the party that received the payment and the applicable agreement.

Logistics partner

Freight charges & service refunds

For freight payments made directly to a logistics partner, contact the provider named on the accepted quote, invoice or payment receipt.

  • Review the provider’s cancellation, service-failure and refund terms.
  • Share the provider invoice, payment receipt and shipment reference.
  • Ask the provider to confirm the refund decision, amount, currency and processing timeline.
  • KRIM cannot reverse or refund freight funds it did not receive.
KRIM Logistics

Separate KRIM charges

If a separate fee was charged by KRIM, raise the query with KRIM support so it can be reviewed against the relevant agreement and transaction record.

  • Include the KRIM transaction reference and the reason for your request.
  • A support request is a review—not an automatic refund approval.
  • Any approved refund is handled under the applicable KRIM terms and payment-provider rules.
  • Do not assume a partner freight payment and a KRIM fee are the same transaction.

How to raise a refund concern

01

Identify the payment

Check the invoice and receipt to confirm who received the payment and which shipment or service it relates to.

02

Prepare your evidence

Keep the shipment reference, invoice, payment receipt and a short timeline of the issue ready.

03

Contact the responsible party

Contact the logistics provider for partner-collected freight payments, or KRIM support for a separate KRIM transaction.

Protect your payment information. Share only the references and documents needed to review your case. Never send a password, one-time password (OTP), full card number or complete bank credentials. Refund decisions and processing times are confirmed by the party responsible for the original payment.
Provider-direct refund responsibility: Provider-direct refunds: refunds for partner-provided freight or logistics services must be requested from the logistics provider that received the payment. This page must not simulate an automated refund request. KRIM does not hold partner freight funds, guarantee provider refunds, or issue refunds on a provider’s behalf. This page does not submit an automated refund request. KRIM can help route a support enquiry and record the status of communications.
