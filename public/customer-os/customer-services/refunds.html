CUSTOMER ACCOUNT & SERVICES · PAYMENT POLICY

Refunds are handled by the party that received the payment.

KRIM currently operates a partner-direct freight payment model. The customer contracts with and pays the selected logistics provider directly; the provider handles its own freight invoices, receipts, refunds and payment disputes.

KRIM does not currently collect freight charges, hold escrow or execute partner freight refunds. This page is guidance only. It does not submit a refund request, approve compensation or initiate a payment.

For a freight refund

Contact the logistics provider named on the accepted quote and its invoice. Follow the payment and cancellation terms in your agreement with that provider. KRIM cannot reverse a payment it did not receive.

For a separate KRIM fee or an older KRIM payment

If you have a separate KRIM-issued invoice or a historical KRIM-collected payment record, create a secure support ticket with the invoice or payment reference. KRIM will review only its own records. A ticket is not an automatic refund approval.

Before submitting a case

  • Include the shipment, invoice or historical payment reference.
  • Describe the issue and the outcome requested.
  • Do not include card numbers, passwords, OTPs or full bank credentials.
  • Keep the original provider invoice and proof of payment.
