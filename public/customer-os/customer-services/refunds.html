Refunds & complaints

Freight services are contracted and paid for directly between the customer and the selected logistics partner. Refund responsibility follows that direct agreement.

Freight refund responsibility
If you paid freight charges directly to a logistics partner, contact that partner to request a cancellation, adjustment or refund. The partner is responsible for deciding and processing refunds under your contract and applicable law. KRIM does not receive, hold or refund those partner-collected freight payments.

How KRIM can help

KRIM can receive a platform complaint, help identify the relevant shipment or partner contact, and route the complaint through its support process. This does not guarantee a refund or replace your contractual rights against the partner.

For a KRIM service fee, if one is introduced and separately charged in future, its refund terms will be disclosed separately before payment. No such fee should be assumed from a freight quotation.
