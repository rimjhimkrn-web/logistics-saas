How quote requests work

A transparent process from shipment requirements to a partner-confirmed offer.

Define shipment requirementsConfirm route, cargo, dimensions, weight, dates, handling and transport mode.
Eligibility reviewKRIM checks the active market/corridor, partner capability, capacity and compliance before representing service availability.
Partner submits an offerOnly a saved, authorized partner quote with explicit currency, validity and service scope is shown.
Review and decideCompare quote terms and follow the separate approval and acceptance workflow.
Quote requests, revisions, expiry and partner responses should trigger essential customer notifications. Operational alerts cannot be silently disabled through marketing preferences.
Pricing is not a booking confirmation. A quote does not reserve capacity until the relevant partner and booking workflow explicitly confirm it. Customers contract and settle freight directly with the logistics provider under the applicable arrangement; KRIM does not claim external freight payment has been received.
