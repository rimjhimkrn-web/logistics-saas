How the quote process works

A transparent sequence from shipment requirements to a partner-confirmed offer.

Define shipment requirementsConfirm route, cargo, dimensions, weight, dates, handling needs and transport mode.
Check service eligibilityRoute, active market, partner capability, capacity and relevant compliance must be confirmed.
Partner submits an offerOnly an authorized quote with explicit currency, validity and service scope is presented.
Review and decideCompare terms and continue through the separate approval and acceptance workflow.
Important: A quote is not a booking confirmation and does not reserve capacity unless the partner and booking workflow explicitly confirm it. Customers contract with and settle freight directly with the logistics provider under the applicable arrangement. KRIM does not represent partner freight charges as KRIM revenue or claim external freight payment has been received.
Service notifications: Quote submission, partner response, revision, expiry and acceptance events should generate essential customer notifications. These operational alerts remain distinct from optional marketing preferences.
