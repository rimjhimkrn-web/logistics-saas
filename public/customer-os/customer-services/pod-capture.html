From handover to verified evidence

Each stage has a defined owner. A delivery status alone does not prove that the supporting document has passed review.

01

Capture at handover

The assigned driver or authorized partner records the permitted delivery evidence against the correct shipment and leg.

02

Validate submission

File type, size, integrity, malware safety and required metadata are checked before the evidence is accepted for review.

03

Independent review

An authorized reviewer checks the submission. The person who submitted evidence cannot approve their own submission.

04

Release securely

Verified evidence becomes available to authorized customer users through short-lived, access-controlled links.

What a trustworthy POD record contains

Capture only what is needed to evidence the handover, protect personal information and preserve a reliable audit trail.

Submission essentials

Evidence is associated with a shipment and a specific delivery event, not simply uploaded into a general file area.

  • Shipment and delivery-leg reference.
  • Submission time recorded in UTC with original metadata preserved.
  • Permitted handover evidence and minimum necessary recipient details.
  • Reason and context for partial, failed or disputed delivery.

Review and audit controls

Evidence moves through explicit states so users can distinguish a new submission from a verified record.

  • Submitted, under review, verified and rejected states.
  • Second authorized reviewer where policy requires it.
  • Submitter and reviewer identities, timestamps and decisions.
  • Immutable event history for material status changes.

Privacy and file security

Delivery evidence can contain personal information. Access and retention must follow applicable law and the relevant service agreement.

  • Private storage with no public bucket exposure.
  • Organization-scoped and role-authorized access.
  • Short-lived signed URLs issued after server-side checks.
  • Retention, deletion and disclosure controls appropriate to jurisdiction.

Customer visibility

Customers can review delivery status and retrieve proof only when the relevant evidence has passed verification and access checks.

  • A delivery milestone is not automatically verified POD.
  • Missing, rejected and disputed evidence remains clearly identified.
  • Verified evidence is linked to its shipment record.
  • Essential delivery and security notifications are tracked and retried.

Keep submission and approval independent

This customer information page does not collect signatures, photos, recipient details or files. Actual capture belongs in the authenticated operational workflow for the assigned driver or partner. Customers should never be asked to create or self-verify the carrier's proof of delivery.
