Know which invoice you need

Two separate commercial relationships may exist within one shipment journey.

01 / KRIM

Platform & agreed fees

Where KRIM has separately contracted to provide a service or coordination arrangement, KRIM may issue its own invoice for that charge.

02 / PROVIDER

Freight & logistics services

The logistics partner contracting with the customer issues its freight invoice directly to the customer. It may sit outside the KRIM invoice register.

03 / RECORD

Adjustments & evidence

Corrections must follow the issuing entity's compliant invoice and credit/debit note process, with the original record and audit trail preserved.

What a valid invoice record should show

Fields vary by issuer and jurisdiction; these are common review points.

Supplier & transaction identity

Supplier's legal name, customer legal entity, unique invoice number, issue date, relevant service description and shipment reference where applicable.

Amounts & tax context

Explicit ISO currency, line amounts, tax breakdown where applicable, total, due date and any recorded payment or adjustment. Currency alone does not determine tax treatment.

Important: This information page does not create an invoice, confirm a payment, determine tax liability or replace an invoice issued by your logistics provider. For private invoice records, use the authenticated register for your active organization.
