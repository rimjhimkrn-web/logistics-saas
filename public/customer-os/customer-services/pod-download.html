Before a document can be downloaded

Every document request should pass a fresh authorization and evidence-state check. A previous link or successful download does not grant continuing access.

01

Confirm the shipment

Identify the correct shipment and delivery leg. Do not rely on a filename or an unverified reference alone.

02

Verify the evidence

The proof record must be independently reviewed and marked verified before customer release.

03

Authorize access

Confirm active organization membership, document permission and access to that exact shipment.

04

Issue a secure link

Generate a short-lived signed link after server-side checks, then record the download outcome.

Document access standards

Protect sensitive delivery information while keeping the evidence trail clear and useful to the customer.

Download eligibility

A customer download is permitted only when all required conditions are satisfied.

  • Verified POD linked to the correct shipment and delivery event.
  • Active user membership in the shipment's organization.
  • Role-based permission for the requested document.
  • Evidence is not revoked, superseded or restricted.

Private file handling

Delivery documents must remain private throughout storage, retrieval and expiry.

  • Private object storage; no public bucket paths.
  • Short-lived signed URLs issued after server authorization.
  • Re-check permission on each new download request.
  • Log document, user, organization, time and result.

When a document is unavailable

Do not substitute an unverified file or imply that evidence exists when it has not been approved.

  • Show pending, rejected, missing or access-restricted states distinctly.
  • Direct evidence questions to the shipment's authorized operating partner.
  • Provide a secure customer support route for access issues.
  • Notify authorized contacts when verified evidence becomes available.

Privacy and auditability

Proof of delivery may contain personal information and must be handled according to applicable law and contract terms.

  • Collect and disclose only the minimum necessary details.
  • Apply jurisdiction-aware retention and deletion rules.
  • Preserve original evidence metadata and review history.
  • Use UTC event timestamps and display in the saved IANA timezone.

Delivery status and proof of delivery are different records

A shipment marked delivered does not automatically mean that its POD has been verified or released. Customer access depends on the separate evidence review, document authorization and current access policy. Essential delivery and security notifications must remain reliable and separate from optional marketing messages.
