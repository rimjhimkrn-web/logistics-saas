How a claim moves

Clear stages, recorded hand-offs and accountable decisions.

01
Submit the caseIdentify the shipment, claim category, facts and requested resolution. A claim reference confirms receipt only.
02
Validate shipment and responsibilityConfirm customer authority, shipment eligibility, transport leg and responsible contracted provider.
03
Review evidenceKeep supporting files private, record verification status and request missing evidence where needed.
04
Record decision and closeShow the authorized decision, rationale and any separately confirmed financial outcome.

Responsibility & safeguards

Claims are handled with clear contractual boundaries.

Important: A claimed amount is a request, not an approved debt or guaranteed compensation. Liability and compensation are determined by the responsible contracting provider and applicable insurance terms. KRIM does not act as insurer or promise freight compensation.
Evidence and audit controls
  • Private evidence storage with access scoped to the authorized organization and case.
  • File type, size and ownership validated server-side.
  • Reviewer identity, decision note and timestamps recorded in the case history.
  • UTC event storage with locale-aware display.
Global operating standards
  • ISO 4217 currencies and correct currency precision.
  • Country-aware address, legal and regulatory fields.
  • Saved IANA timezone and locale-aware dates and numbers.
  • No assumption of partner coverage, insurance or compensation eligibility.
