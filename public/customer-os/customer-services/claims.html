Prepare before submitting

Information that helps

Providing complete, consistent details can reduce avoidable follow-up.

  • ✓Shipment reference
    Use the reference shown in your booking or shipment details.
  • ✓Issue description
    Explain what happened, when it was noticed and which items or packages were affected.
  • ✓Supporting evidence
    Include clear photographs, delivery records, inspection reports or relevant correspondence.
  • ✓Value and currency
    Where relevant, state the amount and currency with supporting invoices or valuation documents.
  • ✓Contact person
    Provide an authorized contact who can respond to assessment questions.
Keep original records. Retain packaging, labels, delivery documents and original photographs until the review is complete, where practical.
What to expect

Case review milestones

Milestones may vary with the service, route, evidence and parties involved.

ReceivedYour concern and shipment reference are recorded for review.
Information checkShipment details and submitted evidence are checked for completeness.
AssessmentRelevant service parties may be asked to provide records or clarification.
Outcome sharedThe decision, explanation and any available next steps are communicated.
Important: Submitting a claim or stating an amount does not establish liability or guarantee compensation. Any remedy depends on the applicable contract, evidence, law and responsible service provider.
Helpful guidance

Good to know

Report promptly

Notify the relevant service contact as soon as you identify an issue. Contractual and statutory notice periods may apply.

Protect your evidence

Keep original files and documents. Share only information relevant to the shipment and claim.

Separate service and payment decisions

A case assessment, a liability decision and any payment or refund are distinct steps and may involve different parties.
