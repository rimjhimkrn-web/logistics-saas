This staging page contains no customer claims or evidence. A claims workflow must verify shipment ownership, organization membership, eligibility, currency and the exact shipment leg before any liability is attributed.

Workflow controls

Global KRIM standard · 54

Claim eligibility

  • Claimant must belong to the customer organization that owns the shipment.
  • The shipment must be in an eligible state and the claim type and description must be validated.
  • Any amount must use an explicit ISO 4217 currency and correct precision.
  • Claims must identify the exact transport leg and the responsible assignment or partner.

Evidence and review

  • Evidence stays in private storage and is initially pending review.
  • The inspected upload controls allow PDF, JPEG or PNG up to 10 MB; any production upload must revalidate type, size and ownership server-side.
  • Independent review requires an authorized reviewer, a meaningful decision note and an auditable event.
  • Approval is not a payment or refund; financial execution is a separate confirmed workflow.

Important release gate

  • A reviewed multi-leg claim attribution weakness exists: the current submission logic may select the latest shipment assignment instead of an exact leg-specific assignment.
  • Do not rely on multi-leg partner-liability claims until an explicit leg/assignment selector and server-side validation are fixed and tested.
  • If no responsible partner assignment exists, route the issue as a KRIM service complaint rather than inventing partner liability.
Global readiness rule. Store event timestamps in UTC and display them in the saved IANA timezone. Use ISO 4217 transaction currencies, canonical stored measurement units, country-specific address and legal fields, locale-aware dates/numbers and RTL only where supported. Never infer live market, corridor, partner, rate, capacity, compliance or payment eligibility from a catalogue or display preference. Essential service and security notifications remain mandatory, with delivery status and retry handling; marketing preferences are separate.
