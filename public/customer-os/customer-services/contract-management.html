How to read a contract lifecycle

01 · DraftBeing prepared; not binding merely because it exists.
02 · IssuedSent to a counterparty; issue is not acceptance.
03 · ViewedAccessed by a party; viewing is not signing.
04 · Part acceptedOne party has accepted; counterparty step remains.
05 · Fully executedRequired parties and evidence are recorded.
06 · EffectiveExecuted terms are in force for the defined scope.
07 · AmendedChange is versioned; prior terms remain auditable.
08 · ClosedExpired, terminated or superseded as recorded.
09 · DisputedSubject to the applicable review and dispute process.

Contract integrity checks

  • Correct parties: exact legal names, authority and contracting roles.
  • Defined scope: service, territory, cargo, route and exclusions.
  • Commercial clarity: currency, rates, taxes, accessorials and payment terms.
  • Evidence: document version, hash, timestamps and acceptance record.
  • Legal alignment: liability, insurance, claims, cancellation and dispute terms.
  • Global readiness: jurisdiction-specific rules; no universal legal assumption.
