Agreement status matters. A draft, quotation, proposal or viewed document is not proof that all parties have accepted a binding agreement.

Before you agree

Check these essentials with the contracting provider.

01
Confirm the parties

Check exact legal names, roles and signing authority.

02
Define the service

Specify mode, route, cargo, locations, exclusions and responsibilities.

03
Review the price basis

Confirm currency, rate validity, taxes, surcharges and payment terms.

04
Keep the signed record

Retain the accepted version, effective dates and supporting evidence.

From draft to effective agreement

Each step should be supported by the right document and acceptance evidence.

01Draft

Terms are being prepared. No assumption of acceptance.

02Issued

A version is shared with the other contracting party.

03Under review

Parties assess scope, price, responsibilities and legal terms.

04Accepted

Required authorized parties complete the agreed acceptance process.

05Effective

Agreed terms apply from the specified date and scope.

06Amended

Changes are documented with a new version and approval trail.

07Renewed

Extension or renewal is agreed before the relevant deadline.

08Closed

Expired, terminated or replaced according to its terms.

Terms worth reviewing

Make sure the written agreement answers the questions that matter to your shipment.

Service & coverageTransport mode, origin, destination, transit points, cargo type and service exclusions.
Rates & chargesRate basis, currency, validity, fuel or other surcharges, duties and applicable taxes.
ResponsibilitiesBooking, packaging, documentation, customs coordination and delivery obligations.
Liability & insuranceApplicable limits, exclusions, claims process and insurance responsibilities.
Payment & billingInvoice party, payment due date, credit terms and dispute handling.
Change & terminationAmendments, cancellation, renewal, suspension and termination conditions.
