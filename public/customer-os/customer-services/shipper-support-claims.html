Choose the right route

Start with the type of issue. The responsible provider, shipment terms and applicable law determine eligibility and outcomes.

◎

Shipment support

For shipment progress questions, missed milestones, booking queries, document corrections or coordination issues.

  • Include the shipment reference and the milestone or event concerned.
  • Describe the impact and the action you need.
  • Keep replies and supporting records in the same case thread.
◇

Loss or damage claim

For reported cargo loss, shortage, visible damage or other contractually covered incidents.

  • Identify affected goods, packages and quantities.
  • Record when and where the issue was discovered.
  • Preserve packaging, delivery remarks and inspection evidence.
⇄

Dispute or service concern

For disagreements about a recorded service event, charge, document or case decision.

  • State the specific point in dispute and why.
  • Reference the relevant invoice, quote, event or case.
  • Request a review with the supporting facts.
↗

Returns and recovery

For return movements, refused deliveries or recovery arrangements that require provider coordination.

  • Confirm the shipment and affected consignment.
  • Describe the requested return or recovery outcome.
  • Wait for provider feasibility, cost and authorization confirmation.

How a case progresses

Operational decisions are recorded by authorized teams; submitting a request does not itself confirm liability, a refund or a service commitment.

01

Submit the concern

Choose the organization, add the relevant shipment when applicable and explain the issue clearly.

02

Attach evidence

Provide the documents and details requested for the issue and shipment type.

03

Review and coordinate

The authorized team checks the record and coordinates with the responsible logistics provider.

04

Follow the recorded outcome

Review case updates, requests for information and the decision or next action recorded.

Prepare before submitting

Providing complete, accurate information helps the responsible team assess the concern.

Useful supporting information

  • Shipment reference, booking confirmation or transport document number.
  • Goods description, package count and affected quantity.
  • Clear photographs of goods, packaging, seals or delivery condition where relevant.
  • Delivery receipt, inspection note, exception record or written remarks.
  • Dates, locations and a concise sequence of events.
  • Invoice or value evidence when requested under the applicable claim process.

Keep your information secure

Upload evidence only through the authenticated workspace or another verified channel provided for your case.

Do not include passwords, full payment-card details or unrelated sensitive personal information in case descriptions or attachments.

Retain original documents and follow any preservation instructions from the responsible provider.

Responsibility and payment clarity. Logistics services are supplied by the relevant provider under the applicable service contract. Claim acceptance, liability, compensation, freight refunds and settlement are determined by that provider and the governing terms. KRIM provides the platform and coordination workflow; a case submission is not a guarantee of approval or payment.

Common questions

Practical guidance for shipper support and claims.

Does opening a support case automatically create a cargo claim?

No. A support case records a concern. A formal claim may require a separate claim submission, specific evidence, notice within contractual time limits and review by the responsible provider.

Who decides whether a claim is accepted?

The responsible logistics provider or other party identified by the applicable contract and law reviews liability, coverage, evidence and any remedy. KRIM does not predetermine that decision.

Can I edit a claim decision or settlement amount?

Claim decisions and financial remedies are controlled by authorized reviewers. If you disagree, submit a dispute or request a review with the relevant supporting information.

How will I know when the case changes?

Important service and security updates should be delivered through the registered service-notification channels, with delivery status and retry handling. Keep your account contact details current.

What if my shipment involves multiple transport legs?

Provide the shipment reference and the event location and time. Responsibility must be assessed against the relevant verified transport leg and assignment; it should not be attributed to a provider when that link is unclear.
