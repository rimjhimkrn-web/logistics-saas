Before you make a payment

Use this short verification flow before following any payment instruction or external payment link.

01

Identify the invoice issuer

Check the legal name and details of the provider that issued the invoice. A logistics partner's freight invoice is not a KRIM invoice.

02

Match the agreed service

Confirm shipment or service reference, scope, charges, currency, tax details and due date against your accepted agreement.

03

Verify the payee and instructions

Use payment instructions supplied through the provider's verified channel. Contact the issuer independently if bank details or beneficiary information have changed.

04

Retain proof and confirm status

Keep the provider's receipt or transaction reference. A transfer receipt alone does not establish that the issuer has reconciled the payment.

Payment safety check

Pause and verify before transferring funds.

  • ✓Beneficiary name matches the invoice issuer or its documented payment agent.
  • ✓Amount and currency match the agreed invoice.
  • ✓Payment destination came from an authenticated or independently verified source.
  • ✓No one is asking for passwords, OTPs or full card details by message or phone.

Who receives your payment?

Customers contract with and pay the logistics partner directly for freight and transport services. KRIM provides the technology and coordination platform; any separately agreed KRIM platform or coordination fee is distinct, must be clearly identified on a KRIM-issued invoice, and is payable only to the named KRIM beneficiary. KRIM does not collect or hold partner freight funds through this information page.

Keep each record clear

Different issuers, different obligations. Check the document before paying.

Partner invoice

Freight & logistics charges

Pay the named logistics provider under your direct service agreement and its verified payment instructions.

KRIM invoice

Agreed platform fees

Any separately agreed KRIM fee is invoiced independently and must identify KRIM as the issuer and payee.

Payment record

Evidence & reconciliation

Use the issuer's confirmation and authorized payment history to check whether a payment has been reconciled.

Need help with a payment?

For an invoice discrepancy, contact the issuer shown on the document. For a KRIM platform-fee or workspace issue, sign in to your customer workspace and use the support channel. Essential payment and service updates are separate from optional marketing communications.
