Dispute Management

Understand how a shipment, service or invoice disagreement should be documented, reviewed impartially and resolved with an auditable decision.

Workflow controls

Global KRIM standard · 55

Case lifecycle

  • Record the disputed shipment, invoice, quote or service event and the parties involved.
  • Separate customer-visible messages from restricted investigation notes.
  • Preserve evidence, response deadlines, decision rationale and case history.
  • Provide an appeal route and independent review where required.

Fair review controls

  • The reviewer must not be a conflicted party or the original decision-maker where independence is required.
  • Keep each organization's records and evidence tenant-scoped.
  • A dispute outcome does not automatically create a refund, credit note, claim approval or shipment change.
  • Refund approval and provider-confirmed execution must remain separate states.

Current readiness

  • No live disputes, investigations, decisions or appeals were evidenced in the latest read-only audit.
  • The existing finance refund/dispute interface is not a complete general cargo and service dispute system.
  • Do not advertise guaranteed resolution times or completed dispute automation until end-to-end case ownership, notification and appeal tests pass.
Global readiness rule. Store event timestamps in UTC and display them in the saved IANA timezone. Use ISO 4217 transaction currencies, canonical stored measurement units, country-specific address and legal fields, locale-aware dates/numbers and RTL only where supported. Never infer live market, corridor, partner, rate, capacity, compliance or payment eligibility from a catalogue or display preference. Essential service and security notifications remain mandatory, with delivery status and retry handling; marketing preferences are separate.
