Customer verification

What to expect during verification.

Requirements may vary by organization type, jurisdiction, service requested and applicable compliance checks. Follow the requirements presented inside your authenticated KRIM workflow.

01
Confirm organization details

Check that your legal or registered business name, business type, registered address and primary contact details are accurate.

02
Prepare supporting information

Have relevant registration, tax or identity details available where requested. Only provide documents specifically required for your account.

03
Submit through secure sign-in

Use the authenticated customer workflow for any requested information or evidence. Do not send sensitive identity or business documents through public forms.

04
Allow the review process

Authorized review may require clarification or additional evidence. A submission is not the same as approval.

05
Check your account status

Use the secure workspace and official account notifications to see the recorded outcome or any next action.

06
Keep records current

Update organization information when material details change and follow any re-verification request shown in your account.

i
Important: this page does not verify your organization.

No verification status, approval, compliance clearance or service eligibility is asserted here. Only a confirmed result recorded by KRIM’s authorized workflow can establish your account’s verification state.

Protect your information

Keep sensitive documents in the right place.

Use secure, authenticated KRIM account workflows and take care when sharing business or personal information.

A

Use verified KRIM sign-in

Access protected account functions only after signing in through the official KRIM website. Never share passwords or one-time verification codes.

B

Share only what is requested

Provide only the documents and details requested for the relevant verification purpose. Avoid sending sensitive records through ordinary email or public contact forms.

C

Watch for official updates

Essential account and security notifications should remain available through configured service channels. Optional marketing preferences are separate.

D

Ask for help when needed

If a requirement is unclear, contact KRIM through the official contact page without attaching sensitive identity documents to an open enquiry.
