Submitting a case does not itself determine liability or guarantee an outcome.

Choose the right pathway

Route the issue to the workflow designed for it.

Invoice or charge dispute

Question a billed amount, line item, tax detail or invoice reference. Identify the legal entity that issued the invoice.

Open invoice register →

Transport service or cargo claim

Report loss, damage, delay or a service concern linked to a shipment. Partner claims may require evidence and partner review.

Open claims workspace →

KRIM platform issue

Report an access, workflow, record display or coordination issue involving KRIM's technology service.

Open customer support →

Payment or adjustment query

Ask about a recorded payment or authorized invoice adjustment. Partner-issued credit notes must be handled by their issuer.

Review credit notes →
