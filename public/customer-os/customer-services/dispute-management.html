What can be disputed?

Identify the issue category before preparing a case.

⌁

Shipment service

Missed or disputed milestones, service-level concerns, route or handling exceptions and delivery discrepancies.

₹

Charges & invoices

Differences in quoted versus invoiced amounts, duplicate line items, accessorial charges or invoice references.

▣

Cargo condition

Reported shortage, damage, packaging concerns or a mismatch between expected and received cargo condition.

A transparent review process

Each stage should retain its owner, timestamp and supporting record.

01 · SUBMITDescribe the concernLink the shipment, invoice or service reference and explain the specific issue.
02 · EVIDENCEAttach relevant proofProvide documents, photographs, correspondence or milestone details where relevant.
03 · REVIEWAssess both recordsThe responsible service provider or authorized case team reviews the facts and responds.
04 · RESOLVERecord the outcomeDocument the decision, agreed action, any adjustment and closure or escalation status.

Prepare a useful case

Good references and clear evidence help avoid unnecessary back-and-forth.

Helpful information

  • ✓Shipment ID, booking reference or invoice number.
  • ✓Event date, location and a concise description of what happened.
  • ✓Relevant photographs, signed documents, receipts or email records.
  • ✓The resolution you are requesting and the reason for it.

Before you submit

  • ✓Check whether the issue is already covered by an open claim or support case.
  • ✓Keep original documents and avoid altering evidence files.
  • ✓Share only information necessary to assess the dispute.
  • ✓Review the applicable contract, service terms and time limits.
