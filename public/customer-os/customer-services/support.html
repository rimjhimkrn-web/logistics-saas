Customer support questions

Practical guidance before you submit a request.

How do I contact KRIM about a particular shipment?

Sign in and open the Support Workspace. Select your active customer organization, link the relevant shipment if it is available in your account, and describe the issue with dates or milestones where useful.

Can I see another organization's support cases?

No. Support case visibility is restricted by the signed-in account, active organization membership and database authorization policies. Do not share account credentials to obtain access.

How do I report cargo loss or damage?

Use the Claims Register rather than a general inquiry where you need to submit a formal claim. Follow the evidence and notice requirements in the applicable customer and logistics-partner agreements.

Who issues freight invoices and handles freight refunds?

The logistics partner providing the freight service issues its freight invoice and handles freight charges or refunds under its agreement with the customer. KRIM's separately agreed platform or coordination fees are distinct from partner freight charges.

Will I receive updates about my support case?

Essential service updates, including case creation, customer-visible replies, escalation and resolution, are operational notifications and are separate from optional marketing messages. The case workspace displays the latest status recorded in the system.

Does KRIM guarantee a response time or 24/7 support?

Do not assume a response-time commitment or continuous staffed coverage unless it is expressly confirmed in your applicable service agreement. The support workspace records cases and their current system status; it does not itself create a contractual service-level guarantee.

Need to raise a new request?

Use the secure customer workspace to submit a case against your active organization and track the saved case reference.
