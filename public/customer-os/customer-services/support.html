How a support case works

Clear ownership and recorded updates at each step.

1Sign in and identify your organization

Private case records are available only after authentication and organization membership checks.

2Provide the issue and context

Choose a category, describe what happened and link a shipment when it is relevant.

3Follow the recorded case status

Use your support workspace to review the case record and its customer-visible updates.

Important: A case submission confirms that a request was recorded; it does not mean the issue is resolved or that a response-time commitment has been made. Essential service and security updates are separate from optional marketing communications. Do not include passwords, full payment-card details or other secrets in a support message.
