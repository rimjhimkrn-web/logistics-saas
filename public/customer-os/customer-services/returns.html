Operational checkpoints

Information required to coordinate a reliable reverse movement.

Original referenceKeep the return linked to the source shipment and relevant cargo lines.
Goods & conditionRecord item count, packaging, condition, weight and special handling.
Route & documentsValidate addresses, market rules, customs and required paperwork.
Partner confirmationConfirm availability, price, service scope and collection arrangements.
Important distinction: a reverse-shipment request does not itself approve a merchandise return, cancel the original freight service, issue a refund or create a credit note. The responsible seller and logistics provider handle their respective commercial obligations. Under KRIM's current partner-led model, freight service and related freight refunds are contracted with and paid directly to the logistics partner. KRIM does not guarantee capacity or rates before partner confirmation.
Service updates: important return events—request received, partner response, collection changes, delivery and exceptions—should generate essential customer notifications with delivery tracking and retry handling. Marketing preferences remain separate from operational notifications.
