Operational reporting

  • Shipment volume and milestones only from verified shipment events.
  • Transit and delivery metrics must define their start/end events and timezone rules.
  • Partner performance and SLA measures require accepted assignments and valid service contracts.
  • Do not compare corridors or partners without consistent measurement definitions.

Financial reporting

  • Use the transaction's ISO 4217 currency; never reinterpret historical amounts using display currency.
  • Keep KRIM service fees separate from partner freight charges and provider-direct payments.
  • Show tax jurisdiction, accounting period and source document for financial measures.
  • Exports must be authorized, audited and scoped to the active organization.

Data trust

  • Display report owner, definition, data source, refresh timestamp and coverage limitations.
  • Store event timestamps in UTC and render in the saved IANA timezone.
  • A missing or stale source must be shown as unavailable, not as zero performance.
  • Available reports and exports depend on your organization permissions.
Global readiness rule. Store event timestamps in UTC and display them in the saved IANA timezone. Use ISO 4217 transaction currencies, canonical stored measurement units, country-specific address and legal fields, locale-aware dates/numbers and RTL only where supported. Never infer live market, corridor, partner, rate, capacity, compliance or payment eligibility from a catalogue or display preference. Essential service and security notifications remain mandatory, with delivery status and retry handling; marketing preferences are separate.
