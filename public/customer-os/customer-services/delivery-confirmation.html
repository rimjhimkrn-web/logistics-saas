What counts as confirmation?

Operational safeguards for delivery records

1 · Shipment identity

The reference must match a shipment inside the signed-in customer organization.

2 · Recorded delivery event

A status or actual-delivery timestamp must come from an authorized operational record, not an ETA.

3 · Proof of delivery

ePOD, recipient acknowledgement and supporting evidence must be independently verified before being described as verified proof.

This page currently provides a read-only review. It does not capture a customer signature, approve a delivery, create ePOD evidence or change shipment status. Those actions require a verified backend workflow and audit trail.
