Bid lifecycle

Understand each response state.

These definitions explain the lifecycle. Your live bid records and timestamps are available only inside your authenticated organization workspace.

01Received

Request submitted

Your shipment or quote request has entered the partner response process. No offer is implied.

02Reviewing

Partner assessment

A partner may review cargo details, route, dates, capacity and service requirements.

03Offer

Bid submitted

An offer is available for review with its scope, currency, charge basis and validity.

04Decision

Customer review

Compare the offer, request clarification, or accept/reject the valid quote version.

05Closed

Response concluded

A bid may be accepted, declined, withdrawn, superseded or expired. Booking is a separate confirmation.

Decision safeguards

Keep the decision tied to the exact offer you reviewed.

  • Check the quote version and validity before accepting.
  • Confirm included services, exclusions and applicable charges.
  • Ask for clarification if cargo or route assumptions differ.
  • Do not treat a bid as dispatch, capacity reservation or booking.
  • Keep a record of the authorized customer decision.
