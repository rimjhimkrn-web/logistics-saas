Finance · Transaction record guide

A clear history of payments, without blurred boundaries.

Understand which transactions belong in KRIM's historical payment records, how their status is confirmed and where to find the right evidence. Partner-direct freight payments remain with the logistics provider you contracted.

How a payment record is established

A payment record should reflect a verified transaction event, not an assumption based on a checkout screen or transfer screenshot.

01

Identify the issuer

Confirm whether the invoice comes from KRIM or from the logistics partner supplying the service.

02

Verify the transaction

Use the authorized payment provider or issuer confirmation and a traceable transaction reference.

03

Reconcile the status

Keep pending, completed, failed and reversed outcomes distinct, with the source event retained.

04

Review the record

Access the saved account record through the secure customer workspace and check its timestamp.

Know what you are looking at

KRIM's historical payment record is not a consolidated statement of every payment made for a shipment.

What may appear in KRIM history

Only historical transactions that KRIM itself received and recorded under a separately documented KRIM transaction, subject to account authorization.

  • Traceable KRIM transaction reference.
  • Recorded amount and currency only where the source data stores them.
  • Server-confirmed status and event timestamps.
  • Corrections or reversals shown as distinct recorded events.

What belongs with your logistics partner

Under the partner-direct model, customers contract with and pay the logistics provider directly for freight and transport services.

  • Partner-issued freight invoices and receipts.
  • Partner bank transfers and external payment confirmations.
  • Provider-side refunds, adjustments and reconciliation.
  • Questions about the partner's beneficiary or payment terms.

When a record looks wrong

Use the reference and issuer shown on the original document to identify the right support route.

  • Check the invoice issuer before raising a query.
  • Do not retry a payment only because a page has not refreshed.
  • Never share an OTP, password, PIN or full card details.
  • Keep the provider's receipt for partner-direct payments.

Reliable record standards

Payment information should be traceable, scoped to the correct account and clear about what it can prove.

  • Organization- and role-authorized access.
  • Server-confirmed status, not browser-only status.
  • Explicit currency code when stored; no inferred currency.
  • UTC event source with display in the saved IANA timezone.

Keep service updates visible

Essential payment, security and case updates are operational notifications, not marketing. They should be delivered with event tracking and retry handling; optional promotional preferences must not suppress essential account notices.
