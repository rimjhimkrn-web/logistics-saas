Authorization safeguards

Principles required before an approval can be treated as operational.

Separation of duties

A request creator cannot approve their own request where independent review is required.

Organization scope

Membership, role and object-level access must be checked by the backend on every decision.

Evidence and reasons

Material decisions require a reason, supporting evidence and an immutable audit event.

Concurrency protection

Concurrent or duplicate decisions must be safely rejected or idempotently resolved.

Delegation and expiry

Delegated authority, limits, effective dates and escalation rules must be explicit.

Downstream verification

Never treat a click or UI message as approval until the server confirms the resulting state.
