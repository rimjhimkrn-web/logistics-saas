Partner-direct payment model

Customers pay the contracting logistics provider directly for freight and transport services. Those external partner payments do not appear in KRIM's historical payment table. Any separately agreed KRIM platform or coordination fee is distinct and should be supported by a KRIM-issued invoice. The legacy payment table does not store a currency code, so this page never guesses or labels a currency.
