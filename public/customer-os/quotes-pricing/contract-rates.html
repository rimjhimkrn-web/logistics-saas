Contract Rates

Understand how negotiated rate agreements are governed, validated and applied to eligible shipments—without assuming a contract or lane is active.

← Return to workspaceRequest a quote
Contract applicability: A negotiated rate is shown as applicable only when the relevant agreement, customer entity, route, service scope, validity period and operating partner have been verified. If no eligible contract is recorded for a request, use the quote workflow instead.

A contract rate is not a general price list. It is an agreed commercial term that applies only to the parties, services, lanes and dates stated in the approved agreement.

Contract scope

  • Contracting customer and authorized legal entities
  • Origin, destination, corridor and any geographic exclusions
  • Transport mode, service level, cargo class and handling requirements
  • Rate basis, minimum charges, accessorials and currency
  • Effective date, expiry, renewal and notice provisions

Before applying a rate

  • Verify the signed agreement and authorized version.
  • Confirm shipment attributes fall within the contracted scope.
  • Check partner capability, capacity, market and compliance eligibility.
  • Apply taxes and charges according to the applicable jurisdiction and agreement.
  • Retain a traceable calculation and approval record.

Customer controls

  • Show the contract reference and applicable version.
  • Explain exclusions and additional charges before commitment.
  • Keep display-currency conversion separate from the transaction currency.
  • Escalate out-of-scope shipments to a fresh quote workflow.

When a contract rate can be applied

01

Agreement verified

Confirm the executed agreement, authorized parties, current version and approval authority.

02

Shipment in scope

Match the origin, destination, mode, cargo, service level and any stated exclusions.

03

Validity & charges checked

Check effective dates, expiry, minimums, accessorials, taxes and transaction currency.

04

Partner eligibility confirmed

Confirm that the relevant provider can actually perform the service for the requested dates and conditions.

What a transparent contract-rate record should show

Agreement identityReference, version, contracting entities and approval status.
Commercial scopeLane, mode, cargo/service scope, rate basis and minimum charge.
ValidityEffective date, expiry, renewal terms and applicable time zone.
Charge transparencyIncluded items, surcharges, exclusions, tax treatment and currency.
Operational conditionsPartner, capacity, compliance and any service-specific restrictions.
Audit trailApproved version, change history and the source of each applied rate.

This checklist describes the information needed to assess a contract rate. It is not a statement that a contract, rate or partner is currently active for your organization.
