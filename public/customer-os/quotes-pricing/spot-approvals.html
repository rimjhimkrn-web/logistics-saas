Spot Approvals

A controlled review framework for one-off freight offers. Confirm the commercial terms, shipment fit and internal authority before anyone commits to a spot quote.

Review quotesSpot pricing guidance
Approval is not booking. An internal approval records an organization's authorization to proceed. It does not accept a partner's offer, reserve capacity, confirm a shipment or change the quote status unless the authorized backend workflow explicitly completes those actions.

Use this framework to review a specific quote version. Do not approve from an email excerpt or an outdated screenshot; return to the current quote record and verify its status and validity.

Commercial review

Confirm the full landed cost and the party responsible for each charge.

  • Partner quote reference, version and issue date
  • Currency, base freight, surcharges and accessorial charges
  • Tax basis, exclusions and payment terms
  • Validity deadline and any expiry or re-quote condition
  • Separate partner freight charges from any KRIM fee

Operational review

Check that the offer actually matches the shipment being considered.

  • Origin, destination, mode and service scope
  • Cargo description, dimensions, weight and handling needs
  • Pickup/delivery windows and transit assumptions
  • Partner capability, compliance and current eligibility
  • Capacity confirmation and exclusions from the partner

Authority and separation

Approval rules belong to the customer's organization and its delegation policy.

  • Identify the authorized approver for the value and risk tier
  • Require a second approver where policy or threshold demands it
  • Separate requestor, reviewer and final approver where required
  • Record any conflict of interest and escalation
  • Do not treat a role label in the browser as proof of authority

Evidence and audit trail

A defensible decision is traceable to the exact quote and supporting evidence.

  • Quote ID and immutable version reference
  • Comparison rationale and alternatives considered
  • Approver identity, role and decision timestamp
  • Decision, conditions, comments and attachments
  • Notifications to requestor and relevant operations users

Recommended approval sequence

01 · IDENTIFYOpen current quoteConfirm shipment, customer organization, partner and current quote version.
02 · VALIDATECheck terms and fitReview charges, exclusions, validity, capability and service requirements.
03 · AUTHORIZEApply approval policyRoute to the correct delegated approver and capture conditions or rejection reason.
04 · PROCEEDSeparate next actionsOnly then follow the dedicated quote acceptance and booking process.

Decision guide

Review outcomeWhen to useRequired follow-up
Approve internallyCurrent version is complete, commercially acceptable and within delegated authority.Record approver, conditions and exact quote version; proceed to separate acceptance step.
Request clarificationCharges, scope, capacity, validity or exclusions are missing or ambiguous.Ask the issuing partner for a revised or clarified offer; re-review the new version.
EscalateValue, risk, exception or conflict exceeds the reviewer's authority.Route to the designated senior or specialist approver; preserve the audit trail.
DeclineTerms are unsuitable, requirements are unmet or policy does not permit proceeding.Record the reason and notify relevant stakeholders; do not imply partner booking.
