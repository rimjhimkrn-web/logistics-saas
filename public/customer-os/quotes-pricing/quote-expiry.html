Quote Expiry

Understand quote validity, expiry and the safeguards that prevent an outdated offer from being accepted or used for a booking.

← Return to workspaceReview quote details
Quote validity is checked against the authoritative quote record. Sign in to your KRIM customer workspace to review your organization’s quote status and validity.

Expiry is determined from the authoritative server timestamp and the quote's stored validity—not the device clock.

Expiry essentials

  • Store validity deadlines as UTC timestamps.
  • Display deadlines in the user's saved IANA timezone, with timezone context when ambiguity is possible.
  • Show the quote version and the validity window together.
  • A country or currency preference never extends quote validity.

When a quote expires

  • Block new acceptance and booking against the expired version.
  • Preserve the expired quote and its audit history; never delete it silently.
  • Offer a new quote request or partner revalidation where available.
  • Re-check current partner eligibility, capacity, corridor and pricing before issuing a replacement.

Notifications

  • Send advance reminders according to the configured operational policy.
  • Notify relevant contacts when expiry occurs or a quote is withdrawn.
  • Track in-app and email delivery outcomes and retry failures.
  • Essential quote lifecycle notifications cannot be silently disabled.

Global operating standard

KRIM connects customers with independently verified logistics partners. Catalogue presence, currency selection or a displayed country does not establish operational eligibility. Market, corridor, partner capability, capacity, approved pricing, compliance and fulfilment must each be verified before a service is offered.
