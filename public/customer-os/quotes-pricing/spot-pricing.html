Spot Pricing

Explore how a shipment-specific spot offer is requested and assessed. Spot prices are not guaranteed until an eligible partner confirms a valid offer.

Review quote requestView quote listCompare offers
Spot pricing is shipment-specific and subject to partner confirmation. This page explains the process; it does not request a price, reserve capacity or create a booking.

No sample amount or illustrative lane on this page represents a live KRIM rate or available service.

What affects a spot offer

  • Origin, destination and actual route feasibility
  • Cargo dimensions, gross weight, commodity and handling
  • Transport mode, service level and requested schedule
  • Capacity, fuel or market surcharges and accessorials
  • Customs, permits, insurance and destination requirements

Offer validation

  • Confirm the operating partner and capability for the requested service.
  • Validate the corridor and capacity for the requested dates.
  • Show the complete charge basis, currency code, exclusions and quote validity.
  • Revalidate material changes before acceptance.
  • Do not present a catalogue entry as operational availability.

After submission

  • The request remains pending until a partner response is verified.
  • A returned offer is subject to customer review and any required approvals.
  • Acceptance must reference the exact valid quote version.
  • Operational booking confirmation is a separate, verified milestone.

Understand the complete offer

Before comparing spot offers, check the issuing legal partner, route and mode, cargo assumptions, service inclusions, transit estimate and its basis, line-item charges, surcharges, taxes, exclusions, payment terms, quote currency and validity deadline. Confirm whether customs, insurance, storage, handling, waiting time, last-mile delivery and fuel adjustments are included or separately payable. Compare equivalent scope—not only the headline total.

What happens when details change

A change to cargo dimensions, weight, commodity, pickup or delivery address, schedule, handling needs or route can invalidate the original assumptions. Ask for a newly issued quote version and review its differences. Do not rely on a verbal estimate or assume the previous price remains valid after a material change.

Customer decision and partner responsibility

The logistics partner issues and fulfils the freight service offer. KRIM facilitates the connection and workflow; it does not guarantee capacity, transit, customs clearance or partner pricing. Customer acceptance applies only to the exact valid quote version and does not by itself prove that operational booking or pickup has been confirmed. Essential status notifications should be delivered to authorized contacts, with delivery outcomes recorded.

Global operating standard

KRIM connects customers with independently verified logistics partners. Catalogue presence, currency selection or a displayed country does not establish operational eligibility. Market, corridor, partner capability, capacity, approved pricing, compliance and fulfilment must each be verified before a service is offered.
