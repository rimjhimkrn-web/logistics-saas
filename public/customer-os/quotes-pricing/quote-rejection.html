Quote rejection

Understand the checks and consequences before a quote is declined. A rejection must be tied to the correct quote, shipment and authorized customer organization, with a recorded reason and an auditable server-side outcome.

Common decision considerations

Use a clear, factual reason that helps the partner and operations team understand the decision without exposing unnecessary confidential information.

Commercial terms

Price, surcharge, payment terms or contract conditions do not meet the approved requirement.

Service fit

Proposed mode, route, transit plan or service conditions do not match the shipment requirement.

Timing and validity

Quote validity, proposed schedule or required collection window is unsuitable or has elapsed.

Operational eligibility

Required capability, capacity, compliance evidence or partner confirmation is not established.

Alternative selected

An authorized decision-maker selected another eligible offer for this shipment.

Shipment changed or cancelled

The underlying shipment requirement changed, was withdrawn or is no longer proceeding.

Before a rejection is recorded

A controlled decision should pass each of these checks.

01
Verify quote and shipment ownership

Confirm the quote belongs to the current organization and the intended shipment.

02
Check authority and approval policy

Confirm the user has permission and any required internal approval has been completed.

03
Check current quote state

Reject only an eligible, non-expired quote. Handle already accepted, withdrawn or superseded records safely.

04
Record reason and audit event

Persist the reason, actor, timestamp and relevant quote version through a server-authorized workflow.

05
Notify relevant parties

Deliver an operational notification and retain delivery status. Do not silently suppress essential service updates.
