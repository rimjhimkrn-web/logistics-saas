Approval path

A control sequence for customer organizations. These steps describe required checks, not a claim that a live approval is pending or completed.

01

Identify the quote and shipment

Confirm the quote belongs to the correct customer organization and shipment. Verify the exact partner offer, amount, ISO 4217 currency and validity window.

Record verification
02

Check organization policy

Review your organization's active approval workflow, delegated authority, spending limits, contract rules and any required number of reviewers.

Policy owner / administrator
03

Obtain authorized internal decision

Only an authorized approver may record the internal decision through a configured, permission-checked workflow. A quote viewer or request creator must not self-approve where policy prohibits it.

Authorized approver only
04

Revalidate partner and quote eligibility

Before customer acceptance, KRIM must independently recheck quote validity, partner assignment acceptance, shipment ownership and server-side eligibility.

Server-side validation
05

Proceed to customer acceptance

After any required internal approval is verified, an authorized customer user can continue to the canonical acceptance page. Internal approval does not itself accept the quote or create a booking.

Separate customer action

Pre-approval checks

Use this checklist before requesting an internal decision.

✓
Correct legal entity and workspaceConfirm the customer organization and authorized workspace.
✓
Commercial amount and currencyCheck the original quote currency and precision; do not compare unlike currencies without an explicit FX source.
✓
Quote validity and conditionsReview expiry, exclusions, surcharge basis and service conditions recorded on the quote.
✓
Partner assignment responsePartner acceptance is a separate prerequisite and must be verified against the matching assignment.
✓
Audit and separation of dutiesEnsure the decision is attributable to an authorized reviewer and retained in the audit trail.
