Current operating model

Customers contract with and pay their selected logistics partner directly. The partner confirms freight price, invoice or receipt, payment beneficiary, service terms and any invoice correction or freight refund. KRIM does not aggregate partner invoices, collect or hold freight funds, operate escrow, or settle partner payables.

Historical payment-provider reconciliation and authorised internal accounting records remain separate from customer billing and partner payout services. Any future KRIM fee or enterprise billing feature requires signed terms, legal and tax review, a verified accounting design and tested controls before activation.
