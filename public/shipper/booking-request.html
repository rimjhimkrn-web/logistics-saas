Build your shipment profile

Start with the international route, select a transport preference, and describe your freight accurately.

* Required fields
01 / Global route planning
→
Search worldwide for cities, towns, airports, seaports, terminals and addresses. Select a suggested result for each endpoint. These results identify geography only; they do not confirm an active market, a freight lane, customs clearance or carrier coverage.
02 / Route type
Choose the route arrangement that best describes the request. KRIM records it as a preference; actual route legs and handoffs must be confirmed.
03 / Transport network preference
Your preference is saved on the shipment record. KRIM must still confirm geographic feasibility, customs or trade requirements, available partners, capacity and pricing before accepting a booking.
04 / Service expectation
This selection is passed to the quote workflow. It does not guarantee a mode, carrier, serviceability, transit time, or price; those require backend confirmation.
05 / Cargo profile
Describe the goods accurately. Restricted, dangerous or regulated cargo may require additional compliance review.
