Global payment safeguards

Server-enforced payment capability registry. Order creation is blocked unless the country, currency and provider are explicitly enabled in the backend registry. INR is the current checkout path; international currencies remain disabled until their capabilities are verified.

Architecture rule: keep quote currency, charge/presentment currency, provider settlement currency and partner payout currency as separate fields where the selected provider supports them. FX rates must be quoted with a timestamp and fee disclosure; refunds must reference the original charge; webhook and verification handling must be idempotent. The server-side capability registry is checked before Razorpay order creation. Planned currencies remain disabled until provider support, merchant eligibility, settlement/refund paths and end-to-end tests are verified.
