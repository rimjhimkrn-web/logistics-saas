Customer payment guidance · current launch model

Partner-direct payments only

Pay the logistics provider you contract with.

KRIM connects customers with logistics partners. Under the current launch model, the selected logistics provider contracts with you, performs the transport or logistics service, issues its own invoice and receives freight payment directly from you.

KRIM does not currently collect freight charges, create customer freight-payment orders or settle partner freight funds. This page does not accept payment details and does not launch a payment gateway. A KRIM checkout or partner payout must not be assumed to be available.

Before you pay a provider

  • Confirm the provider's legal name and the exact accepted quote, currency and service scope.
  • Request an invoice or payment instruction issued by the contracting provider.
  • Verify that the beneficiary name matches the provider's legal/business identity; independently confirm any bank-account change using a trusted contact channel.
  • Ask the provider for its applicable tax invoice and transport documentation.
  • Keep the provider's receipt and proof of payment with your shipment records.

KRIM fees are separate

If KRIM charges a separately agreed referral, marketing or coordination fee, it must be disclosed separately and supported by a separate KRIM invoice or receipt. Do not treat that fee as the provider's freight charge. Tax treatment and invoice requirements depend on the actual service and must not be assumed to be GST-exempt.

KRIM will not ask you to pay freight to a personal account, an unverified UPI ID or an unrelated third party.

Payment issue or suspected fraud?

Stop before transferring funds if the beneficiary, amount, quote or provider identity does not match. Use a secure support ticket for an existing case, or contact KRIM through the public enquiry channel. Do not submit card numbers, passwords, OTPs or banking credentials in a support ticket.

What this page does not do

  • It does not create a payment order or mark a shipment as paid.
  • It does not verify a provider's bank account or guarantee its performance.
  • It does not offer escrow, split settlement, partner payouts or automatic refunds.
  • It does not promise payment-method availability in any country or currency.
