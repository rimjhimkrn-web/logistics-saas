Partner payment guidance

Confirm the final service scope, contract, price and payment instructions directly with your selected logistics partner.

KRIM freight checkout is disabled.
Customers pay logistics partners directly under their agreement. KRIM does not create freight payment orders, collect or hold freight funds, or settle partner freight payments in this launch model.

Before you pay

  1. Verify the partner's legal identity and service capability.
  2. Confirm route availability, service scope, final amount and cancellation terms directly with the partner.
  3. Independently verify the beneficiary name and bank details with the partner.
  4. Obtain the partner's invoice or receipt and retain payment proof.

For freight refunds, contact the partner you paid. KRIM support can route platform complaints but cannot promise or issue refunds for partner-collected freight.
