Global route scope

Route preference is not service confirmation

KRIM can store domestic and cross-border route preferences across supported transport modes. Real serviceability still depends on active-market status, an eligible lane, verified partner capability and capacity, cargo acceptance, customs and sanctions controls, tariff coverage, and operating approvals. The form will not activate a country simply because it appears in geographic search.
