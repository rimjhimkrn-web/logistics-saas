Why partner with KRIM

A platform approach to logistics collaboration

KRIM aims to make partner discovery, coordination and operational information more structured—while keeping service delivery responsibilities clear.

01

Clear commercial boundaries

Under the current launch model, customers contract with and pay the logistics provider directly. KRIM does not hold, escrow, split or settle partner freight funds. Any separate KRIM fee requires a written arrangement and applicable legal and tax review.

02

Relevant opportunities

Potential opportunities may be shared only when a relevant customer requirement, approved service scope and confirmed operating capacity exist. Registration does not guarantee loads, routes or revenue.

03

Operational readiness

Depending on the proposed service, partners may need to provide current registration, permits, insurance, personnel, equipment and service-area evidence. Integrations require separate configuration and testing.

Service ecosystem

Capabilities across the logistics journey

Partners may express interest in one or more service domains. Actual scope is reviewed and confirmed individually; this catalogue is not a statement of current live coverage.

Transport

Road, rail, air and ocean transport services, subject to appropriate operating permissions and partner capability.

Warehousing & fulfilment

Storage, handling, inventory support, distribution and fulfilment services where available and verified.

Forwarding & customs

Freight forwarding, documentation coordination and customs-related services by appropriately authorised providers.

First & last mile

Pickup, local distribution, delivery and regional service support within confirmed operating areas.

Specialist handling

Temperature-sensitive, oversized, project, high-value or other specialist cargo services where permitted and evidenced.

Technology & support

Logistics technology, visibility, data exchange and operational support capabilities that meet agreed security requirements.
