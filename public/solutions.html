How a KRIM shipment moves

A clear process from first enquiry to delivery.

Every stage has a decision point. An enquiry is not a booking, and a quote is not confirmation until accepted and operationally validated.

Submit requirements

Share cargo, route, dates, handling and service details.

Validate the request

Review shipment information and identify route requirements.

Receive a valid offer

Confirm price, inclusions, exclusions, validity and terms.

Accept & confirm

Complete assignment, capacity and payment checks as applicable.

Execute & close

Coordinate progress, exceptions, delivery proof and records.

A network-led operating model

KRIM connects demand with capable logistics providers.

KRIM's role is to organise the digital workflow, commercial hand-offs and shipment context. Transport, storage, customs and other specialist services are delivered by independent providers whose capabilities must be reviewed before activation.

What happens before a provider is activated?

Business identity and contact details are collected.

Service areas, equipment and operating capabilities are reviewed.

Relevant licences, insurance and other documents are checked as applicable.

Commercial terms, capacity and escalation contacts are agreed.

Registration alone does not mean approval or an allocation of shipments.

Frequently asked questions

Know what to expect before you move.

Does submitting an enquiry confirm my shipment?

No. An enquiry starts the review process. A shipment is only confirmed after a valid offer is accepted and the required provider, capacity, payment and operational checks are completed.

Are rates available instantly for every route?

No. Pricing depends on a valid applicable rate or a confirmed offer from an eligible provider. If neither is available, the request may need further review or a partner quote.

Can KRIM handle international shipments?

KRIM is designed with global-ready architecture, but international service availability must be checked by origin, destination, mode, provider capability and local requirements. Do not assume every country or route is currently active.

Does shipment tracking always show live GPS?

No. Tracking displays the shipment information and milestones available from connected and recorded sources. Live GPS or predictive arrival times are shown only where a verified live data source supports them.

Can a logistics company register as a KRIM partner?

Yes. Providers can submit an application for review. Registration is not automatic approval, guaranteed work or a confirmed allocation of shipments.

What if my cargo needs special handling?

Describe the cargo and handling requirements before accepting an offer. Acceptance depends on legal restrictions, equipment, qualified provider capability and route-specific conditions.
