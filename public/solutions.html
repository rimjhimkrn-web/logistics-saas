Platform architecture

Designed for secure, accountable operations.

The platform is intended to support controlled access, traceable workflows and integrations. Security controls and operational guarantees must be verified in the deployed system before being treated as production assurances.

01 · Access control

Organization-aware data access

Role-based permissions and database row-level security can help isolate organization data when policies are correctly configured and tested.

02 · Identity

Authentication and account protection

Sign-in, account recovery and optional stronger verification should follow the security settings enabled for each account and role.

03 · Integrations

Server-side workflow processing

Payment verification, notifications and other privileged actions should be validated by trusted backend functions rather than frontend claims alone.

04 · Accountability

Operational events and audit trails

Shipment milestones and financial events should be recorded with appropriate permissions, timestamps and reconciliation controls.

Global readiness: country-specific services, currencies, regulations, carrier coverage and data-hosting requirements must be confirmed market by market. A global-ready architecture does not imply that every international lane is already live.
