Payment success is a server decision.

KRIM independently checks the authenticated payment order and its matching completed financial transaction. A browser callback, screenshot, provider popup, return URL or customer claim cannot establish payment success on its own. If a result is uncertain, stop and reconcile the original transaction before attempting another payment.
