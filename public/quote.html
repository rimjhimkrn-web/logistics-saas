A transparent process

From shipment details to a decision you can review.

Each stage should make the status and next action clear. Availability and timing depend on the route, cargo, service requirements and participating provider.

01

Describe the shipment

Enter origin and destination, cargo description, dimensions or weight, ready date, preferred mode and any handling requirements.

02

Review the available response

Where a valid approved rate or eligible partner quote is available, review its scope, price, assumptions and validity before deciding.

03

Confirm only when ready

Proceed only after the service, commercial terms, capacity and required payment or approval status are confirmed in the workflow.
