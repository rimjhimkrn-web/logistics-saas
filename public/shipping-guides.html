⚠️ IMDG & IATA Hazardous Material (DG) Class Matrix

Class 1: Explosives

Ammunition, fireworks, flares. Requires special vessel booking permits and direct port transfer.

Class 3: Flammable Liquids

Paints, fuels, alcohols. Flashpoint documentation required on all Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS).

Lithium-ion batteries, EV modules, dry ice. Strict IATA Section II air packing guidelines apply.
