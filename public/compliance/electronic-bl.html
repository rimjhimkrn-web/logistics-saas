Electronic Bills of Lading

Electronic transferable records can carry significant legal and operational consequences. Their validity depends on the applicable jurisdiction, the chosen technology and control model, the parties involved, and the requirements of the relevant carrier, bank and authorities.

KRIM service boundary: This page does not issue, endorse, transfer, finance or register an electronic bill of lading. No bank, carrier, shipment, legal holder, signature or transferable document is represented as connected to KRIM here.

Before using an electronic bill of lading, obtain advice from qualified legal counsel and confirm that the carrier, trading counterparties and relevant jurisdictions recognize the proposed system and process.
