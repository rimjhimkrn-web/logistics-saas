Controlled inventory changes

Direct stock mutation from this ledger is intentionally disabled. Inventory is changed only by completing the authorized inbound goods-receipt or pick/pack task. Dispatch, returns, transfers and manual adjustments require separate approved workflows and cannot be posted from this screen.
