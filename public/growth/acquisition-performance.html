Metrics to measure

  • New source-attributed opportunities per week.
  • Qualification rate = qualified candidates ÷ reviewed candidates.
  • Onboarding conversion = onboarded accounts ÷ qualified candidates.
  • Activation rate = approved active accounts ÷ onboarded accounts.
  • Time to first quote, shipment, or completed partner assignment.
  • Repeat-use and retention rates by customer cohort.
  • Cost per qualified opportunity and contribution margin by market.
  • Opt-outs, complaints, duplicate rate, and invalid lead rate as quality guardrails.
