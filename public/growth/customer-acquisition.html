How the acquisition engine should operate

Build a measured pipeline instead of presenting simulated activity as live growth.

01 · DISCOVERFind suitable markets and prospectsUse approved public business sources, referrals, inbound channels and verified integrations.
02 · QUALIFYCheck fit and service coverageConfirm route, cargo needs, provider capability, pricing and compliance before promising service.
03 · CONVERTEnquiry to quotationPersist the requirement, route it to operations and return a genuine quote when serviceable.
04 · FULFILComplete the shipmentConnect payment, eligible partner assignment, tracking, delivery evidence and support.
05 · MEASUREReconcile commercial resultsTrack recognized revenue, partner obligations, variable costs and contribution per shipment.
06 · IMPROVERetain and expandImprove channels using real completed-shipment conversion and repeat-customer evidence.
KRIM Logistics · Internal operations · Market readiness and real transaction evidence determine activation. This dashboard does not itself run an external prospect crawler or send outreach.
