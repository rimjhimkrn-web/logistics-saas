Network principles

Built around verified capability, not unsupported promises.

Partner participation is scoped to the evidence, approvals and operating requirements relevant to each service and market.

01

Evidence-led onboarding

Organisation identity, authority and capability evidence are reviewed before approval for a defined scope.

02

Partner-led service delivery

Independent approved partners provide the physical logistics services under their own responsibilities and customer agreements.

03

Connected coordination

KRIM's platform supports discovery, relevant opportunity workflows and operational visibility where those features are enabled.

Application to activation

A clear, evidence-based review process

Partner status progresses only when the relevant checks and approvals are completed.

Apply

Share applicant identity, organisation details and proposed service categories.

Verify

Provide current identity, legal, regulatory and supporting evidence requested for review.

Approve scope

Capabilities, service locations and permissions are assessed individually.

Activate

Required agreements, access controls and commercial setup are completed before participation.

Operational and commercial transparency: A submitted application does not guarantee approval, marketplace access, shipment assignments, customer demand, revenue or service coverage. KRIM connects customers with independent service partners; it does not claim to provide or guarantee the physical services listed here. Availability, rates, capacity and routes must be confirmed for the specific requirement.
