Partner admission and activation

  1. Create a secure partner account, confirm your email and complete authenticator setup.
  2. Open Partner Onboarding, select registered-business mode and submit your organisation details and supporting evidence.
  3. Provide legal identity, operating authority and requested supporting documents.
  4. KRIM reviews organisation identity, compliance and submitted evidence.
  5. Submit service capabilities and geographic coverage for separate review.
  6. Commercial access is considered only after verification, capability approval, market eligibility and applicable commercial controls.
Important: Submitting an application does not mean approval, partner status, shipment allocation, guaranteed demand, market coverage or commercial activation. No partner is represented as verified until KRIM records and confirms the required review decisions.
