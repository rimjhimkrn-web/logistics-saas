Activation gates

  • Identity and contact details checked.
  • Service capability, geographic coverage and operational capacity validated.
  • Consent and lawful basis for communications recorded where required.
  • Commercial terms, payout details and applicable compliance checks reviewed.
  • Test transaction / shipment workflow completed where relevant.
  • Human owner approves activation; never auto-activate from a directory listing.
