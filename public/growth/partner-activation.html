The activation pathway

Six stages with clear decision points

Each stage has a defined outcome. Additional evidence or clarification may be requested before moving forward.

01

Enquiry & application

Share your legal or professional identity, contact details, proposed services and operating locations.

02

Identity & document review

KRIM reviews submitted information and relevant registrations, licences, insurance and supporting evidence where applicable.

03

Capability assessment

Define service modes, geographic scope, equipment, capacity evidence, operating limitations and authorised activities.

04

Agreement & commercial setup

Confirm the applicable agreement, responsibilities, commercial terms, escalation contacts and service-specific conditions.

05

Role-based access & readiness

Set approved organisation users, permissions, security requirements, workflows and operational contacts.

06

Controlled activation

Activation follows final approval and readiness checks. Only the approved scope may be presented for potential opportunities.

What activation status means

1Application receivedInformation has been submitted; no approval is implied.
2Under reviewIdentity, evidence and proposed service scope are being assessed.
3Clarification requiredFurther information or corrections are needed before a decision.
4Scope approvedSpecified services and limits have been approved; operational access may still be pending.
5Operationally activatedRequired agreement, access and readiness controls are complete for the approved scope.
Important: An approved profile is not a guarantee of work. Any shipment or service engagement remains subject to a specific opportunity, partner acceptance, customer terms and applicable law.
