Source approval pathway

Use this checklist for directories, associations, public registries, inbound forms, referrals and other potential discovery sources.

01
Identify the sourceRecord the provider, URL, region, source type and intended use.
02
Check terms & licensingReview permitted use, API terms, attribution and redistribution limits.
03
Protect people & dataMinimise personal data and document lawful basis or explicit permission for contact.
04
Approve before activationA reviewer records evidence, restrictions, opt-out handling and review date.
Important: A public listing is not proof that a person wants marketing or that its data can be reused. Do not activate a source until its terms and applicable laws have been reviewed.
