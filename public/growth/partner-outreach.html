Quality over volume

Outreach should be targeted, transparent and respectful. A conversation is an invitation to explore fit—not a promise of approval or business.

  • Prioritise relevant service capability
  • Explain KRIM’s platform role accurately
  • Ask for evidence, not unsupported claims
  • Follow up responsibly and record consent
Engagement approach

A thoughtful path from first contact to review

Keep the message clear, make the next step easy and avoid suggesting that partner demand or assignments are guaranteed.

01

Identify relevant partners

Map providers by service type, operating geography, experience and evidence of capability. Start with a focused shortlist.

02

Introduce KRIM clearly

Explain the platform, the proposed collaboration and the partner’s responsibility for delivering contracted services.

03

Qualify and follow through

Understand scope, documentation, limitations and interest. Agree a next step and keep a factual record of the discussion.

Potential logistics partner introduction — KRIM

Hello,

I’m reaching out on behalf of KRIM Logistics, a technology platform being developed to connect customers with relevant logistics service partners.

We are interested in learning more about your organisation’s capabilities and whether there may be a suitable fit within our partner network.

If you are open to an introductory discussion, we would welcome a brief overview of your services, operating geography and relevant credentials.

Any further review or activation would be subject to capability verification, applicable requirements and mutual agreement. This introduction does not imply guaranteed assignments or business.

Kind regards,
KRIM Logistics

During qualification

1
Understand actual servicesConfirm what the provider performs directly and what is subcontracted.
2
Clarify operating limitsRecord service boundaries, exclusions, availability conditions and evidence dates.
3
Explain next stepsDescribe the application and verification process without implying approval.
4
Keep accurate recordsRecord the discussion outcome, consent and agreed follow-up date in an authorised system.
