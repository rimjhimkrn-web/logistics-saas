Intelligence framework

Six dimensions of a dependable network

These dimensions describe the information KRIM should organise and validate. They are not claims that any particular partner, route or capability is currently available.

01

Identity & trust

Legal or professional identity, authorised representatives, registration evidence and review history.

02

Service capability

Transport modes, specialist services, equipment, facilities, handling limits and permitted activities.

03

Geographic scope

Origin and destination areas, service boundaries, route constraints and evidence supporting declared coverage.

04

Operational readiness

People, resources, operating hours, escalation contacts, process maturity and contingency arrangements.

05

Quality & performance

Validated service milestones, exceptions, evidence quality, complaint outcomes and appropriately contextualised trends.

06

Commercial fit

Approved service terms, quote validity, cost components, capacity conditions and partner acceptance requirements.

Responsible intelligence

From raw information to a defensible decision

Useful intelligence is not simply a dashboard score. It is a traceable decision process with clear limits.

Data quality principles

✓
Source and timestampRecord where information came from and when it was last confirmed.
✓
Evidence and reviewerLink decisions to relevant evidence and an authorised review trail.
✓
Scope and expiryMake service boundaries, validity periods and exclusions visible.
✓
Correction and disputeAllow inaccurate information to be challenged and corrected with history preserved.

How intelligence should be used

1
DiscoverIdentify potentially relevant partner capabilities for a defined requirement.
2
ValidateConfirm current scope, evidence, availability and commercial conditions.
3
DecideApply human review and the required approval controls.
4
MonitorUpdate records when evidence, capacity or performance changes.
Privacy and fairness: restrict access by role and organisation, collect only necessary information, protect commercially sensitive data, and avoid unsupported rankings or automated decisions that could unfairly exclude a partner.
