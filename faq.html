Getting started

01 / Basics
What is KRIM Logistics?

KRIM Logistics is building a Global Logistics Operating System: a digital platform intended to connect customers, logistics partners and businesses through structured workflows for discovering services, requesting quotes, coordinating shipments and accessing operational information. Actual features and service availability depend on the current platform release and verified network capability.

Is KRIM a logistics company, a marketplace or a technology platform?

KRIM uses technology to support logistics discovery and coordination. Its legal and contractual role in a particular shipment depends on the actual service, agreements, payment flow and applicable law. The booking documents should identify the contracting party and performing provider. See the Legal Notice and Service Disclaimer.

Does “global-ready” mean I can ship to every country?

No. Global-ready architecture is not a claim of worldwide operational coverage. A route can be offered only after relevant provider capacity, service scope, documents, permits, customs requirements and legal checks are confirmed for that shipment. Current coverage may be limited.

Who can use KRIM?

KRIM is designed for individuals, small and medium businesses, e-commerce sellers, manufacturers, importers/exporters, enterprises and logistics service partners. Access to particular workflows may depend on account type, verification, eligibility and service availability.

Quotes & bookings

02 / Before dispatch
How do I request a shipment quote?

Use the Request a Quote workflow and provide accurate origin, destination, cargo description, quantity, weight, dimensions, timing and handling details. The platform or KRIM team may request clarification. Submitting a request does not reserve capacity or confirm a price.

Is the displayed quote a final price?

Not necessarily. An indicative rate, automated estimate or partner bid is preliminary unless the authorised contracting party issues or accepts it with a clear scope, validity and charges. Final pricing may depend on verified cargo details, route, equipment, taxes and disclosed additional services. Review the written confirmation before accepting.

When is my shipment booking confirmed?

Only when you receive an authorised booking confirmation or equivalent record identifying the contracting party, provider, route, cargo, service scope, agreed charges, key dates, required documents and applicable conditions. A login, enquiry, quote request, draft shipment or payment initiation alone is not confirmation.

Can I change or cancel a booking?

Request changes or cancellation promptly through the designated support channel and quote your shipment reference. Whether a change is possible, and whether any charge or refund applies, depends on the accepted booking, provider commitments, work already performed and applicable law. Read the Refund & Cancellation Policy before proceeding.

What information should I prepare before requesting a quote?
  • Pickup and delivery locations, including postal codes and access restrictions.
  • Accurate cargo description, package count, dimensions and weight.
  • Packaging, loading/unloading and special handling requirements.
  • Preferred dates, contact details and any required documents.
  • Disclosure of hazardous, restricted, fragile, temperature-sensitive or high-value goods.

Shipments & tracking

03 / In movement
How can I track a shipment?

Use the Track a Shipment page and enter the shipment reference or the details requested by the tracking workflow. Tracking is available only where shipment records and provider updates are connected. If the reference is not recognised or an event appears incorrect, contact support with the booking reference.

Why has my tracking status not changed?

Events may depend on scans or updates from a carrier, warehouse, driver or external system. Updates can be delayed, unavailable between milestones or corrected after reconciliation. A lack of a new event does not by itself prove that the shipment has stopped. Contact support if the delay is material or outside the confirmed service window.

Are estimated delivery dates guaranteed?

Estimated transit times and ETAs are not guarantees unless the authorised contracting party expressly confirms a guaranteed service commitment in writing. Weather, traffic, inspections, congestion, route restrictions, provider capacity and other events may affect delivery.

What should I do if cargo arrives damaged or short?

Record visible damage, shortage, seal issues or exceptions on the delivery record before acknowledging receipt where possible. Photograph the cargo and packaging, retain relevant documents and notify the responsible provider and KRIM promptly. Follow the applicable claims process and notice periods in your contract and the Shipping & Delivery Policy. Do not discard packaging until the issue is resolved.

Can I ship dangerous or restricted goods?

Do not tender prohibited or restricted goods without confirming legality, required declarations, permits, specialist handling and written provider acceptance in advance. Rules differ by cargo, transport mode and jurisdiction. Misdescription or concealment can create serious safety and legal consequences.

Payments & refunds

04 / Money matters
Which payment methods are available?

Available methods are those shown in the secure checkout or payment instructions for the specific transaction. They may vary by customer, currency, provider and location. Do not send money to a personal account or payment destination that is not identified in an authorised KRIM transaction instruction.

My payment succeeded but the booking is not confirmed. What should I do?

Keep the payment reference and shipment enquiry details, then contact KRIM through the Contact page. A successful payment status does not alone establish that a provider accepted the shipment or that capacity is reserved. KRIM will need to reconcile the payment and booking records.

How long does a refund take?

Refund eligibility and processing depend on the accepted booking and applicable policy. Once an authorised refund is initiated, the time for the amount to appear can vary by payment method, bank and payment provider. Check the Refund & Cancellation Policy for the process and reference details. Do not share an OTP, password or card PIN to receive a refund.

Who decides whether I am eligible for a refund?

Eligibility is determined under the accepted booking, KRIM's applicable refund terms, the responsible contracting party's obligations and mandatory law—not by a payment gateway alone. The gateway processes the payment or refund instruction; it does not independently decide the underlying contractual entitlement.

Partners & network

05 / Service providers
How can a logistics provider join the KRIM network?

Start through the KRIM Network page or Contact KRIM and identify your operating locations, services, equipment, capacity, licences, insurance and business references. Submission is an expression of interest, not approval or a promise of work.

What verification is required for partners?

Verification may include identity and business records, tax details, applicable registrations and permits, insurance, bank-account ownership, operating capability, references, safety practices and service-area checks. Requirements depend on the service and jurisdiction. A profile is not treated as verified until the required checks are completed.

Does joining KRIM guarantee shipment leads or revenue?

No. Network participation does not guarantee enquiries, assignments, volumes, rates, revenue or exclusivity. Opportunities depend on customer demand, provider suitability, capacity, geography, service quality and acceptance of the specific work order.

Can partners subcontract or transfer a shipment?

Only where permitted by the applicable partner agreement, shipment work order and law, with any required prior approval and disclosure. A partner remains responsible for its contractual obligations and must not transfer cargo or customer information to an unauthorised party.

Accounts & support

06 / Help & access
How do I sign in?

Use the official KRIM sign-in page. Keep your password and authentication codes private. If you cannot access your account, use the recovery option displayed in the sign-in workflow or contact support. Never send credentials by chat or email.

How do I contact KRIM about an existing shipment?

Use the Contact page, choose the existing-shipment enquiry type and include your shipment reference, the issue, relevant dates and the action you are requesting. Share only information necessary to identify and investigate the matter.

How do I raise a complaint?

Submit the issue through the Contact page and clearly mark it as a complaint. Include your contact details, shipment or payment reference, a concise description and supporting evidence. See the Grievance Redressal & Consumer Support page for escalation guidance and the current disclosure status.

How is my personal information used?

Read the Privacy Notice for information about the categories of data, purposes, sharing, retention and privacy requests. Do not include passwords, one-time codes, payment PINs or unnecessary sensitive information in a support request.

Where can I read KRIM's policies?

Visit the Terms & Conditions, Privacy Notice, Cookie Notice, Refund & Cancellation Policy, Shipping & Delivery Policy, Service Disclaimer and Legal Notice. The policy relevant to your accepted transaction should be read with its booking documents.
