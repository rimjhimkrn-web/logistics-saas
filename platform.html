Global architecture · honest activation

Global-ready by design. Activated market by market.

KRIM's long-term architecture is designed to accommodate country-specific rules, currencies, languages, time zones and operating partners. Public availability must still be confirmed route by route.

Foundations present in code

Digital workflow components

Public shipment enquiry, authenticated customer and partner workspaces, shipment and quote workflow components, and a public tracking interface are present in the codebase. This does not certify end-to-end production operation.

Activation and testing required

Service and market availability

Rates, provider capacity, accepted assignments, live milestone feeds, customs coverage, payment settlement and cross-border services must be validated for each route before being offered as available.

KRIM operating principles

Trust is built into every hand-off.

These principles preserve KRIM's identity as a transparent, partner-connected logistics operating system.

Verified before activation

Partner registration is not approval. Review capabilities and documents before assigning live work.

Price before commitment

No invented rates or implied bookings. Customers accept a valid, clearly scoped commercial offer.

Evidence before closure

Milestones, exceptions, delivery proof and financial records should be traceable to their source and owner.
