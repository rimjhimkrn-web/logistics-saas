01 · Create a draft rate card

Draft only. A positive amount is not approval and does not make a rate customer-usable.

02 · Record provider cost & tax evidence

Requires an active, verified registered business with verified capability for the selected route and mode. Cost + KRIM fee must reconcile to the customer rate.

Do not paste sensitive documents or personal data into this field. Use the approved private evidence repository reference.

03 · Approve a rate card

Approval checks both route-market readiness gates. An unready market, missing provider or missing evidence is rejected by the database.

Approval does not activate the card. Activation is a separate action with fresh provider, evidence, duplicate-rate and market-gate checks.
