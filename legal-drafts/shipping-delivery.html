Shipping & Delivery Policy

WORKING DRAFT — NOT APPROVED FOR CUSTOMER OR PARTNER ACCEPTANCE.
Not legal advice. Do not publish until legal structure, GST, payment flow, service facts, contacts and operational readiness are verified by qualified Indian counsel and tax professionals.

Availability

KRIM's initial operating focus is India. Route availability is confirmed only after a provider, capacity, cargo acceptance, legal permissions and price are verified in writing. An enquiry is not a booking.

Booking confirmation

Before customer acceptance, show the actual service provider, service mode, cargo, origin and destination, estimated transit window, charges and taxes, exclusions, payment recipient, invoice issuer and claims contact.

Pickup and transit

Shipper must provide accurate cargo information, compliant packaging and documents. Provider should record package count and condition at pickup. Tracking data may be delayed and is informational unless confirmed.

Delivery and exceptions

Record recipient and delivery evidence. Note visible shortage or damage before acknowledging receipt where possible. Communicate material delays and exceptions promptly. This policy does not excuse negligence or statutory duties.

Returns and insurance

Return, storage and re-delivery charges must be disclosed and lawful. Insurance is not implied; disclose insurer, policyholder, cover, premium, exclusions and claims process where offered.
