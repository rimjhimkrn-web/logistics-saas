Payment & Settlement Disclosure

WORKING DRAFT — NOT APPROVED FOR CUSTOMER OR PARTNER ACCEPTANCE.
Not legal advice. Do not publish until legal structure, GST, payment flow, service facts, contacts and operational readiness are verified by qualified Indian counsel and tax professionals.

Checkout disclosure

Each checkout must identify the legal recipient, payment aggregator, total amount, taxes, provider charges, KRIM fee (if any), and invoice issuer. Payment success is not itself a GST tax invoice.

Authorised channels

Pay only through authenticated KRIM checkout or a verified invoice. Never share OTPs, UPI PINs, passwords or card security codes with staff or partners.

Fees and partner settlement

Commission, platform fee, partner payable and deductions must be shown in the booking and signed commercial schedule. Split settlement is conditional on payment-provider approval for the exact account, product and flow and on legal compliance. No fixed percentage or settlement date is promised here.

Refunds and chargebacks

Booking terms must identify refund decision-maker and funding party. Refunds are subject to law, payment-network rules, bank processing and reconciliation. Do not mark a refund complete until provider status confirms it.

GST

Supplier identity, SAC, rate, invoice, e-commerce operator/TCS and reporting depend on actual contracts and money flow. Obtain qualified GST review before enabling live transactions.

No wallet

KRIM does not claim to operate a wallet, escrow or stored-value facility. Do not enable such features without separate legal authorisation.
