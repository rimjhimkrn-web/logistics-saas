Customer Terms & Conditions

WORKING DRAFT — NOT APPROVED FOR CUSTOMER OR PARTNER ACCEPTANCE.
Not legal advice. Do not publish until legal structure, GST, payment flow, service facts, contacts and operational readiness are verified by qualified Indian counsel and tax professionals.

1. Platform role and provider identity

KRIM is intended to provide digital freight enquiries, quote workflows and coordination tools. For each booking, the customer must be told whether KRIM acts as an intermediary/agent or as supplier/principal. The actual carrier, warehouse or other provider and contracting party must be identified before acceptance. KRIM must not claim to be a carrier, insurer, customs broker or warehouse operator unless legally entitled and actually acting in that capacity.

2. Enquiry is not a booking

An enquiry, indicative estimate or shipment request does not reserve capacity. A booking is confirmed only by written confirmation showing provider, scope, origin/destination, cargo, timing, charges and taxes, invoice issuer, payment recipient, cancellation terms and claims route, followed by affirmative customer acceptance.

3. Customer declarations

Customer must accurately disclose cargo type, quantity, weight, dimensions, value, packaging, handling needs, addresses and required permits. Dangerous, restricted, fragile, high-value, perishable or temperature-controlled goods must be disclosed before quotation.

4. Provider performance

The identified provider remains responsible for physical service and statutory obligations. Nothing excludes liability or consumer rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

5. Quotes, taxes and payment

Quotes must state validity, inclusions/exclusions, GST treatment and invoice issuer. Pay only through disclosed authorised checkout or verified invoice. A payment receipt is not necessarily a tax invoice.

6. Changes, cancellation and refunds

Changes depend on capacity and written confirmation. Cancellation charges must be disclosed before purchase and comply with law. Refund funding party, amount and route must be stated in booking terms; statutory rights remain unaffected.

7. Delivery and claims

Transit times are estimates unless expressly guaranteed. Record visible damage/shortage at delivery, preserve packaging and evidence, and notify provider promptly. Contractual deadlines cannot override mandatory law.

8. Prohibited cargo and compliance

Do not tender unlawful or prohibited cargo or evade customs, tax, safety or transport requirements. Providers may refuse unsafe or undeclared goods subject to law.

9. Privacy and complaints

Personal data is handled under final privacy notice. Complaints may be submitted through verified grievance channel. These terms do not prevent access to statutory consumer forums.

10. No blanket waiver

No clause waives fraud, wilful misconduct, statutory duties or non-excludable liability. Governing law, dispute forum and document hierarchy must be reviewed and completed in final contract.
