Critical pre-publication blockers

  1. Marketplace eligibility: Rule 4(1)(a) of the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 describes an e-commerce entity as a company incorporated under the Companies Act or specified foreign entity/office. KRIM is currently a sole proprietorship. Obtain a written legal opinion on whether the planned marketplace activity is covered and whether a company structure is required before operating it.
  2. 2026 amendment: The Department of Consumer Affairs states that the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Amendment Rules, 2026 commence on 1 January 2027. Verify the final Gazette text and implement the amended duties before that date.
  3. GST and payment flow: Confirm who contracts with customers, supplies each service, issues invoices, collects funds, funds refunds, bears claims and reports GST/TCS. Match Razorpay approval and live software.
  4. Grievance details: Insert verified legal name, principal address, customer-care contacts and grievance officer identity/contact. Do not invent or publish placeholders.
  5. Privacy: Map actual Supabase, Razorpay, CRM, Meta, WhatsApp, Telegram, analytics and email data flows; implement security, retention, deletion and processor terms.
  6. Service reality: Publish only routes, partners, insurance and service levels that are verified and operational.
