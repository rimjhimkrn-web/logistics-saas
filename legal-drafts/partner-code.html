Partner Code of Conduct

WORKING DRAFT — NOT APPROVED FOR CUSTOMER OR PARTNER ACCEPTANCE.
Not legal advice. Do not publish until legal structure, GST, payment flow, service facts, contacts and operational readiness are verified by qualified Indian counsel and tax professionals.
  • Operate only within valid licences, permits, insurance and verified capabilities.
  • Provide truthful business, vehicle, facility, pricing and service information.
  • Never discriminate unlawfully, harass, threaten, bribe or retaliate.
  • Protect cargo, customer property, data and credentials.
  • Never falsify tracking, weight, delivery proof, invoices or compliance records.
  • Do not request off-platform payment contrary to the executed agreement.
  • Report safety incidents, loss, damage, theft, accidents and data breaches promptly.
  • Use approved subcontractors and comply with labour and safety laws.
  • Cooperate with lawful audits and corrective actions.

Reporting and enforcement

Publish a monitored reporting channel before enforcement. Corrective action, suspension or termination must follow the signed agreement and a fair process, except urgent safety action.
