Prohibited & Restricted Goods Policy

WORKING DRAFT — NOT APPROVED FOR CUSTOMER OR PARTNER ACCEPTANCE.
Not legal advice. Do not publish until legal structure, GST, payment flow, service facts, contacts and operational readiness are verified by qualified Indian counsel and tax professionals.

This minimum screening guide is not exhaustive. Apply all central, state, local, carrier and route-specific laws.

Do not tender

  • Illegal drugs, narcotics or controlled substances except lawful authorised shipments.
  • Explosives, fireworks, ammunition, firearms or weapons except where expressly lawful and handled by authorised specialist providers.
  • Hazardous, toxic, radioactive, infectious or biohazard materials unless lawful and accepted by qualified authorised carriers under required controls.
  • Counterfeit, stolen, smuggled or unlawfully obtained goods and protected wildlife contraband.
  • Any cargo prohibited by law, sanctions, customs or carrier rules.

Restricted — prior written approval

Batteries, aerosols, liquids, perfumes, alcohol, tobacco/nicotine, medicines, food, plants, seeds, animal products, high-value goods, cash, live animals, perishables and oversized cargo may require specialist approval, permits, packaging and documentation.

Declarations

Customer must disclose contents, value, weight, dimensions and dangerous-goods classification accurately. Partners must screen cargo and never misdeclare goods to evade controls. Maintain a route/provider-specific acceptance matrix.
