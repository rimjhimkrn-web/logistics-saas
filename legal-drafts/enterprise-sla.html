Not legal advice. Do not publish until legal structure, GST, payment flow, service facts, contacts and operational readiness are verified by qualified Indian counsel and tax professionals.

This template creates no guaranteed service level until signed and backed by measured capacity, staffing and partner commitments.

Scope

Order form identifies lanes, modes, cargo, service hours, providers, integration scope, contacts and exclusions.

Metrics

Any uptime, response, pickup, transit, tracking or delivery metric must define measurement window, source, exclusions, sample size, reporting and remedy. Do not publish a target until measurable and supportable.

Incidents

Classify safety, loss, security, major outage and routine cases. Set response targets only after staffing and alerting are operational. Keep auditable timelines.

Security and exit

Signed agreement must specify access controls, encryption, incident notice, subcontractors, data location, retention, audit, deletion, credits and termination.
