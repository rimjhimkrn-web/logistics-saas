Claims, Loss & Damage Policy

WORKING DRAFT — NOT APPROVED FOR CUSTOMER OR PARTNER ACCEPTANCE.
Not legal advice. Do not publish until legal structure, GST, payment flow, service facts, contacts and operational readiness are verified by qualified Indian counsel and tax professionals.

Responsible party

Booking must identify the physical service provider, claims contact and party responsible for decision and payment. KRIM may coordinate where authorised but must not describe itself as insurer or carrier unless it is.

Immediate steps

  1. Notify provider and KRIM promptly through the verified booking channel.
  2. Record exceptions on delivery receipt where possible.
  3. Photograph packaging, labels, seals, contents and damage.
  4. Preserve packaging, invoices, packing lists and communications.
  5. Take reasonable steps to prevent further loss.

Investigation and settlement

Responsible provider investigates and provides a reasoned outcome. Claim value, exclusions, limits and settlement depend on contract, applicable law and valid insurance. No automatic full-value reimbursement is promised; non-excludable rights remain intact.

Insurance

Insurance exists only where a valid policy/certificate is issued. Disclose insurer, policyholder, sum insured, premium, deductibles, exclusions and claim steps before purchase.
