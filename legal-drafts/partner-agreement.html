Logistics Partner Agreement

WORKING DRAFT — NOT APPROVED FOR CUSTOMER OR PARTNER ACCEPTANCE.
Not legal advice. Do not publish until legal structure, GST, payment flow, service facts, contacts and operational readiness are verified by qualified Indian counsel and tax professionals.

Appointment and status

Partner may receive shipment opportunities only after due diligence and a signed agreement. Partner is an independent provider; no employment, partnership, franchise or exclusivity is created by this draft.

Verification

Provide genuine legal identity, constitution, PAN/GSTIN where applicable, bank proof in legal name, business address, licences, permits, insurance, vehicle/facility details and authorised contacts. Approval is limited to verified service and geography.

Assignments

Accept each assignment expressly and confirm capacity, route, price, equipment, timing, cargo restrictions, insurance and terms. Customer must be told who contracts to perform the service. Subcontracting requires disclosed approval.

Compliance

Partner complies with transport, motor vehicle, labour, safety, tax, environmental, customs and data laws. Do not falsify tracking, delivery proof, invoices or documents. Report incidents promptly.

Commercial terms and settlement

A signed schedule must define rates, KRIM fee/commission, taxes, invoice issuer, deductions and settlement. Any payment split is subject to payment-provider approval of the precise product and flow, KYC, refunds, chargebacks and law. This draft promises no fixed percentage or settlement date.

Claims, data and termination

Allocate cargo responsibility and insurance in booking-specific terms consistent with law. Use customer data only to perform assignments, secure it and report breaches. Final agreement must define audit, suspension, response, termination and settlement reconciliation.

Execution

Insert legal names, addresses, authorised signatories, governing law and dispute forum after legal review. Binding only when executed by authorised parties.
