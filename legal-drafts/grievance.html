Grievance Redressal Procedure

WORKING DRAFT — NOT APPROVED FOR CUSTOMER OR PARTNER ACCEPTANCE.
Not legal advice. Do not publish until legal structure, GST, payment flow, service facts, contacts and operational readiness are verified by qualified Indian counsel and tax professionals.

Details to complete before publication

Insert verified legal entity name, principal address, customer-care email and phone, and grievance officer name, designation, monitored email and phone. No placeholder or personal contact is invented here.

Submit a complaint

Use the verified contact page or booking support channel. Include name, reply method, booking reference, issue, dates and evidence. Never include passwords, OTPs, UPI PINs or full card details.

Acknowledgement and resolution

Where the E-Commerce Rules apply, configure acknowledgement within 48 hours and redressal within one month, and preserve a complaint record as required by rules then in force. Provide complaint reference and fair escalation.

Statutory remedies

Do not restrict access to competent consumer commissions or regulators. Record receipt, acknowledgement, investigation, provider response and outcome proportionately.

2027 change

The Department of Consumer Affairs states the 2026 amendment commences on 1 January 2027. Verify Gazette text and complete any National Consumer Helpline convergence and other amended duties before that date.
