The onboarding journey

From introduction to approved participation

Registration begins a review. It does not automatically activate a partner or guarantee shipment opportunities.

01

Choose your pathway

Register as a Registered Business or submit Individual Partner Interest. Development & Test access is a separate, non-commercial workspace.

02

Share your profile

Provide accurate organisation or individual details, operating locations, service scope and contact information.

03

Complete verification

Submit applicable identity, business, authority, insurance and capability evidence for review.

04

Await a decision

KRIM reviews eligibility and service scope. Commercial access is enabled only after approval.

Important operating note: KRIM does not promise guaranteed loads, fixed freight volumes, universal geographic coverage or immediate payouts. Availability, pricing, contracts and responsibilities must be confirmed for each approved route and engagement. Partners remain responsible for their legal permissions, personnel, assets and service delivery.
