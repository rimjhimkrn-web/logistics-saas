A CLEAR REVIEW PROCESS

From interest to approved participation

Registration starts the review process. It does not automatically activate a partner or guarantee shipment opportunities.

1Create your account

Choose the appropriate registered-business, individual-interest or development/test pathway.

2Submit your profile

Provide accurate organisation, location, service, coverage and contact details.

3Complete verification

Submit applicable identity, business, authority, insurance and capability evidence for review.

4Await activation

KRIM reviews eligibility and service scope. Commercial access is enabled only after approval.

Important: KRIM does not promise guaranteed loads, fixed freight volumes, universal geographic coverage or immediate payouts. Actual service availability, pricing, contracts and responsibilities must be confirmed for each approved route and engagement. Partners remain responsible for their own legal permissions, personnel, assets and service delivery.
