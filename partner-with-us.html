A CLEAR REVIEW PROCESS

From introduction to approved participation

Submitting an interest profile starts a review. It does not automatically activate a partner or guarantee shipment opportunities.

KRIM reviews eligibility and scope. Operational access follows approval and any required agreement.

Operational clarity: KRIM does not promise guaranteed loads, fixed freight volumes, universal geographic coverage, fixed rates or immediate payouts. Availability, pricing, contracts and responsibilities must be confirmed for each approved route and engagement. Partners remain responsible for their own legal permissions, personnel, assets and service delivery.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Partner onboarding, clearly explained

Does registration guarantee loads or revenue?

No. Registration is an expression of interest and review. Shipment opportunities depend on verified capability, customer demand, route suitability and commercial agreement.

Can an individual or a developing business apply?

The onboarding pathway is designed to distinguish registered businesses, individuals and development/testing profiles. The available scope and approval requirements may differ.

Is KRIM currently active in every country and route?

No. India is the first market activation. Coverage is represented only when relevant partners, route requirements and local conditions have been validated.

When can a partner access operational opportunities?

After the relevant profile and evidence review, approval, and any required commercial or operational agreement. Access may be limited to approved services and locations.
