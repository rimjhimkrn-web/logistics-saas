Global Partner Architecture

KRIM partners are not limited to one transport category or one country. The platform can connect carriers, transporters, warehouses, freight forwarders and specialized logistics providers across markets.

Partner authorization, organization relationships, capabilities, documents, compliance and network participation belong to the backend security and operating model. The frontend only presents data that the authenticated user is authorized to access.

Partner capabilities and capacity can subsequently participate in quote pricing, shipment assignment, dispatch, tracking, exception management, claims and financial settlement.
