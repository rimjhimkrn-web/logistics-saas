Partner lifecycle

From discovery to network participation

Participation is progressive. A registration or expression of interest is not itself a verification or service approval.

Discover & apply

Share organization details, contact information and the service domains relevant to your business.

Verify & review

KRIM reviews identity, business evidence, capabilities and applicable compliance requirements.

Approve & configure

Approved organization users, service scope, operating locations and permissions are configured.

Participate & improve

Eligible partners may receive relevant opportunities and participate in authorized workflows.

Clear operating boundaries

Trust through transparent responsibilities

The platform coordinates a network; it does not replace the legal, operational or regulatory responsibilities of each service provider.

What KRIM provides

Digital discovery and partner onboarding workflows, capability and evidence management, authorized marketplace connections, coordination tools and shipment visibility features where enabled.

What each partner remains responsible for

Its own service delivery, vehicles and personnel, licenses and permits, insurance, safety, pricing commitments, customer contracts and compliance with applicable laws.

Frequently asked questions

Partner network, explained

Does KRIM itself operate trucks, ships or warehouses?

KRIM is the platform and coordination layer. Physical logistics services are delivered by the relevant independent partners under their own responsibilities and agreements.

Can a partner join in more than one domain?

Yes. An organization may apply for multiple service domains. Each capability and operating scope is reviewed and approved separately as applicable.

Does registration guarantee loads or customers?

No. Registration does not guarantee approval, marketplace access, shipment assignments, revenue, rates or a minimum volume of work.

How are countries and operating locations handled?

Market participation is evaluated according to partner evidence, local operating requirements and the services approved for that market. KRIM's initial market activation is India.

How can an organization express interest?

Use the partner application pathway to submit organization and capability information for review. Do not submit passwords or confidential payment credentials.
