Claim Lifecycle

1. Report Claim Authorized party reports a loss, damage or dispute.
2. Evidence Collect Documents, POD, images and relevant records are attached.
3. Review Investigate Authorized team reviews facts and responsibility.
4. Decide Outcome Claim is approved, rejected or escalated.
5. Settle Compensation Approved financial settlement is processed.
6. Close Audit Resolution and evidence remain permanently auditable.

Global Claims Architecture

KRIM Claims is a controlled resolution layer connecting shipments, tracking, documents, proof of delivery, exceptions, disputes and finance.

A claim can contain its shipment context, claimant, claim type, evidence, investigation, decision, approved amount and settlement state. Claims can therefore move from operational incident to governed financial resolution without creating a disconnected process.

Claim approval and settlement are privileged operations. The frontend does not determine liability or authorize financial settlement; those decisions remain controlled by backend workflows, permissions, RLS and audit records.
