Capacity Flow

1. Network Resource Partner/fleet/warehouse enters network.
2. Availability Schedule Resource availability is recorded.
3. Matching Route Capacity is matched to demand.
4. Allocation Booking Authorized capacity is allocated.
5. Dispatch Execution Allocated resource enters operations.
6. Release Completion Capacity becomes available again.

Global Capacity Architecture

KRIM capacity is designed as a shared global operating layer. Capacity resources can represent vehicles, fleets, carriers, containers, rail resources, warehouse space, air cargo capacity or other authorized logistics resources.

Availability can be linked to market, location, route, transport mode, time window, weight, volume, equipment and partner capability. The capacity engine can then support quote pricing, shipment assignment, dispatch and network planning.

The frontend does not invent available capacity. Actual capacity, allocation and utilization should come from authorized backend records and network rules.
