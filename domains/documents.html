6. Audit Retain Access and lifecycle events remain auditable.

Global Document Architecture

KRIM Documents is a shared document-management layer for shipments, partners, finance, customs, compliance and proof of delivery.

Documents remain associated with authorized organizations, shipments, transactions or operational entities. Access is controlled by authentication, roles, permissions and backend policies.

The architecture supports secure storage, metadata, verification, auditability and future regional data-storage requirements without making the public frontend a security boundary.
