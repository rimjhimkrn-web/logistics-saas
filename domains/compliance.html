Global Compliance Architecture

KRIM Compliance is a global governance layer designed to evaluate shipments, organizations, partners, documents, markets and transactions against applicable rules.

The architecture is market-aware and can support country-specific requirements, customs controls, sanctions screening, restricted goods, dual-use controls and other regulatory policies as the KRIM network expands.

Compliance decisions must be enforced by authorized backend services, database policies and controlled workflows. The frontend only presents authorized compliance information and actions.
