Global Network Architecture

KRIM's network is designed as a global operating layer rather than an India-only network. Countries and markets can be activated independently while the underlying organization, relationship, location, capability, capacity and shipment architecture remains global.

Network participants can include customers, carriers, transport partners, warehouses, service providers and other authorized logistics organizations. Their relationships and capabilities are controlled through the backend authorization model.

Network capacity can feed shipment planning, quote pricing, matching, dispatch and tracking. This allows KRIM to operate as a connected logistics network instead of a collection of isolated market systems.
