Network Operating Model

KRIM is designed as a global logistics network rather than a single-country transport marketplace. The global KRIM Core provides the common operating architecture while individual markets activate their own organizations, locations, currencies, regulatory rules, partners, capacity and operating requirements.

This allows India to operate as the first active market without making India the architectural boundary of the platform.
