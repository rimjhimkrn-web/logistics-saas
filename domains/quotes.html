Global Commercial Architecture

KRIM quotes are designed around a global pricing model. Quote calculation can combine network capacity, carrier/partner cost, route, mode, cargo, service level, market rules, currency, taxes, duties, fees and KRIM commercial policies.

A quote can progress from request → pricing → review → customer delivery → acceptance → shipment creation. Actual commercial values should come from authorized backend pricing and network data rather than hard-coded frontend prices.
