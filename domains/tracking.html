Global Tracking Architecture

KRIM tracking is designed as a multi-source infrastructure layer. Vehicle GPS, driver mobile location, carrier APIs, telematics, container tracking and manual events can feed the same global shipment and shipment-leg tracking model.

GPS availability does not determine whether a shipment can be tracked. KRIM can continue using milestones, events, carrier updates and other authorized tracking sources when live GPS is unavailable.
