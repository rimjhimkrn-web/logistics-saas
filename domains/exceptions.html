Exception Lifecycle

1. Detect Signal System, user or integration creates an exception signal.
2. Classify Priority Type, severity and operational impact are determined.
3. Assign Owner Authorized operational owner takes responsibility.
4. Act Resolution Corrective action is executed.
5. Escalate Governance Critical or overdue cases move to higher control.
6. Close Audit Resolution and evidence remain recorded.

Global Exception Architecture

KRIM Exceptions is the operational control layer for unexpected events across shipments, tracking, capacity, finance, documents, compliance, partners and platform infrastructure.

Exceptions can originate from automated workflows, tracking signals, integration events, user actions or backend risk detection. Each exception can carry priority, ownership, SLA, escalation and resolution information.

The frontend only presents and requests authorized actions. Actual exception creation, ownership, escalation and resolution must be enforced through KRIM backend authorization, RLS and controlled workflows.
