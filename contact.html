Helpful answers

Before you contact us.

What details should I include for a shipment enquiry?

Include pickup and delivery locations, cargo type, approximate weight and dimensions, preferred movement date, and any handling or delivery requirements you already know.

Does submitting the form confirm a shipment booking?

No. The form records an enquiry only. A booking is confirmed only through a separate confirmation after the service, provider, route, capacity, price and applicable requirements have been reviewed.

How can a logistics provider apply to join KRIM?

Use the partner onboarding journey to share your business and service capabilities. Submission does not mean automatic approval or guaranteed work; partner details and operating capabilities require review.

What if my shipment is already moving?

Start with the tracking page using your KRIM shipment reference. If you still need help, select “Existing shipment — support or status” in this form and include the reference number.

Can I contact KRIM about an international route?

Yes. Share the origin, destination and shipment details. International interest can be reviewed, but a route is not commercially available until its providers, operating arrangements and applicable requirements are validated.
