Our operating model

A digital coordination layer. Real-world delivery by capable operators.

KRIM is designed to organise the customer journey and connect it with logistics providers. Physical services are performed by the relevant providers under agreed scope and responsibilities.

01 · DEMAND

Customer

Shares origin, destination, cargo details, timing and service needs.

02 · COORDINATION

KRIM platform

Structures requests, coordinates suitable options, records decisions and supports communication.

03 · CAPABILITY

Logistics partner

Confirms service scope, capacity, terms and the work it is qualified to perform.

04 · EXECUTION

Shipment journey

Operational milestones, exceptions and delivery evidence are captured as supported by the workflow.

A platform workflow does not itself guarantee transport capacity, a price, a transit time or delivery. Those commitments require confirmation from the responsible provider for the specific shipment.

The KRIM operating community

Nine user types. One coordinated operating model.

A shipment can cross several teams and independent businesses. KRIM is designed to give each participant a defined workspace and the information needed for their authorised part of the journey. Access must be enforced by identity, organisation membership and server-side permissions.

Customer / Shipper

Creates shipment requests, reviews quotes, manages documents and follows shipment progress.

Logistics Partner / Carrier

Manages its organisation profile, service capability, opportunities, quotes and execution responsibilities.

Driver

Receives assigned movement tasks, records milestones and submits delivery or exception evidence.

Warehouse Operator

Coordinates facility receiving, handling, inventory, dispatch and custody events.

Customs & Trade

Handles authorised trade documentation, declarations and jurisdiction-specific coordination.

Finance

Reviews charges, payments, refunds, partner settlements and reconciliation records.

Operations Control Tower

Monitors shipment milestones, hand-offs, exceptions and escalation ownership.

Enterprise

Uses organisation-level shipment visibility, workflows and agreed reporting controls.

Platform Administrator

Private, restricted governance of platform configuration, security and access. This role has no public sign-in or navigation link.

How KRIM creates value

Technology coordinates. Qualified providers perform the physical service.

KRIM's intended value is to organise fragmented logistics workflows into a more coherent digital experience. The service provider responsible for each movement must be identified and confirmed for the specific scope.

01 / DISCOVER

Structure the requirement

Capture shipment needs consistently so customers and providers can understand the cargo, locations, timing and service scope.

02 / COORDINATE

Connect suitable capability

Present or coordinate provider options only where the relevant capability, availability and commercial terms can be confirmed.

03 / ACCOUNT

Keep responsibilities visible

Make clear who is responsible for each service leg, hand-off, customer communication and agreed operational commitment.

KRIM does not claim to own or operate every transport asset.

Transport, warehousing, forwarding and specialist execution may be performed by independent third-party providers under their applicable authority and agreed responsibilities. The precise legal and commercial role for a transaction must be clear in its terms and documentation.

India-first · Global-ready

Build locally. Expand when the operating chain is ready.

KRIM's initial commercial focus is India. The platform is being structured with international expansion in mind, but global architecture is not a claim of live global coverage.

Initial commercial focus

India

Develop domestic customer demand and a dependable partner network, then validate specific services and routes before accepting operational commitments.

Availability is confirmed shipment by shipment. Not every location, mode or route is automatically active.
Partner-led expansion

International markets

Expand through capable local providers, trade-lane validation, country-specific requirements and workable customer support and payment arrangements.

A market moves from design to live service only after its operational and commercial readiness has been checked.

KRIM distinguishes four readiness states: Designed → Configured → Partner-validated → Operationally active. Only confirmed active services should be presented as bookable.

Our trust principles

Trust is earned in the details.

We aim to make reliability a practical operating discipline: clear information, careful partner onboarding, responsible data handling and honest communication about what is — and is not — ready.

Our public promise

We will not describe a designed capability as an active service, an unconfirmed rate as a quote, or an unvalidated route as bookable. Specific commitments must be checked before they are made.

Clear commitments

Explain service scope, charges, assumptions, responsibilities and known limitations before confirmation.

Partner diligence

Check identity, relevant credentials, capability, service area and commercial terms appropriate to the service.

Privacy and access

Handle customer and partner information carefully, with access limited according to role and business need.

Operational records

Maintain useful records of requests, decisions, status updates and hand-offs where the platform workflow supports them.

Responsible growth

Activate markets and services only as provider, compliance, financial and support requirements are addressed.

Continuous learning

Use customer feedback and real operating outcomes to identify gaps and improve the experience.
