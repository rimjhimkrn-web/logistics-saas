About KRIM Logistics

A better way to connect the people and processes behind freight.

KRIM is building a Global Logistics Operating System: one connected digital layer for customers, logistics partners and the workflows that move goods across supply chains.

Our commercial starting point is India. Our architecture is designed to support expansion across markets as local partners, regulations, service capability and routes are validated.

Why KRIM exists

Logistics should feel connected, not fragmented.

Moving freight can involve many separate conversations, documents, service providers and hand-offs. KRIM’s goal is to make those steps easier to discover, coordinate and understand through a shared operating system — while keeping real-world execution with qualified providers and accountable teams.

01 / CLARITY

Clear decisions

Make service scope, quote assumptions, shipment milestones and responsibilities easier to understand.

02 / CONNECTION

Connected workflows

Bring customers, partners and operational teams into a coordinated process instead of isolated tools.

03 / ACCOUNTABILITY

Trust through evidence

Build confidence through partner checks, traceable updates and transparent communication — not unsupported promises.

How the model works

A digital core, connected to real-world operators.

KRIM is designed to coordinate the logistics journey while specialist providers perform the services they are qualified and contracted to deliver.

01

Customers

Share shipment needs, receive suitable options and follow progress.

02

KRIM platform

Organise requests, quotes, workflows, records and communication.

03

Verified partners

Provide transport, forwarding, warehousing and other agreed services.

04

Operational delivery

Milestones and proof are recorded as the shipment progresses.

Partner availability, route coverage, pricing, transit times and service commitments are confirmed case by case. A market being part of our expansion design does not by itself mean that service is currently available there.

Our principles

Build trust into the way work gets done.

We want the system to make good logistics decisions easier — with practical safeguards and a clear distinction between platform capability and live service readiness.

Security and privacy

Protect customer and partner information with access controls, secure handling and ongoing review of the platform’s security posture.

Operational transparency

Show meaningful shipment status and retain clear records of quotes, decisions and hand-offs where the workflow supports them.

Responsible expansion

Activate new services and markets only as partner capability, legal requirements, commercial terms and customer support are confirmed.

Continuous improvement

Learn from customer and partner feedback, real shipment outcomes and measurable service performance.
