Inclusive access

Access should be for everyone.

KRIM Logistics is working to make its digital services clear, usable and accessible across devices, abilities and assistive technologies. This statement explains our approach, current verification status and how to tell us about an accessibility barrier.

Digital accessibilityUpdated 4 November 2026
Honest status: KRIM is working towards WCAG 2.2 Level AA as a design and development target. This is not a claim of certification or full conformance. Comprehensive manual testing with assistive technologies has not yet been completed across every page and user journey.

Accessibility is an ongoing product responsibility, not a one-time checklist. We aim to reduce barriers so customers, partners and other visitors can understand information, navigate workflows and request support without unnecessary difficulty.

1. Our commitment

KRIM Logistics aims to design and maintain digital experiences that are perceivable, operable, understandable and robust. We recognise that people may access our services using keyboards, screen readers, magnification, voice control, captions, switch devices and other assistive technologies.

We aim to consider accessibility from the start of design and development, include it in quality checks and address reported barriers in a practical and prioritised way.

2. Scope

This statement applies to the KRIM public website and, as they are developed and made available, relevant digital platform journeys such as account access, quote requests, shipment information and partner workflows.

Some linked services, embedded content or payment and identity flows may be operated by third parties. Their accessibility may be governed by their own products and statements; KRIM will seek to identify barriers and provide an appropriate support route where possible.

3. Accessibility target

Our development target is the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA, published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C), alongside applicable Indian accessibility requirements and other laws relevant to the service and user.

Referencing WCAG 2.2 AA describes our target; it does not mean that an independent audit has confirmed conformance. Applicable requirements can differ by product, content type and jurisdiction.

What this means: We aim for text alternatives for meaningful images, sufficient contrast, keyboard operation, visible focus, understandable labels and errors, responsive reflow, and compatibility with common assistive technologies. Each item must still be tested in the actual experience.

4. Keyboard access

We aim to make core navigation and interactive controls usable without a mouse. This includes logical tab order, visible keyboard focus, meaningful link and button names, a way to bypass repeated navigation and predictable focus behaviour in menus and dialogs.

Where a page includes a menu or other expandable control, it should expose its state to assistive technology and support expected keyboard interaction. A feature that appears visually usable is not considered complete until its keyboard behaviour has been checked.

5. Screen readers and assistive technologies

We aim to use semantic headings, landmarks, lists, form labels and status messages so that content structure and actions can be understood programmatically. Images that convey information should have appropriate text alternatives; decorative images should not create unnecessary announcements.

We intend to test key journeys with representative screen readers and browser combinations. Support can vary by operating system, browser, assistive technology version and third-party component.

6. Visual clarity and responsive design

We aim to provide readable text, adequate colour contrast, clear focus indicators, layouts that adapt to different screen sizes and content that remains usable when text is enlarged or spacing is adjusted. Colour should not be the only way important information is communicated.

Some complex tables, maps, dashboards or dense operational views may need additional design work to remain understandable at high zoom or on narrow screens. We will prioritise core tasks and provide alternative assistance where practical.

7. Motion, flashing content and media

We aim to respect reduced-motion preferences and avoid unnecessary animation, flashing or time limits. Where motion is not essential, it should be possible to reduce or avoid it. Audio or video content published by KRIM should include appropriate alternatives such as captions or transcripts where needed.

8. Documents and third-party content

Documents, maps, embedded tools, payment interfaces and content supplied by external providers may not be fully controlled by KRIM. We will seek accessible alternatives or assistance routes for important information and transactions, and will raise material barriers with providers where possible.

If you cannot access a document or complete a task because of an accessibility barrier, contact us and tell us what information or alternative format would help. Do not send passwords, one-time passcodes or payment credentials.

9. Known limitations and verification status

KRIM's digital platform is evolving. At the date of this statement, we do not claim that every page, form, dashboard, document, mobile viewport or third-party workflow has been independently audited or verified against WCAG 2.2 AA.

  • Some legacy pages or dynamically generated content may have inconsistent heading structure, labels, focus behaviour or contrast.
  • Some third-party integrations may introduce controls or content that KRIM cannot directly modify.
  • Manual testing with screen readers, voice control, magnification and other assistive technologies remains necessary across representative journeys.
  • Accessibility of future features must be checked before those features are represented as fully accessible.

These are areas for testing and improvement, not a statement that every listed issue is present on every page.

10. Feedback and assistance

If you encounter a barrier, please use the KRIM Contact page. Include the page or feature, what you were trying to do, the barrier encountered, your device/browser if known, and the format or assistance that would help.

You do not need to disclose a disability or provide medical information to report an accessibility issue. Please do not include passwords, OTPs, card PINs or other sensitive credentials.

Need help with a task? Tell us which information or action you cannot access. Where reasonably possible, we will try to provide the information in an alternative format or help you complete the task through an accessible channel.

11. Response and escalation

KRIM will review accessibility feedback and aim to acknowledge and assess it within a reasonable period. The time needed to resolve a barrier depends on its severity, complexity, third-party involvement and available workaround. Urgent barriers affecting account security, payments or an essential customer task should be identified as such in the message.

If a concern is not resolved, you may ask for it to be reviewed again through the contact channel. Any statutory complaint or escalation rights available under applicable law remain unaffected.

12. Testing, improvement and updates

We aim to combine automated checks with manual keyboard, zoom, contrast and assistive-technology testing. Testing should cover key tasks—not only the homepage—including navigation, forms, sign-in, quote requests, tracking, payment hand-offs and partner workflows as those features become available.

We will review this statement when material changes are made to the platform, accessibility target, known limitations or support process. The updated date will be shown near the top of the page.

Ongoing work: Accessibility improvements should be recorded, prioritised by user impact, assigned to an owner and re-tested after implementation. This statement is informational and does not replace a formal audit or legal advice.

Tell us about an access barrier

We welcome clear, practical feedback and will use it to improve the KRIM experience. You can contact us without sharing sensitive personal or account credentials.
