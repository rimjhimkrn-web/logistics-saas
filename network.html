PARTNER STATUS & ACTIVATION

Every application moves through visible, distinct stages.

A profile should never appear operational merely because an account or application exists.

01 · Submitted

Application received. Details are pending review; no approval or shipment allocation is implied.

02 · Under review

Identity, credentials, service capability and relevant risk information are being checked.

03 · Clarification required

Missing, expired or inconsistent information must be resolved before a decision.

04 · Approved for defined scope

Approval applies only to the services, geography and conditions specifically reviewed and agreed.

05 · Operationally activated

Commercial terms, contacts, capacity and workflow readiness are confirmed for the relevant lane or service.

06 · Paused or under reassessment

Coverage may be suspended or reassessed when documents expire, capacity changes or service issues arise.

PARTNER QUESTIONS

Before you apply.

Does applying guarantee shipment opportunities?

No. Opportunities depend on demand, geography, capability, route fit and agreement.

Do I need to operate across all of India?

No. State your actual service areas. Focused regional coverage can be assessed.

Can an individual transporter apply?

Describe the legal business arrangement accurately, including vehicles and permits.

Which documents are required?

Requirements vary. Prepare business identity, registrations, licences, insurance and vehicle or facility details as relevant.

When does my profile become verified?

Only after review. Submitting a form does not itself mean verified or approved.

Can providers outside India apply?

International applications may be considered as local coverage and compliance capabilities develop. This does not mean a country or lane is active.
