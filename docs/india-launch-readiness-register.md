## Operating scope assumed for initial review

KRIM Logistics is presently intended to operate as an India-based sole proprietorship offering partner discovery, marketing, lead qualification and referral/coordination. The initial model proposes that the customer contracts with and pays the independent logistics provider directly; KRIM does not take custody of freight, issue the carrier's invoice, operate vehicles/warehouses, act as a customs broker, insure cargo, or collect/settle freight funds.

This is an assumption to be tested against the actual product screens, contracts, communications and money flows. Labels such as “referral platform” do not determine legal classification.

## Important legal interpretation boundaries

1. **GST is unresolved.** Do not activate GST-sensitive marketplace transactions, agency supplies, freight collection, TCS or section 9(5) workflows based on this register alone. A written CA opinion must cite the current amended law and the exact KRIM contracts and screens.
2. **Consumer e-commerce status is unresolved.** The E-Commerce Rules expressly address digital/electronic networks and marketplace models. The entity-form requirement in Rule 4(1)(a) is a material issue for a proprietorship. Obtain Indian counsel's written view before marketplace ordering/booking.
3. **Common-carrier status is unresolved.** The Carriage by Road Act applies to persons carrying on the business of a common carrier. KRIM must not accept carriage responsibility, issue goods receipts, contract as carrier or otherwise perform regulated activities without advice and required registration.
4. **Partner obligations are separate.** A partner's transport permits, vehicle fitness, driver licence, insurance, common-carrier registration, warehouse permissions, customs broker licence or mode-specific safety evidence must be checked according to the actual service and route.
5. **Customs and IEC are conditional.** Do not represent KRIM as customs broker, importer/exporter, or declarant. Refer those services to appropriately authorised parties unless KRIM's own role is separately cleared.
6. **Privacy compliance is phased.** The DPDP Rules have staggered commencement. Do not state that every provision is already in force; maintain a dated implementation schedule and check the Gazette for amendments/corrigenda.
7. **Local permissions depend on facts.** Confirm the business premises, employees, contractors and activities before deciding which Jharkhand/Ranchi registrations apply.

- PAN and proprietor identity proof (store securely; never commit to Git).
- Udyam certificate / acknowledgement.
- Proof of business address and right to use premises (ownership/rent/consent as applicable).
- Business bank account details and cancelled cheque/bank proof (never commit account numbers).
- Current GST application/certificate status and any portal notices.
- Any local registrations already held.

### Written CA opinion (must address each point)
- Actual supply by KRIM versus partner; principal/agent/ECO classification.
- Section 22 threshold, sections 23/24, section 52 TCS and section 9(5), current notifications and inter-State consequences.
- Whether KRIM's own partner marketing/referral fee is a taxable supply and registration/invoicing/return consequences.
- Whether any proposed commission or partner subscription changes the conclusion.
- Exact point at which KRIM must register before enabling a feature.

### Written Indian counsel opinion
- Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules applicability and Rule 4(1)(a) entity-form issue for proprietorship.
- Carriage by Road Act/common-carrier/booking-agent/forwarder boundaries.
- Customer/partner contract structure and liability disclosures.
- Jharkhand/Ranchi local establishment permissions.
- Privacy and consumer grievance obligations.

## Launch gating decision

Until the above legal opinions and required registrations are obtained, the system may be prepared for controlled partner registration, verification, non-binding enquiries and internal testing only where those activities themselves are cleared. Do not enable customer booking, order acceptance, freight collection, regulated transport execution, or fee collection until the relevant legal/tax gate is approved.
