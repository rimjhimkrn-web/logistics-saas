# KRIM Partner Onboarding, Verification and Capability Lifecycle Audit — 2026-11-16

## Scope and method
Read-only review of the public partner onboarding page, browser-side session flow, live Supabase function definitions, relevant RLS/storage policies, and current record counts. No partner records were changed, no documents were uploaded, no approval RPCs were invoked, and no production schema/function was modified.

## Current live counts
- Partner verification cases: 1
- Partner compliance reviews: 0
- Partner capabilities: 1

These counts do not establish that a case is approved, a capability is verified, or any partner is bookable.

## Lifecycle reviewed

### 1. Partner entry and onboarding
The page requires an authenticated session and active organization membership. It resolves exactly one active partner organization and pauses if multiple partner organizations exist, reducing the risk of attaching documents to the wrong entity.

Three pathways exist:
- Registered business: submits legal identity, registration/authority reference, registered address, operating country, service categories and coverage, and private registration evidence; insurance evidence is optional at initial submission.
- Individual interest: saves an interest profile only; the UI and RPC return commercial_access=false.
- Development/test: sample/test mode only; commercial_access=false.

The krim_set_partner_account_mode RPC is SECURITY DEFINER with an empty search_path and checks auth.uid(), active partner membership and active organization. It prevents already approved/verified organizations from switching to non-commercial modes.

### 2. Registration submission and case creation
The multi-category submission RPC delegates core identity submission to krim_submit_partner_onboarding_global_v2. The core RPC:
- Validates required business fields and country/category inputs.
- Requires 1–2 document paths; verifies paths are scoped to the organization and submitting user and that each object exists in the private krim-partner-verification bucket.
- Requires active membership in the matching active partner organization and registered-business mode.
- Sets organization verification and compliance statuses to pending.
- Creates or updates an open verification case and writes an audit event.

The admin verification approval RPC checks that submitted documents still exist in private storage and are owned by the original submitter. It requires AAL2 and admin.platform. A rejection requires a reason. Approval changes identity verification to verified but deliberately leaves compliance pending; it is not commercial activation.

### 3. Compliance review
The compliance review RPC requires AAL2, platform-admin permission, a 20–2000 character reviewer rationale, exactly four distinct baseline checks, and a future validity date (no more than 365 days) for approval. The four checks are business registration, tax registration, operating authority, and insurance liability. A verified check must reference a current uploaded evidence record whose owner, MIME type, file size and path match the stored object. A not-applicable check needs a reason of at least 10 characters. Pending/rejected checks prevent approval.

This is a meaningful baseline gate, but not a complete jurisdiction- and service-specific compliance matrix. The four fixed codes do not themselves prove that every locally required permit, carrier/forwarder authority, customs licence, cargo-class approval, safety control, or insurance scope has been identified. Country operating packs and corridor controls remain independently required.

### 4. Service capability submission and independent review
The multi-category onboarding RPC creates capability rows with active=false and verification_status=pending. However, those rows are created with operational category and coverage metadata but do not populate submitted_by and evidence_paths metadata required by krim_admin_review_partner_capability.

The capability review RPC requires AAL2 and admin.platform; pending capability status; a reviewer different from the capability submitter; a current verified/compliant registered-business organization; active country and transport mode; one to five evidence paths present in the private bucket and owned by the original submitter; and an approval note of at least 10 characters. Hazardous-goods and refrigerated/cold-chain capabilities are blocked from standard approval pending specialized controls. Effective dates and maximum weight are validated.

**Important workflow gap:** capability rows generated by multi-category onboarding do not appear to carry the submitter/evidence metadata required by the capability approval RPC. A partner may need to separately resubmit each capability using the dedicated capability-submission workflow before an administrator can approve it. The page does not clearly explain this next step. Reconcile before treating onboarding as complete.

### 5. Private document access
The private bucket has an own-organization read policy for active partner members and a separate AAL2 platform-admin read policy. The initial onboarding upload path is organization_id/user_id/random-file.ext, matching the scoped organization read policy. This audit did not test signed URL delivery or every upload/delete policy. No document contents were accessed.

## Safety conclusions
- Application submission is not identity approval.
- Identity approval is not compliance clearance.
- Compliance clearance is not capability approval.
- Capability approval is not route/corridor authorization or shipment assignment.
- Development and individual-interest modes do not receive commercial access.
- The page's 'eligible subject to approved service capabilities' language is directionally correct, but a complete eligibility decision must also respect market activation, country operating packs, corridor dossiers/controls, shipment-specific compliance and customs gates.
- Do not use organization status labels alone as proof of bookability. Live capability, assignment and corridor gates remain authoritative.
- No partner is certified or bookable merely because one verification case and one capability row exist.

## Priority corrections
1. **P1 — Resolve onboarding-to-capability handoff.** Either populate submitted_by and appropriate evidence metadata on onboarding-created capability rows using an evidence-backed submission contract, or explicitly direct partners to the separate capability evidence submission step. Preserve independent reviewer approval.
2. **P1 — Make compliance checks jurisdiction/service aware.** Keep the four baseline checks, but require applicable country, service category, cargo class and operating-permit controls to be enumerated from approved country operating packs before activation.
3. **P1 — Improve partner status UI.** Display identity, compliance, each capability, and market/corridor activation as separate gates; avoid a single generic 'eligible' state as operational approval.
4. **P2 — Verify complete storage lifecycle.** Test upload, partner own-org read, unauthorized cross-org read denial, admin AAL2 read, orphan cleanup, signed evidence access, and retention/deletion restrictions in staging.
5. **P2 — Test independent review and expiry.** Verify submitter/reviewer separation, expired compliance review, rejection/resubmission, capability renewal, revoked/suspended partner status, and prevention of new assignments after any gate expires.

## Explicitly not done
No live partner verification, compliance or capability decision was made. No production DDL, RLS, storage policy, function, or partner record was changed. No real-world service capability, insurance, route coverage, or legal authorization was inferred from submitted form fields.
