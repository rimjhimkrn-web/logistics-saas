# KRIM India First-Market Activation and Partner Acquisition Operating Plan — 2026-11-16

## Executive recommendation

**Recommended pilot candidate: domestic ROAD full-truckload (FTL), Ranchi ↔ Jamshedpur, Jharkhand.** This is a candidate for validation, not an approved KRIM corridor and not a claim that KRIM currently has a carrier, capacity or customer demand.

Why this candidate:
- It starts in KRIM's first intended market and near the current Ranchi operating base.
- Jharkhand government/DPIIT LEADS material identifies Ranchi and Jamshedpur as industrial hubs and reports rail connectivity inefficiency between them; it also identifies MSME cargo aggregation and warehousing gaps as opportunities.
- A road-freight operator's published FY2025–26 lane data reports 135 Jamshedpur→Ranchi consignments, 632 tonnes, 12/12 active months and 42 hired trucks over Sep 2025–Aug 2026. This is one operator's self-reported register, not an independent national dataset and not a KRIM supply commitment.
- A June 2026 public report records central approval of ₹214.79 crore for overlay work on the 126.497 km Ranchi–Mahulia road section. This is infrastructure context, not proof of current road conditions or a transit guarantee.

The first pilot should be deliberately narrow: **non-hazardous, non-perishable, general commercial cargo; road FTL; one origin/destination pair; one named carrier per booking; no cross-border customs, no warehouse handling, no cold chain, no dangerous goods, no oversized/over-dimensional cargo, and no promised ETA until a carrier confirms it in writing.** Customer contracts with and pays the carrier directly. KRIM only earns a separately agreed service/referral fee if and when its legal, tax and commercial setup is approved.

## Evidence and limitations

1. DPIIT, LEADS 2024, Jharkhand section: identifies Ranchi–Jamshedpur rail inefficiency, MSME aggregation constraints and limited warehousing. Government stakeholder consultation, not a lane demand forecast.
2. Jharkhand Government public report, 15 June 2026: ₹214.79 crore approved for overlay on the 126.497 km Ranchi–Mahulia (Tata) road section. Funding approval does not establish completion, work timing or actual transit conditions.
3. Safe & Secure Logistics (SSL) published FY2025–26 lane data: 135 Jamshedpur→Ranchi consignments, 632 tonnes, 12/12 months active, 100% FTL, approx. 129 km, and 42 hired trucks Sep 2025–Aug 2026. This is a single operator's self-reported operating data. Its published median booking-to-delivery time is 13 days, with middle half 8–23 days; do not use this as a KRIM ETA or service benchmark without clarification of its measurement method.
4. Public business directory listings in Ranchi show multiple potential transport providers and national network offices. Listings/reviews do not verify legal identity, fleet ownership, insurance, corridor coverage, operational quality or willingness to contract with KRIM.

## Partner acquisition funnel — exact stages and evidence

### Stage 1 — Prospect identified (not approved)
Capture only public business contact details and prospect source. No implication of partnership. Record:
- legal/trade name and business address;
- public website/office contact and source/date;
- business type (carrier, fleet owner, transporter, broker);
- stated service modes and lane claims;
- contact permission and communication history.

### Stage 2 — Identity and legal entity verification
Obtain and independently cross-check, as applicable:
- PAN and legal constitution / proprietorship declaration;
- GST registration status and GSTIN verification where registered/applicable;
- Udyam certificate if claimed;
- CIN/LLPIN and MCA status for companies/LLPs;
- registered/principal business address;
- authorized signatory identity and authority;
- bank account proof matching the contracting legal entity;
- litigation/insolvency/adverse media screening proportionate to risk.

Never treat a GST certificate, Udyam certificate, directory listing or phone conversation alone as proof of operational fitness.

### Stage 3 — Transport authority and fleet verification
For each vehicle offered:
- registration certificate and registered owner;
- valid fitness certificate where required;
- applicable national/state permit and route permissions;
- motor third-party insurance and comprehensive cover where claimed;
- pollution-under-control certificate where applicable;
- vehicle class, payload/GVW and body type;
- ownership, lease or written fleet-owner authorization;
- inspection date, expiry dates and verification source.

For each driver:
- identity and consent/notice;
- valid driving licence, correct class and validity;
- employment/engagement or carrier authorization;
- driver training and safety process appropriate to cargo;
- emergency contact and incident reporting process.

### Stage 4 — Cargo, insurance and service capability
Require:
- explicit accepted cargo categories and exclusions;
- vehicle/equipment compatibility and payload limits;
- goods-in-transit / carrier liability insurance wording, insured party, limits, exclusions, territory, validity and claims process;
- loading/unloading responsibility and equipment;
- packaging, seal, weighment and shortage/damage protocol;
- proof-of-pickup and proof-of-delivery format;
- incident escalation and customer support contacts;
- subcontracting policy and approval controls;
- data-protection and driver-location handling terms.

A general motor policy is not automatically goods-in-transit cargo cover. Verify the actual policy wording and exclusions.

### Stage 5 — Lane and capacity proof
For Ranchi↔Jamshedpur specifically:
- actual pickup/delivery pin codes and service radius;
- recent completed comparable loads (redacted consignment proof, dates, vehicle class and lane);
- frequency and seasonal capacity;
- owned vs attached/hired vehicle ratio;
- available vehicle classes and payload;
- dispatch lead time, operating days, cut-off and loading windows;
- return-load availability, if claimed;
- route restrictions, tolls, weighbridges and known disruption plan;
- named dispatch supervisor and escalation contact;
- written confirmation of capacity for a specific future load, not a generic fleet count.

### Stage 6 — Commercial and contract approval
Before activation:
- signed carrier service agreement with scope, rates/quote process, cancellation, waiting/detention, loading/unloading, taxes, insurance, loss/damage, claims, subcontracting, data protection, audit, termination and dispute provisions;
- customer-facing terms make carrier identity and responsibility clear;
- written customer–carrier contract or order accepted by both sides;
- KRIM's separate referral/coordination fee terms, if any, with correct tax treatment confirmed by qualified CA/legal adviser;
- explicit no-custody/no-freight-settlement boundary;
- approved operating corridor and cargo scope.

### Stage 7 — Controlled activation
Only after independent review:
- partner organization verified;
- capability approved for ROAD FTL / general cargo;
- exact Ranchi/Jamshedpur service areas approved;
- current vehicle/driver evidence verified;
- corridor/market controls approved;
- capacity availability recorded and independently confirmed;
- partner status and public discovery state accurately updated;
- one named KRIM operations owner assigned.

A verified organization is not automatically a verified capability, service area, vehicle, driver, capacity commitment or shipment assignment. These are separate gates.

## Suggested acquisition targets (planning targets, not existing commitments)

- Identify 8–12 prospects from official company websites, government/company records, industry directories and local transport associations.
- Complete first-pass desk checks on at least 5.
- Seek full evidence from 3 serious candidates.
- Independently approve at least 1 carrier and retain 1 alternate only if both meet the same standard.
- Do not activate a partner merely to reach a numeric target.
- Do not represent any prospect as a KRIM partner until contract and verification are complete.

Potential discovery examples surfaced in public listings include established logistics networks and local road transport firms in Ranchi. These are **unverified prospects only**, not endorsements or recommendations, and must not be imported as approved partner records without consent and verification.

## First pilot acceptance gates

Before accepting a real customer booking, all must be green:
1. Migration/schema reconciliation complete; no unresolved production DDL uncertainty.
2. Shipment mode and opportunity creation fixed and tested atomically/idempotently in staging.
3. Market/corridor gate enforces approved operating scope server-side.
4. Compliance evaluator fails closed when checks are absent or incomplete.
5. Exact partner quote and customer award are linked; partner separately accepts.
6. Capacity is current, exact-lane, exact-date, exact-vehicle-class and atomically reserved.
7. Driver, vehicle, insurance and permit evidence are current and verified.
8. Dispatch single-leg/multi-leg guard defect resolved and staging-tested.
9. Direct pickup/delivery custody events and milestone order are reliable.
10. POD upload, metadata and independent review are recoverable and tenant-safe.
11. Exception, claims and incident owner/communications process exists.
12. Backup, restore, operator escalation and outage runbooks are usable.
13. Customer–carrier contracting and direct freight payment boundary are clear.
14. CA/legal review confirms KRIM fee model, GST position and relevant consumer/transport obligations.
15. One named operations controller manually monitors the load from request through verified delivery.

## Release decision

**NO-GO for activating Ranchi–Jamshedpur or soliciting live bookings through KRIM today.** The lane is a reasonable candidate for due diligence, not a currently bookable service. KRIM should focus first on one verified road FTL carrier, exact service scope and a fully tested domestic single-lane workflow. This is a safer, lower-cost path than trying to activate many modes, warehouses, customs and countries simultaneously.

No partner was contacted, no customer lead was created, no partner status changed, and no production capability, service area, route, capacity or booking was activated.
