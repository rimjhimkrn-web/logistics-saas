## Suggested first outreach sequence

1. Start with Kiran Group and SafeShift for regional transport/distribution coverage; approach Rapiditors separately for warehousing.
2. Contact only publicly published business channels. Ask for a short introductory meeting; do not imply an existing KRIM relationship.
3. Explain KRIM as a technology-led introduction and workflow platform. The partner remains the actual service provider and contracts with and collects freight charges directly from the customer.
4. Discuss a separately agreed KRIM referral fee only after the partner confirms its interest and KRIM has obtained CA/legal advice on GST, intermediary and invoicing treatment.
5. Do not request sensitive documents over informal messaging. Use a secure, access-controlled process for registration, permits, insurance and authorised signatory evidence.
6. Record outreach date, named business contact, response, exact legal entity, verified scope, agreed lane, contract status, evidence review and next action in the CRM.

## Minimum verification before any partner can be represented as active

- Legal name, CIN/LLPIN or proprietorship evidence, current registration status and registered address.
- GST registration/tax treatment where applicable; PAN and bank beneficiary name verification through a secure process.
- Authorised signatory and written authority to sign.
- Relevant transport permits, vehicle documents, driver controls and applicable operating licences.
- Current motor/carrier liability, warehouse/fire and other relevant insurance.
- Real operating lanes, mode, service category, cut-off times, capacity and subcontractor disclosure.
- Customer references and claims/incident handling process.
- Signed KRIM partner agreement covering referral trigger, fee, payment timing, customer consent, data protection, liability, claims, dispute handling, termination and non-exclusivity.
- Human approval and evidence-backed capability activation in KRIM; no self-approval.

## Commercial guardrails

- No freight funds collected or held by KRIM.
- Customer and partner contract and pay each other directly for logistics services.
- KRIM charges only its separately contracted, earned referral/coordination fee.
- Do not call the model GST-exempt; obtain qualified Indian tax advice before invoicing.
- No partner coverage, pricing, availability or service guarantee until verified for the exact lane and date.
- Do not pay for ads or subscriptions before the first organic referral converts and the fee is collected.
