## Outreach sequence
1. Call the published business number during business hours and ask for the person responsible for operations or business development.
2. Ask permission to send a short company introduction by email or WhatsApp.
3. Send the matching message below only after permission.
4. Follow up once after 3 business days if there is no response.
5. Close the lead after the second unanswered attempt; do not repeatedly message or scrape personal contacts.
6. Record every response and next step in the KRIM partner acquisition tracker.

## 15-minute discovery agenda
1. Exact services delivered directly today (FTL, PTL, parcel, warehousing, C&F, forwarding, etc.).
2. Current origin/destination lanes and service areas.
3. Available capacity, vehicle/equipment types, cargo restrictions and lead time.
4. Owned, leased and subcontracted asset split.
5. Required licences, GST status where applicable, insurance, claims process and references.
6. Current quotation, dispatch, tracking and proof-of-delivery workflow.
7. Interest in a software walkthrough and a no-fee initial partner application.
8. Authorised decision-maker and agreed next step.

## Qualification scorecard
Score each item 0 (unknown/no evidence), 1 (partially evidenced), or 2 (verified):
- Legal identity and authorised representative
- Service capability and route evidence
- Equipment/facility evidence
- Insurance and claims process
- Operational capacity and service-level commitment
- References and performance evidence
- Data protection and willingness to use secure KRIM workflows

Do not activate a capability if any mandatory legal, safety, insurance or service evidence is missing. A high score does not replace document review or written approval.

## Non-negotiable KRIM rules
- No guaranteed leads, loads, bookings, revenue, rates or route coverage.
- No partner is active merely because an enquiry or application is received.
- No sensitive documents over personal WhatsApp; use the authenticated private KRIM upload workflow.
- Do not charge for initial application or verification review.
- No paid software plan until useful functionality, price, terms and support are clearly presented and accepted.
- Do not confirm customer bookings until an eligible provider explicitly accepts the specific job, price, capacity and terms.
- Keep partner services, KRIM software subscriptions and any marketplace commission contractually and financially distinct.
