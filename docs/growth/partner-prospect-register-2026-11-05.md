# KRIM Partner Prospect Register — India-first
**Research date:** 5 November 2026  
**Status rule:** Every organisation below is an uncontacted research lead, not a KRIM partner. Public information is preliminary and is not verification, endorsement, availability, capacity confirmation or an agreement.

## Acquisition priorities
1. Start with Eastern India organisations that can strengthen Ranchi–Kolkata, Ranchi–Patna and intra-Jharkhand coverage.
2. Prioritise established fleet/transport operators and integrated 3PL/warehouse providers; use larger pan-India networks for formal partner-channel discussions.
3. Collect real legal identity, GST/CIN, operating permits, insurance, fleet ownership/attachment, capacity, lanes, safety controls, claims history, references and bank-account ownership during due diligence.
4. Keep all outreach human-reviewed, targeted and consent-aware. No mass WhatsApp/SMS or automated solicitation.
5. Prospect status stays `discovered` until the organisation responds and submits an application. Only the authorised KRIM review workflow can record verification and capability approval.

## Outreach control
No email, phone call, WhatsApp message, web form, CRM contact, partner application, or agreement has been sent or created by this research. Before outreach, confirm KRIM's business identity/contact details and use a named business contact or official partnership channel. Record date, channel, purpose, consent/response, owner and next follow-up. Never send marketing messages to scraped personal numbers or bulk lists.

## Acceptance criteria for this pipeline
- Prospect has a verifiable legal entity and official business channel.
- The right decision-maker or authorised vendor-onboarding team is identified.
- Written interest and permission for follow-up are recorded.
- Application and required evidence are submitted.
- Organisation-level verification is approved by authorised KRIM reviewer.
- Each service capability is separately reviewed and approved.
- Only then may the organisation enter commercial evaluation; rates and activation require the Step 3 pricing and market gates.
