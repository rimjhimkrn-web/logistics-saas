## First discussion agenda (15 minutes)

1. Which exact services do you currently provide directly: FTL, PTL, parcel, warehousing, C&F, freight forwarding, customs, or another service?
2. Which origin/destination lanes are genuinely open for new business today?
3. What capacity can you commit, by vehicle/equipment type, cargo type, and time window?
4. Which assets are owned, leased, or subcontracted?
5. What documents can be independently verified: incorporation/registration, GST where applicable, operating licences, insurance, safety and claims process?
6. Would you consider a no-fee initial KRIM partner application and a software walkthrough, with no guaranteed loads and no automatic paid subscription?
7. Who is authorised to approve a pilot, data sharing, and commercial terms?

## KRIM admission and activation gates

1. Initial enquiry / discovery — not a partner approval.
2. Partner creates an account and submits the relevant application.
3. Independent legal identity, authority, compliance and document review.
4. Service capability evidence and exact route/service-area review.
5. Capacity, insurance, cargo constraints, service terms and escalation review.
6. Written commercial agreement where required.
7. Only then activate the specific capability/route in KRIM.
8. A customer job is confirmed only after a specific provider accepts it with price, capacity and terms.

Partner application and initial verification review remain free. Approval does not guarantee leads, loads, bookings, or revenue. Do not collect sensitive documents over personal messaging; use KRIM's authenticated private evidence upload workflow.
