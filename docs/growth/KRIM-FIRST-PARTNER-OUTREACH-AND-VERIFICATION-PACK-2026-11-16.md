## 4. Introductory call agenda

1. Confirm exact legal entity name and who has authority to discuss partnerships.
2. Understand service categories actually operated (not just advertised).
3. Confirm current serviceable lanes, operating locations, cut-offs and exclusions.
4. Establish owned fleet vs subcontracted capacity and subcontractor controls.
5. Discuss insurance, permits, claims and incident escalation.
6. Ask whether the organisation accepts transparent, non-exclusive customer referrals.
7. Explain that customers contract and pay the provider directly; KRIM does not handle freight funds.
8. If mutually interested, agree a secure diligence route and follow-up owner. Do not promise leads, volumes or exclusivity.

## 5. Partner diligence checklist

### Legal identity and authority
- Exact contracting legal name and constitution.
- CIN/LLPIN or applicable proprietorship/partnership registration evidence.
- Current registration status and registered/principal operating address.
- Authorised signatory name, role and authority evidence.
- PAN/GST details only through a secure, restricted diligence channel and where professionally advised as necessary.

### Operations and capability
- Exact services: full truckload, part load, parcel, courier, warehousing, distribution, C&F or other.
- Specific origin/destination lanes and service exclusions.
- Fleet ownership, hired vehicles and subcontracting split.
- Capacity confirmation process, lead time, cut-offs and seasonal limits.
- Warehouse facility, address, usable capacity, handling conditions and WMS capability where relevant.
- Driver, vehicle, safety and proof-of-delivery controls.

### Risk and service quality
- Applicable permits/licences and insurance certificates.
- Cargo liability limits, exclusions and claims process.
- Incident escalation contacts and operating hours.
- Two or more relevant business references, subject to consent.
- Data handling and customer confidentiality practices.

### Commercial fit
- Willingness to accept non-exclusive, consent-based introductions.
- Written referral fee proposal, calculation basis, precise earning trigger and payment deadline.
- Cancellation, failed delivery, claim, credit-note and dispute treatment.
- No payment for unqualified leads; no guaranteed lead volume.
- Confirm customer-to-provider contracting and freight payment remain direct.

### Independent review
- Founder must not self-approve their own diligence submission.
- Record reviewer, date, evidence references, expiry dates and exceptions.
- Verify critical claims independently; do not accept a website or brochure as sufficient evidence.
- Keep documents in restricted private storage, never in a public repository.

## 6. Pilot agreement terms to obtain professional review on

- Parties' exact legal names and authorised signatories.
- Non-exclusive referral scope and geographic/service boundaries.
- Definition of a valid referred lead and customer consent.
- Attribution window and treatment of existing customers/leads.
- Fee basis (fixed amount or percentage), calculation base, currency and taxes.
- Exact fee-earning trigger (recommend only after defined service completion and agreed evidence, unless counsel approves another trigger).
- Invoice issuer, invoice timing, payment due date and late/disputed amounts.
- Cancellations, partial completion, claims, refunds and credit notes.
- Provider remains responsible for transport/warehouse service, licences, insurance, subcontractors, customer contract and freight collection.
- KRIM's limited role, no custody of freight money, no authority to bind provider, and no unsupported service guarantees.
- Data protection, permitted sharing, confidentiality, security and retention.
- Liability allocation, indemnities, complaint escalation and dispute resolution.
- Term, termination, survival of earned fees and post-termination attribution.
- GST, TDS, invoicing and intermediary treatment as advised by an Indian CA/lawyer.

Do not sign or invoice from this outline. Have the final agreement and tax treatment reviewed professionally.
