## 3) Driver acquisition — India first
- DriversPlease is a public India transport-jobs board describing truck/bus/cab driver vacancies, stated pay and duty patterns, free driver access, and a manual operator/licence-check process. It is a potential sourcing channel, not a KRIM verification authority: https://driversplease.com/about
- Build a lawful pipeline by role and vehicle class: LCV, HCV, truck/container, delivery vehicle, specialized cargo; distinguish independent owner-drivers from employed drivers and fleet referrals.
- Required checks before eligibility: identity, valid licence/category, experience, vehicle class, insurance/fitness/permits as applicable, safety history, references, consent, and local labour/contract terms.
- Never request a joining fee/deposit from drivers; never mark a driver approved or dispatchable until authorized review is complete.
