# KRIM Official Brand Standard

Status: Approved master identity
Effective: 11 November 2026

## Official master logo
- Canonical full wordmark: `assets/krim-primary.svg`
- This file is the single source of truth for the KRIM Logistics full logo.
- Preserve the globe emblem, KRIM wordmark, fine full-width line beneath KRIM, LOGISTICS wordmark, and full-width orange line beneath LOGISTICS exactly as supplied.
- Do not redraw, crop, recolor, remove either line, change spacing, or replace the full wordmark with the emblem in normal page or app interfaces.

## Usage
- Website headers, public pages, authentication pages, dashboards, app login screens, and app splash screens use the complete master logo.
- The emblem-only asset `assets/krim-icon.svg` is reserved for constrained square placements such as favicons, launcher icons, and adaptive app icons.
- Do not use the icon-only asset as a substitute for the full logo in a normal page header or app screen.
- Maintain aspect ratio and adequate clear space. Use a white or otherwise approved high-contrast background.

## Change control
- This standard remains in force for future KRIM website and app work.
- Any change to the official master logo requires explicit approval from the KRIM owner.
- New app builds must use the approved master logo for their login and splash artwork. Existing installed app packages only receive visual asset changes after a new build and release.
