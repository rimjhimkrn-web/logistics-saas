Customer care & complaint handling

Your concern matters. We take it seriously.

A transparent route to report a shipment problem, payment issue, partner conduct concern, privacy question or other service grievance—and understand what happens next.

We aim to handle concerns fairly, respectfully and with clear records. A complaint is not treated as a confirmed finding of fault; it is an issue that KRIM will review according to the actual service role, transaction records, applicable contract and mandatory law.

1. Scope and who can complain

This process is intended for customers, prospective customers, business users, shipment recipients, authorised representatives and network partners who need to raise a concern about a KRIM website interaction or a service transaction involving KRIM.

Where a transport, warehousing or other logistics service is performed by an independent provider, the provider may also have a separate complaint process. KRIM will review the part of the issue within its actual role and route or coordinate the remaining issue where appropriate. This does not remove any party's legal responsibility.

2. Types of grievances

Shipment and delivery

Missed pickup, delay, tracking discrepancy, shortage, loss, damage or proof-of-delivery concern.

Quote and booking

Incorrect shipment details, quote dispute, booking acceptance, service scope or undisclosed charge.

Payment and refund

Duplicate debit, payment status, failed transaction, refund delay or settlement discrepancy.

Partner conduct and safety

Unprofessional conduct, suspected fraud, unsafe handling, unauthorised substitution or compliance concern.

Account and platform

Sign-in issue, inaccurate account data, access problem, security incident or platform malfunction.

Privacy and accessibility

Personal-data concern, suspected disclosure, accessibility barrier or request for an alternative format.

3. How to submit a complaint

Use the KRIM Contact page and describe the issue clearly. If it relates to an existing shipment or payment, include its reference number. The Contact page is the current general submission route; do not send confidential credentials or payment secrets.

If the online form is unavailable, retry when connectivity is restored or use another contact method expressly published by KRIM. Do not assume an unverified email address, social-media account or personal mobile number is an official grievance channel.

For urgent safety events: If a person is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or the relevant authorities first. Then notify KRIM with the shipment reference and a safe callback method. Do not wait for a website response to address an emergency.

4. Information to include

To help us investigate, provide only information relevant to the issue:

  • Your name and a working contact method, plus your relationship to the shipment or account.
  • Shipment, quote, booking, invoice or payment reference, if available.
  • Relevant dates, locations, service provider (if known) and a concise description of what happened.
  • The outcome you are requesting, such as an explanation, correction, status update, refund review or investigation.
  • Relevant supporting records, such as booking confirmation, invoice, payment reference, photographs of visible cargo damage or delivery records. Remove unrelated personal information where possible.

Never provide a password, OTP, card PIN, CVV, full card number, bank login or remote-access code. KRIM will not need those secrets to investigate a complaint.

5. Receipt and reference

After submitting through the contact form, look for the on-screen receipt and reference shown by the form. Keep that reference and any acknowledgement for follow-up. A successful enquiry receipt confirms submission only; it is not an admission of liability, approval of a refund, acceptance of a claim or confirmation that a shipment is booked.

If no receipt appears, the submission may not have completed. Check the form's response, avoid repeatedly sending sensitive attachments and retry or contact KRIM through the published Contact page.

6. Review and investigation

KRIM will assess the issue against available platform records, booking and payment information, communications, provider updates and relevant policies. Where another service provider, payment institution or third party controls material information, additional verification may be required.

We may ask for reasonable clarification or supporting documents. We will seek to distinguish confirmed facts, information supplied by a party, matters still under investigation and decisions made under the contract or applicable law. A complaint may be referred to the party legally responsible for the relevant service component, while preserving any rights you may have against KRIM.

7. Response timelines

KRIM aims to review submissions promptly and provide a meaningful update when the available facts permit. Some matters—especially cargo loss or damage, insurance, bank/payment reversals, multi-party movements and cross-border transactions—may require external evidence and additional time.

Where the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules apply to KRIM as an e-commerce entity, the grievance officer must acknowledge receipt of a consumer complaint within 48 hours and redress it within one month from receipt, as required by the applicable rules. The 2026 amendment also requires a copy of the complaint as recorded by the grievance officer and comes into force on 1 January 2027. These statutory timelines are not a claim that KRIM has already appointed or published a compliant officer.

Where a different mandatory legal or contractual deadline applies, that requirement governs. If a final decision cannot reasonably be made immediately, we aim to explain the outstanding steps and provide a status update rather than imply that the issue is resolved.

8. Shipment, cargo and delivery issues

For a delay or missed milestone, provide the latest confirmed status, expected impact and any instructions received. For visible damage, shortage or suspected tampering, preserve packaging, seals, photographs, delivery records and relevant documents. Where safe and lawful, note exceptions on the delivery receipt before signing.

Report loss, damage, shortage or other cargo claims as soon as possible. Contractual and statutory notice periods may apply and can be short. This grievance process does not extend, replace or waive a claim-notification deadline under the applicable transport law, booking terms, insurance policy or partner contract. Do not dispose of damaged goods or packaging before evidence and inspection requirements are clarified, unless safety requires it.

9. Payment and refund issues

For payment concerns, provide the order or booking reference, amount, date, payment method and transaction reference shown by your bank or payment provider, if available. Mask account numbers and other sensitive data. Never send an OTP, PIN, CVV or password.

For refund requests, see the Refund & Cancellation Policy. A gateway status such as initiated, pending, processed, failed or reversed may differ from the final credit reflected by the bank. KRIM will not describe a refund as received until the available confirmation supports that status. Refund eligibility depends on the transaction, responsible contracting/payment-collecting party and applicable law.

10. Partner conduct and safety concerns

Concerns about a carrier, driver, warehouse, subcontractor or other provider should include the shipment reference, date, location, nature of the concern and any immediate safety risk. KRIM may seek records from the provider, restrict platform access or escalate the matter to the responsible authority where justified and lawful.

Do not confront a person or enter a restricted facility to collect evidence. For suspected criminal conduct, serious accident, injury or immediate danger, contact the relevant emergency or law-enforcement authority. KRIM's internal review does not replace a statutory report or police complaint.

11. Privacy and confidentiality

Complaint information is handled under the KRIM Privacy Notice and applicable law. Access should be limited to people who need the information to assess, investigate, resolve, audit or lawfully disclose the matter. We may share relevant details with a service provider, payment institution, insurer, professional adviser or authority where necessary and lawful.

Do not include unrelated sensitive personal information. If your complaint concerns a privacy or security incident, identify the affected account or record without sending credentials or copies of identity documents unless specifically requested through a verified secure process.

12. Escalation and consumer rights

If you are dissatisfied with the response, reply through the existing contact thread or submit a follow-up through the Contact page, quoting the original reference and explaining what remains unresolved. Ask for review by a senior responsible person. This is an internal escalation route and does not restrict access to a consumer commission, regulator, court or other competent authority.

Nothing in this process waives statutory consumer rights, limits a lawful remedy or requires a consumer to complete internal escalation before exercising a right available under law.

13. National Consumer Helpline

Consumers in India may also approach the Government of India's National Consumer Helpline for pre-litigation consumer grievance support. The Department of Consumer Affairs lists the toll-free number 1915 and the official web portal below. Availability, languages and channel instructions are governed by the Government service.

14. Grievance Officer disclosure and appointment status

KRIM has not yet verified and published a named Grievance Officer, designation, dedicated contact details and formal appointment for this page. No individual is represented here as an appointed statutory officer. The general Contact page is provided for current enquiries only.

If KRIM's business model is determined to be an e-commerce entity subject to the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, it must appoint the required grievance officer and prominently display the verified name, designation and contact details, alongside the other operator disclosures. The eligibility of KRIM's current sole-proprietorship structure must be resolved before enabling marketplace-style paid transactions; see the Legal Notice.

Launch gate: Before applicable e-commerce operations go live, confirm the lawful entity structure, appoint an authorised resident grievance officer where required, publish verified contact particulars, establish complaint logging and escalation ownership, test acknowledgement and redress workflows, and assess the required National Consumer Helpline convergence partnership. Do not treat this page alone as completion of those steps.

15. Records, monitoring and updates

Where applicable and lawful, KRIM should maintain a complaint register with a unique reference, receipt date, category, responsible owner, acknowledgement date, action history, evidence, outcome and closure date. Access and retention should follow the Privacy Notice, legal obligations and a documented retention schedule.

KRIM may update this process when services, contact channels, laws or operating arrangements change. Material changes should be reflected in the revision date and related customer-facing policies.
