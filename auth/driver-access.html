I already have a KRIM driver account

Sign in securely
02

I need access from my organization

Ask your transport-company or organization administrator to provision a driver account and assign the correct organization membership. Self-selecting “Driver” does not grant access.

03

I am testing KRIM as its builder

Use a dedicated test driver identity linked to a test organization and driver membership. Do not use a real driver’s credentials or invent licence details. Live assignments remain unavailable until authorized.

Security and honesty: KRIM does not offer unrestricted public driver registration because drivers must be linked to an authorized organization. This page does not create an account or grant permissions. If sign-in reports missing membership, the account must be provisioned by an authorized administrator before the Driver Workspace can open.
