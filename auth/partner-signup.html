PARTNER ACCESS · 01 / ACCOUNT

Create your partner account

Create an individual login first. Legal entity, operating authority, service scope and country evidence are collected separately and reviewed before activation.

If registered, enter the exact legal business name. If applying as an individual, enter your own name. You will provide the detailed legal and service information during onboarding.

Your choice does not grant commercial approval. Verification and operational permissions remain separate. If your country is not listed, contact KRIM before selecting a different country; do not choose an inaccurate country.

Use at least 10 characters and never reuse another service's password.

Registration creates an account identity only. It does not verify your business, activate a service, guarantee assignments or enable payments. After email confirmation and authenticator setup, continue to Onboarding & Verification. KRIM will never ask you to share your password or OTP by email.
