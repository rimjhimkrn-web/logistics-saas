Each commercial and operational step remains controlled by the organization’s current status and the permissions applicable to that action.

Before committing to work

Confirm each of the following independently; a dashboard view or profile status is not itself an assignment.

  • ✓Active organization membership and appropriate user permission.
  • ✓Current organization verification and compliance standing.
  • ✓Exact service capability and any required jurisdictional authorization.
  • ✓Confirmed route, capacity, resources, service provider and operating terms.
  • ✓Valid assignment authority and shipment-specific instructions.
  • ✓Evidence-backed milestones, exceptions and delivery closeout.
KRIM platform and partner responsibility

KRIM is the technology platform connecting customers with logistics partners. Partners provide the actual logistics services and remain responsible for their own licences, personnel, equipment, insurance, cargo custody and legal obligations. A listed capability, indicative quote or partner profile does not establish confirmed availability. Customer and partner freight contracts and payments must follow the agreed commercial model; KRIM does not claim to be the carrier or service provider.
