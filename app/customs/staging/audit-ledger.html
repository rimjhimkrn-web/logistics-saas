Audit governance controls

Design the ledger so privileged users cannot silently rewrite history.

Event capture & integrity lifecycle

Every event should be attributable, time-bound and independently verifiable.

1
Capture at the source

Record authenticated actor/service identity, organization, role, action, target record, timestamp and correlation reference at the time the action occurs.

2
Preserve before-and-after context

Retain permitted change metadata, prior and new version references, reason, source provenance and relevant authorization decision.

3
Protect append-only history

Prevent ordinary application roles from updating or deleting historical events. Use restricted service roles, integrity controls and monitored administrative access.

4
Correlate independent evidence

Link internal workflow events to separately stored official acknowledgements, declarations, permits and authority messages without conflating their origin.

5
Review, export & retain

Provide scoped, logged audit access; approved exports; retention schedules; legal holds; and documented archival or disposal approvals.

Audit exception states

Exceptions remain visible until independently resolved.

A
Integrity check pending

Event chain or source evidence has not yet been verified.

B
Evidence mismatch

Hash, version, source or linked record does not reconcile.

C
Access anomaly

Unexpected, denied or privileged access requires review.

D
Control breach / investigation

Potential unauthorized change or missing event requires escalation.

E
Resolved with approval

Resolution, reviewer, rationale and evidence are retained; original events remain unchanged.

Audit integrity and legal record boundaryAn internal KRIM event ledger is not a customs authority system of record, government receipt, legal certification or proof of release. Official messages and source documents must retain their origin, authenticity evidence and exact scope. Historical events should not be silently edited or deleted; corrections should be new linked events with reasons and approvals. Restrict sensitive evidence by organization, role and purpose; log access and exports; apply lawful retention and legal-hold requirements. Do not expose personal or commercially sensitive information in broad audit views.
