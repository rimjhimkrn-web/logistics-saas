Goods admission & inventory control

Track goods by lot, status and location without assuming customs release.

Admission lifecycle

Admission requires the relevant authority and facility evidence.

1
Pre-arrival & eligibility

Confirm the designated zone, eligible goods, responsible importer/exporter, declared purpose and applicable admission procedure.

2
Declaration & authority evidence

Link applicable customs declaration, transport documents, permits, inspection results and authority acknowledgement or approval.

3
Physical receipt & reconciliation

Record received quantities, condition, package/lot identifiers, discrepancies and authorized facility receipt evidence.

4
Inventory status & segregation

Preserve customs status, ownership, restrictions, location and movements. Do not merge lots with differing legal or quality status.

Goods status model

Keep physical custody separate from legal status.

A
Expected

Pre-advice only; goods have not been physically received.

B
Received — reconciliation pending

Physical receipt recorded; quantity and authority evidence still being checked.

C
Held / restricted

Goods remain subject to customs, regulatory, quality or operator restrictions.

D
Authorized activity

Only the specific verified handling, storage or processing scope may proceed.

E
Exit / transfer evidence pending

Do not close the movement until required exit, transfer or customs status evidence is recorded.

Jurisdiction and status safeguardsFree trade zones, special economic zones, bonded warehouses and customs-controlled facilities are not interchangeable legal categories. Rights, duty suspension, eligible activities, inventory controls, reporting, transfers and exit procedures differ by jurisdiction and designation. Verify the applicable law, authority, zone designation, operator scope and effective dates before relying on any treatment. Physical receipt, internal inventory status, customs admission, duty suspension and customs release are separate facts. KRIM must not infer a tax or duty benefit, or mark goods released, from a facility label or internal workflow alone.
