Classification research workflow

Document the reasoning and evidence—not just a code value.

From product identity to reviewed decision

Classification requires complete product facts and applicable legal references.

1
Capture complete product characteristics

Record composition, material, function, operating principle, technical specifications, presentation, intended use and supporting product literature.

2
Research the applicable nomenclature

Consult the current legal tariff schedule, HS version, section and chapter notes, headings, subheadings and relevant interpretative rules.

3
Compare plausible headings

Record candidate codes, competing interpretations, exclusions and the factual distinctions that determine the classification.

4
Independent expert review

Require a qualified reviewer to confirm the code, legal rationale, jurisdiction and effective date. Escalate ambiguity for authoritative advice where appropriate.

5
Publish controlled decision

Record scope, rationale, source snapshots, reviewer, approval date and re-review triggers. Preserve superseded decisions and their history.

Classification record essentials

Minimum traceability information for a defensible decision.

A
Product facts

Exact product identity, specifications, images or technical documents, composition and intended function.

B
Code & legal rationale

HS edition, tariff line, interpretative rule applied, explanatory material and competing headings considered.

C
Jurisdiction & effective date

Import/export territory, national tariff extension, measure source, publication date and period of applicability.

D
Review & change history

Reviewer identity, decision rationale, evidence version, approval, challenge and subsequent amendment events.

Classification and tariff integrityAn HS code suggestion is not a binding ruling or legal determination. Do not infer a code from a short product name, assume a universal national tariff line, or reuse an old decision without checking product identity, HS edition, jurisdiction and effective date. Duty rates and trade measures must come from current authoritative sources. Unresolved or incomplete product facts must remain unclassified—not silently marked clear.
