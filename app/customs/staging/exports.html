Export coordination lifecycle

Separate commercial readiness, regulatory review and official authority outcomes.

Order to departure hand-off

Every milestone needs a responsible actor and traceable evidence.

1
Exporter and transaction setup

Link exporter identity, buyer/order, goods description, origin, destination, transaction terms and shipment reference.

2
Goods and document readiness

Gather commercial invoice, packing and transport details, origin evidence and other documents required for the specific goods and jurisdiction.

3
Export controls and permits review

Identify possible licensing, sanctions, restricted-party, dual-use, product and destination controls using current authoritative sources and qualified review.

4
Broker and declaration coordination

Validate broker mandate and scope; record declaration preparation, submission acknowledgement, authority queries and inspection evidence.

5
Release and departure evidence

Record official export release separately from carrier acceptance, loading, departure and proof of delivery or export evidence.

Export control checklist

Applicability depends on the transaction facts and law.

A
Party and destination

Verify exporter, consignee, intermediaries, end user, destination and routing as required by applicable rules.

B
Goods classification

Confirm product identity, HS classification and any controlled-goods classification using appropriate sources.

C
Licence and restrictions

Determine whether a licence, permit, authorization or other restriction applies; record issuing authority and scope.

D
Filing & exit evidence

Preserve declaration reference, official messages, release evidence and reliable departure records.

Export authorization and status integrityExport declaration submission, customs acceptance, release, carrier booking, loading, departure and proof of export are distinct events. A successful internal review or empty screening result does not establish legal permission to export. Applicable controls depend on origin, destination, parties, goods, end use and effective law. KRIM must preserve source, timestamp, jurisdiction, reviewer and decision scope; unresolved or unavailable data stays unresolved. Actual logistics services remain subject to verified partner availability and separate agreements.
