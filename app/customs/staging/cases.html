Case lifecycle

Recommended controlled sequence; actual transitions remain subject to server-side authorization and evidence rules.

CONTROLLED FLOW
01
Case opened and shipment linked

Validate tenant ownership, shipment identity, trade direction and relevant jurisdiction.

02
Requirements and evidence collected

Attach required documents and source-backed jurisdiction requirements to the case.

03
Broker assigned and accepts

Confirm active broker organization, authorization and licence scope for that jurisdiction.

04
Review and submission evidence

Record reviewer, timestamps, declaration references and verifiable submission evidence.

05
Authority outcome recorded separately

Release or hold outcomes require competent authority evidence and independent verification.

Case controls

Mandatory checks before a case can progress.

✓
Organization and shipment binding

Prevent cross-tenant, unrelated-shipment or wrong-customer case access.

✓
Jurisdiction validation

Require approved, effective-dated regulatory requirements for the applicable route.

✓
Evidence completeness

Missing mandatory checks or documents must block or require review, never pass by default.

✓
Independent review

Separate document preparation, broker activity and privileged review where required.
