Controlled case lifecycle

Operational sequence; transitions require verified server-side permissions and supporting evidence.

CASE CONTROLS
01
Open and bind the case

Confirm organization ownership, shipment identity, trade direction and relevant jurisdiction.

02
Establish requirements and evidence

Use effective-dated, approved jurisdiction sources. Missing checks never pass by default.

03
Appoint an eligible broker

Verify organization status, licence scope and jurisdiction fit before relying on broker activity.

04
Review and record submission evidence

Preserve declaration references, responsible reviewer, timestamps and source evidence.

05
Record authority outcome separately

Release or hold requires competent authority evidence and independent verification.

Mandatory case safeguards

Controls that must hold before case progression.

✓
Tenant and shipment binding

Prevent cross-organization or unrelated-shipment access.

✓
Jurisdiction validation

Require approved, effective-dated requirements for the applicable route.

✓
Evidence completeness

Missing mandatory documents or checks trigger review or a block, never an automatic pass.

✓
Independent review

Separate preparation, broker activity and privileged review where required.
