Controlled declaration lifecycle

Every state change requires an authorized actor, evidence and a recorded timestamp.

Preparation to authority outcome

Separate internal workflow progress from official customs status.

1
Draft assembly

Link the shipment and case; capture parties, goods lines, classification references, origin, valuation, procedure and supporting evidence as applicable.

2
Data validation

Run jurisdiction- and procedure-specific checks for required fields, code formats, consistency, evidence links and effective rule versions.

3
Authorized human review

Record declarant authority, broker mandate, reviewer identity, exceptions and approval. The preparer cannot approve their own submission where segregation is required.

4
Submission evidence

Only an authorized integration or declarant may submit. Preserve the actual submission timestamp, channel, payload reference and acknowledgement.

5
Authority response & follow-up

Record official reference, queries, amendments, assessment, acceptance, release or rejection only from a traceable authority response.

Key declaration controls

Do not promote a declaration based on incomplete or assumed data.

A
Jurisdiction & procedure

Identify the correct customs territory, office, procedure and applicable effective date.

B
Goods & party data

Validate goods descriptions, quantities, values, origin, tariff references and party identifiers against source records.

C
Evidence & authorization

Check required supporting documents, broker mandate and declarant permissions before submission.

D
Immutable event history

Retain versions, changes, actor identity, validation outcomes, submission acknowledgements and authority messages.

Authority-status integrity“Ready for review”, “approved internally”, “submitted”, “accepted by authority” and “released” are distinct states. A successful internal validation is not proof of submission. A transmission acknowledgement is not customs acceptance. Never infer release, clearance, duty payment or regulatory approval without an authoritative, traceable status record.
