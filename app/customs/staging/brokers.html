Required control sequence

Every check must pass for the exact case, activity and jurisdiction.

1
Organization & user authority

Confirm the legal organization, active membership and permission of the acting user.

2
Licence evidence review

Validate source, authenticity, validity dates and independent approval.

3
Jurisdiction & activity match

Check the current authorization covers the exact customs activity and territory.

4
Case-specific request

Link the assignment to an eligible customs case and record the request.

5
Authorized broker response

Record acceptance or decline with actor, timestamp and audit history.

Regulatory and security boundaryA broker profile, uploaded licence or assignment status is not proof of legal authorization. Missing, expired, mismatched or unreviewed evidence must block assignment. Production actions require authenticated, authorized organization membership, strong authentication, independent verification and immutable audit events. Assignment acceptance does not mean a customs authority has accepted a declaration or released goods.
