Evidence completeness workflow

Completeness is evaluated against a reviewed checklist for the specific transaction.

Document lifecycle

Keep every transition attributable and preserve prior versions.

1
Expected

A checklist item is identified for a case, based on its jurisdiction, customs procedure, goods and effective requirements source.

2
Submitted

A document is received with uploader identity, timestamp, source, issuer and case linkage recorded.

3
Under review

An authorized reviewer checks legibility, completeness, consistency, authenticity indicators and validity as applicable.

4
Accepted for internal case evidence

Internal review is complete for the stated purpose; this is not customs authority acceptance or release.

5
Rejected / superseded / expired

Record the reason and preserve history. A replacement must create a new version rather than erase the earlier record.

Evidence record essentials

Capture the minimum metadata needed for traceability and controlled review.

A
Identity & linkage

Document type, unique record ID, shipment, customs case and responsible organization.

B
Provenance

Uploader or source, issuing party, source reference, received time and retrieval context.

C
Version & validity

Version number, effective date, expiry date where applicable and supersession relationship.

D
Review & access

Reviewer, decision, rationale, timestamps, access permissions and immutable audit events.
