Import coordination lifecycle

Track operational readiness separately from official customs decisions.

Pre-arrival to delivery hand-off

Each milestone must be supported by a reliable source and responsible actor.

1
Import file setup

Link purchase/order details, importer identity, goods information, shipment reference, transport documents and destination jurisdiction.

2
Pre-arrival readiness

Review estimated arrival information only from a connected, authorized source; identify permits, product controls and document gaps for human review.

3
Broker and declaration coordination

Confirm broker mandate and scope, provide approved evidence and track declaration preparation and submission acknowledgements.

4
Authority assessment & release

Record queries, inspections, assessments, payment evidence and release only when supported by traceable authority or payment records.

5
Delivery and file closure

Coordinate the authorized transport hand-off, delivery evidence, exceptions and final reconciliation. Do not close while material issues remain unresolved.

Import control checklist

Context-specific checks require source-backed review.

A
Importer & goods identity

Verify legal entity, importer identifiers, product details, origin and intended customs procedure.

B
Documents & permits

Apply a jurisdiction- and goods-specific checklist based on current authoritative requirements.

C
Broker authority

Check licence scope, validity, appointment and declarant permissions before hand-off.

D
Milestone evidence

Store source, event time, received time, actor and supporting reference for each status update.

Import exception controls

Unverified information remains pending; it must not be converted into a positive status.

Customs and logistics status integrityArrival estimates, port events, declaration submission, customs acceptance, duty payment, authority release and final delivery are different events. A broker update or internal checklist cannot substitute for official release evidence. KRIM must not claim that goods are cleared, available for collection or delivered unless the relevant source and responsible party confirm it. Actual services remain subject to verified partner availability and separate agreements.
