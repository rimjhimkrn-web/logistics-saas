Verification standard

Evidence review must be complete, current and independently attributable.

Required verification dimensions

Every applicable dimension must be recorded with its source and reviewer.

1
Issuing authority & source authenticity

Identify the competent authority and retain a source reference, retrieval date and evidence provenance.

2
Legal entity identity

Match the licence holder to the broker's verified legal entity; resolve name changes and entity mismatches.

3
Jurisdiction & permitted activities

Record territorial scope, customs role, activity restrictions and any conditions or exclusions.

4
Validity & current standing

Check issue, effective, expiry, suspension and revocation information where authoritative data exists.

5
Independent approval & audit trail

Require an authorized reviewer distinct from the submitter, with decision rationale and timestamp.

Decision states

Use explicit states; never treat missing data as approval.

A
Submitted

Evidence received but not yet reviewed. No authorization is implied.

B
Under review

Authenticity, identity, scope or validity checks remain open.

C
Verified for stated scope

Independent evidence checks passed for the recorded jurisdiction and activity only.

D
Rejected / expired / suspended

Eligibility is blocked until a new authoritative review supports a change.

Fail-closed compliance ruleMissing, unreadable, expired, suspended, revoked, mismatched or unverified evidence must not be treated as valid. A verification decision applies only to the named legal entity, licence instrument, jurisdiction, activity and effective period. A platform review is not a substitute for a competent authority's determination.
