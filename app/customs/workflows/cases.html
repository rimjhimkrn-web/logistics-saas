Controlled case lifecycle

Operational sequence for a traceable customs case.

WORKFLOW
01
Open and bind the case

Confirm the customer organization, shipment identity, trade direction and applicable jurisdiction.

02
Establish requirements and evidence

Use current, approved jurisdiction sources. Missing requirements or checks remain unverified and must trigger review or a block.

03
Coordinate an eligible customs broker

Check broker authorization, licence scope, validity and jurisdiction fit before assignment.

04
Record declaration and submission evidence

Preserve declaration references, responsible parties, timestamps and supporting evidence.

05
Verify the authority outcome independently

Record official acceptance, query, hold or release only against verifiable competent-authority evidence.

Mandatory case safeguards

Controls required before case progression.

CONTROL POINTS
✓
Organization and shipment binding

Prevent access to unrelated organizations, customers or shipments.

✓
Jurisdiction validation

Require approved, effective-dated requirements for the applicable route.

✓
Evidence completeness

Missing mandatory documents or checks trigger review or a block, never an automatic pass.

✓
Independent review

Separate preparation, broker activity and privileged approval where required.

✓
Authority release verification

Internal case completion must never be treated as customs clearance.

NO ASSUMPTIONS
Case status is not customs clearanceOpening a case, uploading documents, assigning a broker or recording an internal submission does not prove government filing acceptance, approval or release. Authority outcomes require separate official evidence and verification.
