Capacity planning controls

Keep resource planning traceable, time-bounded and subject to partner confirmation.

Capacity planning sequence

Each capacity plan should be checked for resource definition, location, time window, partner evidence, demand assumptions and constraints.

01 / RECEIVEDefine scopeSet network area, resource type, facility or lane and planning period.
02 / VALIDATEModel demandEstimate expected demand using documented assumptions and source periods.
03 / RECONCILEConfirm supplyObtain partner or facility confirmation with quantity, validity window and restrictions.
04 / RETAINReview scenarioCompare demand and confirmed supply, flag gaps and record reviewer decisions.

Capacity planning quality checks

Apply these checks before capacity is used for commitments or operational allocation.

✓
Resource & location definitionConfirm supply origin, destination, intermediate hubs and any access or delivery restrictions.
↗
Time-bound capacity confirmationRecord confirmed quantity, validity dates, source, partner and operating restrictions.
◎
Demand & utilization reviewSeparate forecast demand from confirmed supply; review utilization, peak periods and capacity gaps.
⌁
Scenario & audit trailReview scenario original event payload, corrections, actor and reason for amendment.
