Customs & trade readiness sequence

Each cross-border movement should be checked for applicable jurisdictions, commodity classification inputs, required documents, filing responsibilities, regulatory controls and release evidence.

01 / RECEIVEDefine trade scopeCapture shipment parties, origin, destination, goods description, trade direction and applicable jurisdictions.
02 / VALIDATEBuild document setIdentify applicable invoices, packing details, transport documents, certificates, permits and declarations as required for the specific movement.
03 / RECONCILEValidate & submitCheck consistency across documents, confirm authorized declarant or broker, and retain submission references and acknowledgements.
04 / RETAINTrack decision & releaseTrack authority queries, additional evidence, duty or fee dependencies and official release evidence before onward movement.
