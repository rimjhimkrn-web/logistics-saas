Operational controls

Dispatch & handover controls

Keep dispatch release and custody transfer attributable, documented and independently reviewable.

Lifecycle control sequence

Each dispatch should be checked for shipment identity, release authority, document readiness, party acknowledgement and custody evidence.

01 / RECEIVEPrepareConfirm shipment, assigned service partner and dispatch instruction.
02 / VALIDATECheckVehicle and driver details, required documents and release conditions.
03 / RECONCILEReleaseRecord authorized release decision, pickup time and dispatch reference.
04 / RETAINHandoverCapture receiving party acknowledgement, timestamp and proof of handover.
