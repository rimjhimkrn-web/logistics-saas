Operational safeguards

• Facility registration does not activate a site.
• Only approved facilities can be used.
• Inventory changes occur through atomic RPCs.
• Replayed requests are idempotent.
• Evidence is stored privately.
• Development/Test and individual-interest accounts cannot perform commercial warehouse operations.
