Allocation lifecycle & safeguards

01
Reserve
Only independently released stock at an active, approved facility can be reserved. Existing reservations reduce the unallocated balance.
02
Pick
Picking posts a controlled inventory movement and records the operator, time and idempotency key. Stock must still be released and sufficient.
03
Release
An unpicked reservation can be released with a reason. Picked allocations cannot be cancelled from this screen.

Integrity control: the browser does not edit inventory quantities directly. Every reservation, pick and release is submitted to a server-side database function which rechecks authorization and operational conditions. A successful UI response is not proof of physical picking or dispatch.
