Handling-unit identity

A handling unit must have a unique, durable identity independent of the shipment or product it currently contains.

▣
Unit types & attributes

Pallet, carton, crate, tote, roll cage, container or reusable transport item; dimensions, tare weight, max gross weight and handling restrictions.

#
Unique identifiers

SSCC or approved internal identifier, barcode/RFID token, issuer, check digit and duplicate protection.

◎
Condition & seal

Condition grade, inspection evidence, seal number, tamper status and authorised seal break/reseal events.

⌖
Ownership & custody

Asset owner, current custodian, organisation, facility, location and custody transfer history.

Unit composition & hierarchy

Support nesting and aggregation without losing item-level traceability.

1
Create or receive unit
Assign unique identifier, unit type, facility, condition and source event.
2
Attach contents
Link SKU, lot, quantity, unit of measure, quality status and source receipt or allocation.
3
Build hierarchy
Place cartons on pallets or pallets in containers with parent-child links and cycle-safe validation.
4
Reconcile every change
Validate quantity conservation on pack, unpack, split, merge and transfer; preserve full history.

Container & shipment controls

A container assignment is not a shipment booking or a customs clearance.

⇄
Shipment association

Link handling units to authorised shipment legs, load plans and consolidation groups.

⚖
Weight & capacity

Validate gross, tare, payload, dimensions, stacking, centre-of-gravity and equipment limits.

⚑
Compliance & segregation

Apply dangerous-goods, temperature, customs, seal, food-grade and cargo compatibility rules.

✓
Load & unload verification

Scan each unit at custody handover; reconcile expected versus actual units and contents.

Backend readiness & release gates

The inspected database includes krim_warehouse_shipment_allocations for shipment-linked stock reservations and krim_warehouse_evidence for task-linked evidence metadata. Neither represents a reusable handling-unit registry or container contents ledger. No dedicated handling-unit/container tables or related RPCs were found in this inspection.

A
Design unit data model
Unit master, identifiers, types, dimensions, ownership, custody, seals, parent-child relationships and lifecycle events.
B
Build atomic content APIs
Pack, unpack, split, merge and move with quantity conservation, lot/quality preservation and idempotency.
C
Secure scan & custody
Facility-scoped roles, authorised scan devices, duplicate detection, offline reconciliation and append-only audit.
D
Validate real operations
Test nested units, damaged seals, partial loads, wrong facility, mixed quality, over-capacity and lost connectivity.
