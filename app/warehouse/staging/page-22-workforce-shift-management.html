Safe staffing controls

A roster must never assign a person to work outside verified skills, working-time rules or facility permissions.

✓
Competency match
Validate current training, equipment authorisation and task-specific qualifications before assignment.
◷
Working-time safeguards
Enforce maximum hours, minimum rest, breaks, overtime approvals and local labour requirements.
⌖
Facility & role scope
Restrict roster visibility and supervisor actions to authorised organisation and facility scope.
⚑
Fatigue & safety escalation
Provide protected reporting for fatigue, unsafe conditions, incidents and unavailable equipment.

Privacy, compliance & release gates

Workforce data is personal data. Collect only what is necessary, define retention and access rules, and keep sensitive employee records out of broad operational dashboards.

A
Build the data model
Worker profiles, employment/organisation links, facility assignments, skills, shift templates, roster entries, time events, leave and handover records.
B
Enforce permissions server-side
Worker self-service, supervisor, facility manager, HR and administrator scopes; no reliance on hidden UI controls.
C
Protect integrity
Immutable clock events, correction reasons, approval separation, idempotency, audit events and notification delivery records.
D
Validate local rules
Test labour-hour limits, rest periods, overtime, leave, night shifts, daylight-saving changes and facility-local time zones before live use.
