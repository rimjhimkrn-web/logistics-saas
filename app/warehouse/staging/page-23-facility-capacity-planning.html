Facility capacity dimensions

A reliable capacity plan combines physical space, handling capability, workforce, equipment and time-window constraints—not just a single square-foot or pallet figure.

▦
Storage footprint

Usable floor area, rack positions, pallet slots, bin locations, stacking limits, aisle clearances and reserved zones.

⇄
Inbound & outbound throughput

Receiving, put-away, picking, packing, staging and dispatch capacity by shift and operating window.

⌖
Dock & yard resources

Dock doors, vehicle bays, yard positions, appointment windows, equipment and safe traffic constraints.

♙
Workforce & equipment

Qualified staff, forklifts, material-handling equipment, charging, maintenance downtime and shift coverage.

Capacity planning controls

Capacity must be calculated from verified resources and time-bound commitments.

01
Define effective capacity
Separate rated, safe usable, operational and currently available capacity by resource and time window.
02
Deduct commitments
Account for confirmed bookings, reservations, active tasks, safety buffers and maintenance downtime.
03
Apply compatibility
Validate cargo dimensions, weight, temperature, hazardous-goods segregation, handling and access restrictions.
04
Reforecast continuously
Update forecasts from verified receipts, dispatches, stock movements, workforce and equipment status.

Planning workspaces

The complete facility planner should provide auditable planning views for each resource category.

▤
Space & storage map

Facility zones, racks, bins, pallet positions, cubic capacity, blocked locations and utilisation by zone.

◷
Time-window planner

Dock appointments, receiving/picking throughput, peak forecasts, cut-offs and shift-level resource limits.

▣
Resource register

Equipment, dock doors, vehicles, workforce skills, operating status and scheduled maintenance.

◎
Demand & scenario forecast

Expected inbound/outbound volumes, seasonality, safety buffers and what-if scenarios with assumptions.

⚑
Constraint & risk register

Congestion, temperature zones, compliance restrictions, equipment outages and overflow escalation plans.

✓
Capacity approvals

Independent review, effective dates, change history and approval before partner/customer commitments.
