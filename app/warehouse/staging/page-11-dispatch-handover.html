Handover controls

01
Validate picked allocations
At least one picked allocation is required and no reserved allocation may remain unpicked for the selected shipment leg.
02
Create custody task
The authenticated RPC verifies operator access and creates an auditable task with an idempotency key.
03
Complete picking first
The server requires a completed picking_packing task for the exact same shipment and leg before handover completion.
04
Dispatch separately
Carrier assignment, shipment dispatch, notifications and transport milestones remain controlled by Operations.
