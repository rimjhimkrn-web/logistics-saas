Gate-in control sequence

Gate entry must be connected to a verified appointment or authorised operational exception. Never grant entry solely from a typed registration number.

01
Identify the visit

Resolve facility, appointment or shipment reference, carrier organisation, visit purpose and expected arrival window.

02
Verify driver & vehicle

Confirm authorised driver-to-vehicle binding, registration jurisdiction, required documents and current verification status.

03
Safety & security checks

Record seal, load, PPE, safety declaration and any site-specific restrictions; route discrepancies to an authorised reviewer.

04
Authorise gate-in

Server validates facility scope, visit status and duplicate entry; capture trusted timestamp, gate operator and reason.

Yard positioning & movement

A live yard board requires a facility-defined map or location register with occupancy and safety constraints.

⌖
Assign staging position

⇄
Track internal moves
Origin, destination, movement authorisation, operator, vehicle and event time.
◷
Manage dwell time
Queue age, appointment variance, dwell thresholds and escalation to the control tower.
⚠
Handle exceptions
Blocked lane, unsafe vehicle, missing documents, overstay, damage or unauthorised movement.

Gate-out & departure release

Departure is a controlled release—not simply a second scan.

▣
Dispatch readiness

Validate required warehouse handover, loading completion, outbound reference and any applicable release controls.

✓
Final checks

Capture seal and vehicle condition checks, authorised release decision and exception clearance.

⇥
Close the visit

Record gate-out timestamp, operator, destination reference and immutable visit event; prevent duplicate close-out.

Database readiness & release gates

Schema inspection found krim_vehicles, krim_vehicle_documents and krim_vehicle_driver_bindings. These are fleet identity/compliance records, not a yard-management system. No dedicated yard visit, gate log, queue or location tables were identified.

A
Design canonical yard model

B

Test physical operations
