Inventory integrity rules

Read-only by design. This page does not create, edit or delete stock records. Goods receipt, put-away, picking, dispatch, returns and approved adjustments must use their respective server-controlled workflows. Never interpret a failed load as zero inventory.

Quality-held, rejected or otherwise restricted stock must not be allocated as pickable inventory. Verify the server-side quality state and facility permissions before any physical movement.
