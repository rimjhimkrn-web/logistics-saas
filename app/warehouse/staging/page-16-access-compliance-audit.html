Identity & access governance

Every access decision must be linked to a verified identity, organisation membership, approved facility and explicit permission set.

01
Identity assurance
Authenticated account, verified recovery route, session lifecycle and secure sign-out.
02
Organisation membership
Active membership with role assignment controlled by authorised administrators—not user-editable profile metadata.
03
Facility scope
Approved facility membership and task scope checked server-side for every read and mutation.
04
Least privilege
Separate read, create, review, approve, release and export permissions; independent review for sensitive decisions.

Audit event lifecycle

Audit events should be generated server-side alongside the business transaction and protected against edits or deletion by ordinary workspace users.

1
Capture actor & context

Actor ID, organisation, entity type/ID, event type, source, request/correlation ID and trusted timestamp.

2
Record state transition

Previous and new state, decision rationale and references to supporting evidence—without unnecessary sensitive payloads.

3
Restrict & monitor

Read access scoped to authorised auditors and administrators; privileged access monitored and reviewed.

4
Retain & export safely

Documented retention schedule, legal holds, tamper-evident export and auditable export access.

Release gates before enabling records

A
Verify permission model
Map warehouse roles to approved actions and validate organisation/facility isolation using real authenticated test accounts.
B
Authorise audit reads
Design a narrow server-side audit reader with field minimisation, access logging and pagination; retain RLS as defence in depth.
C
Protect audit integrity
Ensure application roles cannot update/delete audit records; validate transaction coupling, idempotency and tamper evidence.
D
Complete compliance ownership
Assign jurisdiction-specific control owners, evidence expiry, review cadence and legal review before marking obligations satisfied.
