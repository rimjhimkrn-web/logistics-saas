Essential service notifications

Assignment offers, safety alerts, shipment changes, exception notices, security events and other essential service communications must remain enabled through an appropriate channel. Marketing preferences must not disable operational or legally required messages.
Notification delivery can depend on configured provider, verified contact details, device permissions and delivery status. A notification record is not proof of successful external delivery.

When you need urgent help

For immediate danger, injury, fire or a road emergency, contact local emergency services first. Then notify your dispatcher or carrier through the verified contact channel provided by your organization.

For shipment delays, access issues, cargo discrepancies or app problems, preserve factual details and the assignment reference before contacting dispatch.
