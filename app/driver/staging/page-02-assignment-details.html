Before you proceed

Use this review sequence on every dispatch.

1Confirm assignment identity
Check the dispatch reference and assigned driver.
2Check route and timing
Resolve missing or conflicting location and schedule details.
3Review cargo requirements
Confirm handling, safety and documentation instructions.
4Verify vehicle readiness
Proceed only with an authorized, suitable vehicle.
