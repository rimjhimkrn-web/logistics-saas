Failed delivery checklist

1. Confirm safetyMove to a safe location. If there is immediate danger, contact local emergency services before using this platform.
2. Record the actual outcomeCapture the real attempt time, location, factual reason, contact outcome and any recipient or site instructions. Do not guess or backdate.
3. Protect the shipmentKeep cargo secure, preserve seals and packaging, and document damage or discrepancies without altering original evidence.
4. Contact dispatchProvide the assignment reference and factual details. Wait for an authorized decision before leaving cargo, redelivering or returning it.

Report an unsuccessful delivery attempt

This records a driver exception for dispatch review. It does not mark the shipment failed, authorize a return or create a redelivery instruction.

The current secure driver RPC stores this as an exception event. Dispatch must separately authorize any return, redelivery or shipment status transition.
