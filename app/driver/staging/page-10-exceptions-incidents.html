Incident response checklist

Use the relevant steps and preserve evidence. Do not delay emergency response to complete a platform report.

Delay or route obstruction
  • Move to a safe location where possible and inform dispatch.
  • Report the actual location, time, cause if known, and revised risk to the delivery schedule.
  • Do not invent an ETA or change an approved route without authorization.
Cargo damage, shortage or seal discrepancy
  • Stop handling if continued movement could worsen damage or create danger.
  • Preserve packaging, seal details, counts and clear photographs where safe and permitted.
  • Record who was present and obtain instructions before release or onward movement.
Safety, accident or security incident
  • Protect life first; contact local emergency services when needed.
  • Notify dispatch and follow applicable emergency/site procedures.
  • Share factual time, place, people involved and actions taken; avoid speculation.
