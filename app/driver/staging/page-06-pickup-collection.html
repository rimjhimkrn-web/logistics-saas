Collection verification checklist

Use this checklist at the collection site. The pickup milestone is recorded only when you confirm actual handover through the secure action below and the server validates the assignment state.

1 · Shipment identityMatch the shipment reference and authorized pickup location with the dispatch instruction.
2 · Authorized handoverConfirm the collection contact is authorized to release the consignment.
3 · Package reconciliationCount packages and compare the physical count with the shipment manifest.
4 · Cargo conditionInspect visible packaging and seals; record damage, shortage, excess or tampering as an exception.
5 · Evidence and custodyCapture required evidence and timestamp through the approved operational workflow before custody is considered transferred.

Collection integrity

Do not guess package quantities, condition, contact identity or collection time. If details disagree with the physical consignment, stop and report an exception. Do not confirm pickup until the physical handover and package reconciliation are complete.
