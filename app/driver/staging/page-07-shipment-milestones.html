Milestone sequence reference

A guide to the typical transport journey. These steps are not presented as completed events for any shipment.

1
Assignment acceptedDriver acceptance is confirmed by the assignment workflow.
2
Pickup / collectionPhysical handover and required evidence are recorded through the approved pickup workflow.
3
In transitAuthorized transit events and checkpoints are recorded against the correct shipment.
4
At destinationArrival is recorded through the authorized milestone workflow; GPS alone does not prove arrival or delivery.
5
Delivery / exceptionVerified proof of delivery or a documented failed-delivery/exception workflow closes the journey.
