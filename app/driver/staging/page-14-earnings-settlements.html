Who pays the driver?

Under the current partner-direct operating model, your contracting carrier/employer agrees and pays your wages or trip earnings directly. KRIM does not hold or disburse driver wages through this page.
No verified partner earnings feed or driver settlement contract is connected here. Amounts are intentionally not estimated from trip count, distance or shipment value.

Need a payment clarification?

Contact your carrier/employer payroll or operations contact and quote the relevant assignment reference. Keep your written rate agreement and payment confirmation for your records.
