Proof of delivery requirements

Recipient confirmationRecord the actual recipient name and their authorized role or relationship, using the approved workflow.
Delivery evidenceUse original, legible evidence such as an authorized recipient signature or approved delivery document/photo.
Shipment and leg bindingEvidence must be bound server-side to the exact shipment and delivery leg, the assigned driver and the authorized organization.
Independent verificationWhere required, a separate authorized reviewer verifies evidence before delivery closure or downstream settlement.
