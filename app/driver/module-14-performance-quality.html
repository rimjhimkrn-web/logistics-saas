How quality should be assessed

TimelinessCompare verified actual milestone times with the applicable agreed schedule and approved exception windows.
Custody and evidenceUse shipment-bound, independently reviewed pickup and delivery evidence. A file count alone does not establish valid proof.
Safety and exceptionsReview factual incident reports and authorized outcomes without penalizing a driver for properly reported safety issues.
Customer feedbackOnly verified, shipment-linked feedback with a known source and review process should influence a quality score.

Current data limitations

The available driver event and delivery-evidence tables contain operational records, but do not establish a complete audited scorecard or customer-rating system. This page therefore reports record counts and event history only. No rating, on-time percentage or ranking is calculated.
