Before you accept

Make sure the offer is operationally feasible.

1 · Confirm availabilityYou can meet the scheduled pickup window and complete the assigned leg.
2 · Check the routePickup and delivery locations are clear and achievable.
3 · Confirm vehicle readinessYour assigned vehicle, documents and equipment are appropriate for the load.
4 · Escalate uncertaintyContact dispatch if cargo, route, timing or safety instructions are unclear.

What acceptance means

Accepting confirms your response to the assignment offer. It does not record pickup, cargo loading, location sharing, delivery or proof of delivery. Those are separate verified milestones.
