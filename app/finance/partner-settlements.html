Partner Settlements

Maintain clear visibility over partner-direct payment references and reconciliation checkpoints. KRIM connects customers with logistics providers; partners provide the services and receive freight payments directly from their customers.

Partner-direct paymentsNon-custodial modelAudit-orientedMulti-currency aware
Important operating model: KRIM does not collect freight charges, hold partner funds, operate escrow or execute partner freight payouts in this workflow. Customers contract with and pay the logistics partner directly. Any separately agreed KRIM referral or coordination fee must be invoiced independently. This page does not imply that a partner payment has occurred.
