Controlled issue-to-close

Invoice lifecycle

Every state change should be authorized, recorded and reversible only through a documented adjustment.

01 · SOURCEBilling trigger

Verify agreement, service evidence and fee calculation.

02 · PREPAREDraft invoice

Populate payer, issuer, reference, line items and terms.

03 · REVIEWValidate & approve

Check duplicate, arithmetic, tax fields and delegated approval.

04 · ISSUEDeliver document

Issue final copy and record timestamp and delivery evidence.

05 · CLOSEReceipt / adjustment

Match payment or issue a controlled credit note and reconcile.

Document governance

Billing safeguards

Protect document accuracy, legal compliance and the integrity of the receivables ledger.

#Unique numbering

Prevent duplicate invoice references and preserve sequence integrity under the configured policy.

⌕Duplicate detection

Flag repeated billing basis, payer, period and amount before issue.

▣Version history

Retain issued document versions and never silently overwrite a finalized invoice.

◎Tax validation

Require jurisdiction-specific tax review; do not assume a universal tax rate or exemption.

↶Credit notes & voids

Use linked, approved adjustment documents with a complete reason and audit trail.

⇄Ledger consistency

Reconcile invoice totals, adjustments and verified receipts to receivables records.
