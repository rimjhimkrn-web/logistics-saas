Invoice matching control

A structured review prevents unsupported, duplicate or incorrectly coded supplier obligations.

01 · COMMERCIAL BASISOrder or agreement

Confirm approved scope, supplier identity, rates and authorization.

02 · DELIVERY EVIDENCEReceipt or acceptance

Verify goods received, service completed or milestone accepted.

03 · FINANCIAL DOCUMENTSupplier invoice

Validate invoice number, tax details, currency, totals and due terms.

Payment release gate: a mismatch, missing document, duplicate warning or unresolved tax review keeps the obligation on hold. No payment instruction is generated by this staging preview.

Governance by design

Controls that protect the ledger

Core safeguards for accurate, accountable supplier payables.

⌁Duplicate invoice protection

Flag repeated supplier, invoice number, date and amount combinations before approval.

◎Segregation of duties

Separate invoice creation, review, approval and payment confirmation permissions.

▣Evidence retention

Preserve source documents, supporting records, review notes and change history.

⌂Supplier verification

Maintain verified payee identity and approved payment instruction history.

◷Due-date monitoring

Surface upcoming obligations only from validated invoice terms and dates.

⇄Reconciliation gate

Do not mark an obligation paid without independently verifiable payment evidence.
