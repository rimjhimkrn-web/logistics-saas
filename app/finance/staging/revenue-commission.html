Eligible KRIM fee categories

Revenue must arise from an actual, separately agreed KRIM service.

01
Referral fee

Earned under documented partner terms after the agreed qualifying referral event.

02
Marketing service fee

Supported by a defined campaign, deliverable or agreed marketing service.

03
Coordination fee

For an expressly contracted KRIM coordination service and completed milestone.

04
Platform service fee

Only where a valid agreement defines the service, price and earning condition.

Revenue recognition

Fee-to-cash lifecycle

Do not recognize projected, unearned or partner-owned freight amounts as KRIM revenue.

01 · CONTRACTApprove fee terms

Record parties, service scope, fee formula, currency and earning conditions.

02 · TRIGGERVerify milestone

Attach evidence that the agreed service or referral condition occurred.

03 · REVIEWApprove recognition

Validate ownership, amount basis, reversals and applicable accounting policy.

04 · BILLIssue KRIM invoice

Apply reviewed tax treatment, numbering and customer delivery controls.

05 · COLLECTMatch receipt

Verify KRIM-owned payment receipt and reconcile to the finance ledger.

Revenue governance

Commission integrity framework

Make every earned fee explainable, reviewable and tied to an actual KRIM service.

▤Agreement-first

No fee without an approved agreement, scope, rate basis and earning event.

◎Partner freight exclusion

Freight charges and partner collections never enter KRIM's revenue ledger.

✓Evidence-backed triggers

Attach the qualifying referral or service evidence before recognizing a fee.

♙Independent approval

Separate fee setup, trigger validation, invoice approval and receipt matching.

↺Reversal and clawback

Preserve adjustment reasons, original entries and contract-based recovery logic.

▣Audit-ready history

Retain agreement versions, approvals, invoices and payment references.
