Four currency dimensions

Preserve the original currency at every stage of the transaction.

01
Display currency

Optional presentation preference; never changes the underlying contractual amount.

02
Contract currency

The currency expressly agreed by the parties in the applicable contract or order.

03
Invoice currency

The currency and tax presentation stated on the issued KRIM invoice.

04
Receipt / settlement currency

The currency actually received or paid, confirmed by provider or bank evidence.

KRIM operating boundary

Keep partner freight outside KRIM's ledger

Currency conversion controls apply only to amounts that genuinely belong to KRIM.

i
Partner-direct operating model: customers contract with and pay logistics partners directly. KRIM does not collect, hold, convert, split, settle or disburse partner freight funds. Partner freight amounts and currency exposure must not be recorded as KRIM cash, revenue, receivables or payables. This workspace covers only KRIM's own separately agreed referral, marketing, coordination or platform-service fees and its own obligations. Accounting, tax-currency and foreign-exchange treatment must be confirmed under the applicable professional accounting and tax advice.
