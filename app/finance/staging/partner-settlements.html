Controlled processing

Obligation lifecycle

Every status transition is supported by source evidence and an authorized actor.

01 · INTAKERecord obligation

Capture counterparty, contractual basis, currency and supporting documents.

02 · VALIDATECheck ownership

Confirm KRIM is the liable party and exclude partner-owned freight flows.

03 · REVIEWIndependent approval

Apply role permissions, conflict checks and documented approval.

04 · EXECUTERecord payment

Capture verified KRIM payment reference only after authorized execution.

05 · RECONCILEClose the record

Match bank evidence, retain history and resolve discrepancies.

Governance and boundaries

Financial control framework

Protect partner trust while keeping KRIM's own obligations auditable.

⌑Separate financial ownership

Partner freight invoices and customer payments remain outside KRIM's payable ledger.

♙Role-based authority

▣Evidence-first records

↺Controlled adjustments
