Receivables lifecycle

Each amount must be earned, documented, billed and reconciled in sequence.

01 · AGREEMENTFee terms recorded

Identify payer, service scope, agreed fee and contractual trigger.

02 · EARNEDTrigger verified

Confirm the qualifying service event and supporting evidence.

03 · INVOICEInvoice issued

Generate the correct legal entity, tax and payment terms.

04 · COLLECTIONReceipt matched

Verify the receipt, payer, amount, currency and reference.

05 · CLOSEReconcile & report

Apply receipts, resolve differences and retain audit evidence.

Accuracy and accountability

Receivables control framework

Controls designed to prevent premature revenue recognition and inaccurate collection status.

⌁Earned-fee verification

Do not invoice a fee until the documented agreement trigger and service evidence are satisfied.

▤Invoice integrity

Enforce unique invoice references, correct payer identity, currency and approved terms.

⇄Receipt matching

Match receipts against invoice references and verified bank or provider evidence.

◎Dispute handling

Keep disputed amounts visible and prevent silent edits to invoice or collection history.

◷Ageing discipline

Calculate overdue status from approved due dates and the relevant accounting date.

▣Audit-ready history

Retain invoice versions, reminders, receipt evidence, adjustments and approvals.
