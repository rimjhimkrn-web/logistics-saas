Evidence checklist

Reviewers must have enough evidence to reach a documented, fair decision.

01
Ownership and eligibility

Confirm KRIM is the responsible contracting or billing party.

02
Original transaction

Match amount, currency, payer, provider reference and receipt.

03
Supporting evidence

Retain agreement, correspondence, service evidence and reason code.

04
Independent decision

Record reviewer, approval authority, rationale and timestamp.

Resolution process

Case lifecycle

Keep request intake, decision authority and financial execution separate.

01 · INTAKERegister request

Capture case owner, transaction reference, reason and received timestamp.

02 · VALIDATEConfirm responsibility

Check contract, payment ownership, eligibility and applicable deadlines.

03 · INVESTIGATEReview evidence

Gather provider events, correspondence and supporting records.

04 · DECIDEAuthorize outcome

Independent approval with reason, amount basis and communication record.

05 · CLOSEVerify and reconcile

Match provider confirmation and KRIM ledger entry before closure.

Governance and safeguards

Resolution control framework

Prevent unauthorized refunds, duplicate actions and disputes assigned to the wrong party.

◎Correct case ownership

Route partner freight disputes to the responsible logistics partner, not KRIM's ledger.

♙Separation of duties

Case creator cannot approve their own refund or financial adjustment.

⌁Duplicate prevention

Idempotency and original transaction checks prevent repeat refund execution.

◷Deadline management

Track source-confirmed provider and scheme deadlines with escalation history.

▣Evidence retention

Preserve submitted documents, communications, decisions and external references.

⇄Verified closure

Close only after provider outcome and KRIM-owned ledger evidence reconcile.
