▣Evidence retention

⇩Audit exports

Restrict exports by role and log report scope, requester and generated version.

KRIM operating boundary

Protect the partner-direct model

KRIM treasury is not a pool for customer or logistics-partner freight money.

i
Non-custodial operating boundary: customers contract with and pay logistics partners directly. KRIM does not collect, hold, pool, convert, split, settle or disburse partner freight funds. Partner bank accounts, freight receipts, freight refunds and partner settlement balances are outside KRIM's treasury register and reconciliation. This workspace is limited to KRIM-owned bank accounts, its own separately agreed service-fee receipts and KRIM's own expenses and obligations.
