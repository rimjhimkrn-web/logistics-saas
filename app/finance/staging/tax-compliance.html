KRIM operating boundary

Separate KRIM fees from partner freight

Tax treatment must follow the actual contracting and invoicing parties.

i
Partner-direct operating model: customers contract with and pay logistics partners directly. KRIM does not collect, hold, split or disburse partner freight funds. The partner remains responsible for its own freight billing and applicable tax obligations. This KRIM workspace covers only KRIM's own separately agreed referral, marketing, coordination or platform-service fees and related obligations. The treatment of each service and transaction requires professional review; partner freight values must not be treated as KRIM turnover or revenue by default.
