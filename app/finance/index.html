Treasury controls & audit readiness

01
Payment is not payout

A successful customer payment and a completed partner payout are separate events and must be reconciled independently.

02
Reconcile before settlement

Verify provider references, amount, currency, shipment or assignment, and settlement status before any approval.

03
Least-privilege access

Database row-level security remains authoritative. The interface does not bypass access policies.

Restricted financial actions
Refunds, payout execution, settlement approval, invoice edits and journal adjustments are not enabled in this workspace until the authorized server-side workflows, idempotency, validation and audit trail are verified.
