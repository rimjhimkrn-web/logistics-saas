Regulatory responsibility

Authorization and customs role boundaries

Every regulated action must be performed by the person and entity legally entitled to perform it.

Before accepting customs work

Verify both platform eligibility and the separate statutory authority for the exact jurisdiction and service.

  • ✓Organization verification and compliance are current.
  • ✓Relevant customs-broker licence or registration is valid.
  • ✓Importer/exporter authorization or power of attorney is in place where required.
  • ✓Named professionals hold the required local qualifications and permissions.
  • ✓Documents are accurate, current and shared with authorized users only.
  • ✓Clearance and release statuses are supported by authoritative records.
KRIM operating and legal boundary

KRIM provides a technology platform connecting customers with logistics partners. Partners provide actual services. KRIM platform verification does not confer a customs licence, appoint a customs representative, create a power of attorney, approve a declaration or guarantee clearance. Duties, taxes, regulatory decisions and release are determined by the competent authorities and the responsible parties.
