WORKSPACE 20 · SPECIALIST CAPABILITIES

Service-specific operating controls

Specialist handling is approved by service and qualification—not by a broad partner label.

01 · QUALIFICATION

Declare the exact service

Describe the cargo class, handling method, geography and operating limitations. Attach applicable licences, training or technical evidence.

02 · RESOURCES

Match equipment and personnel

Evidence fit-for-purpose equipment, trained personnel, insurance and handling procedures where applicable. Never infer capability from a generic category.

03 · CONTROLLED EXECUTION

Respect cargo-specific rules

Dangerous goods, temperature-controlled, oversized, high-value and project cargo require their own applicable approvals and controls before acceptance.
