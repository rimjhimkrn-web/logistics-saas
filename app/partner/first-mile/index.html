Operating authority

Collection control and handover standards

Keep collection execution traceable and within the agreed scope of service.

Before a first-mile collection

Confirm that the job, organization, resources and pickup instructions are aligned.

  • ✓Organization verification and compliance are current.
  • ✓First-mile service capability and pickup area are approved.
  • ✓Assignment, cargo details and service scope are confirmed.
  • ✓Vehicle, driver and capacity are authorized for the job.
  • ✓Pickup evidence and exception process are understood.
  • ✓Handover recipient and next transport leg are identified.
Important operating boundary

KRIM provides the platform and partner-network coordination. The logistics partner remains responsible for the services it undertakes. This workspace does not certify a route, confirm a rate, create a shipment assignment, grant a licence or authorize customs activity.
