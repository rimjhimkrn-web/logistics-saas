Measurement definitions

Comparable, documented methodology

• On-time delivery compares verified delivery time with the agreed delivery window.

• Milestone completeness measures required events supported by valid records.

• Exception incidence is reviewed in context and is not automatic proof of fault.

• Quality scores require defined criteria and a traceable review source.
