KRIM operating and commercial boundary

KRIM is the technology platform connecting customers with verified logistics partners. Partners provide the actual logistics services. Routes, rates, capacity and service coverage must be confirmed for the relevant partner and engagement. A displayed work area does not prove that a service is available or authorise a shipment action. KRIM does not represent unverified capability as confirmed service.
