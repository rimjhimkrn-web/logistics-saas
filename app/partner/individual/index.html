INDIVIDUAL PARTNER INTEREST

An expression of interest—not an operating approval.

Individual-interest registration helps KRIM understand your experience and potential service interest. It does not create a confirmed partner appointment or grant access to commercial assignments.

  • Profile and identity information reviewed separately
  • Eligibility depends on service and local requirements
  • Commercial activity requires explicit approval

INDIVIDUAL PARTICIPATION JOURNEY

Six work areas to guide your next steps.

Use these resources to understand the pathway, prepare accurate information and contact KRIM when you need assistance. They are guidance and reference areas, not confirmation of eligibility.

06 WORK AREAS
Participation boundary. Individual-interest registration records an expression of interest only. It does not make you a verified logistics provider, establish an employment or agency relationship, or authorise freight handling, customer commitments, quoting or shipment execution. Any future service relationship is subject to identity checks, applicable law, documented terms and KRIM approval.
