Operational runbook

Recommended investigation sequence

01 · Confirm subscriptionCheck that the endpoint and required event types match the intended partner integration.
02 · Inspect dispatch outcomeFilter failed or blocked dispatches and read the recorded failure code and reason.
03 · Follow the event traceCompare event time, status transition and dispatch identifier before escalation.
04 · Escalate securelyUse the existing KRIM support route. Never paste webhook secrets or access tokens into a ticket.
