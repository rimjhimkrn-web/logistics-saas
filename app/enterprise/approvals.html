Financial approval records may represent internal controls; KRIM does not claim to hold partner freight funds or execute partner payouts.

CONTROL PRINCIPLES

Decision safeguards

POLICY
Separation of dutiesRequired

Where dual control applies, requester and approver must be independently authorized. The interface does not infer eligibility.

Approval is not executionRequired

A recorded approval does not prove a payment, refund, bank transfer or external transaction has completed.

Currency integrityRequired

Every amount is displayed with its own currency code. No implicit FX conversion or cross-currency total is shown.
