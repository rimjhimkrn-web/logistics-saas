MARKET GOVERNANCE / FAIL-CLOSED CONTROL

Operational market activation

A market catalogue entry is not an operating licence. This control independently evaluates mandatory legal, tax, payments, privacy, safety and network readiness before an authorised two-person activation decision.

No market is activated by this page automatically. Activation requires every mandatory assessment to be approved with evidence, every mandatory requirement to have a reviewed source, active service locations and at least one verified active partner capability. A second administrator must approve the request.
