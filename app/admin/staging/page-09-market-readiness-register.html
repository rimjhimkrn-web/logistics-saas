Track the evidence and approvals required before a country, corridor or service can move toward operational activation. Maintain a single accountable readiness record, with explicit gaps, owners, source references and review dates.

◉ STAGING · READ-ONLY PREVIEW
Market governance · Readiness assurance

Do not activate a market on optimism. Activate it on verified readiness.

KRIM's architecture is global-ready, while market activation is evidence-led and phased. India is the initial activation focus. A country or corridor must not be represented as available until its legal, partner, service, operational, customer-support and safety requirements are assessed and approved for the specific scope.

!
Readiness and staging boundary

This preview has no live readiness register, market score, approval or activation decision. The controls shown are a governance framework, not a declaration that any country, route, partner or service is ready. Production decisions require current evidence and authorized review.

01 · Legal & regulatory

Jurisdictional operating prerequisites

Document the relevant laws, licences, registrations, customs obligations, data requirements and professional advice for the proposed scope.

  • Applicable entity and contracting model
  • Transport, customs and sector-specific permissions
  • Tax, consumer, privacy and records obligations
02 · Network readiness

Verified partner and capability coverage

Confirm that qualified service providers can actually deliver the proposed service on the named lanes and dates.

  • Partner identity, compliance and authority
  • Mode, service area and corridor evidence
  • Capacity confirmation and contingency coverage
03 · Customer & operations

Service delivery and support

Validate the complete customer journey, operational ownership, escalation paths and dependable service communications.

  • Booking, quote and exception handling
  • Customer support and mandatory journey notifications
  • Claims, incident and service continuity processes

Readiness review lifecycle

Illustrative governance · no live scores loaded
01 · Scope proposedDefine country, corridor, transport mode, service, customer segment and target launch date.
02 · Evidence collectedAssign each control to an owner and attach authoritative, current supporting evidence.
03 · Independent reviewResolve gaps, document residual risks and record approvals or explicit restrictions.
04 · Decision recordedApprove, defer or reject activation for the exact scope; re-review when evidence changes.

Readiness control domains

Each domain requires evidence, owner and review date
Regulatory & legal

Authority and compliance

Record official sources, applicable licences, regulatory interpretation, legal review, jurisdictional restrictions and unresolved compliance dependencies.

Partner & service

Real network capability

Confirm partner verification, permitted service scope, lane coverage, operational capacity, insurance and documented service commitments.

Technology & resilience

Operational controls

Validate market configuration, currency/time-zone handling, secure access, audit history, incident response, integrations and service continuity.

Customer readiness

Transparent customer journey

Review localized content, quote disclosures, support channels, complaint handling, customer notices and event-triggered shipment notifications.

Finance & commercial

Commercial and payment clarity

Confirm contracting, invoicing, tax treatment, partner settlement model, payment flows, currency exposure and approved commercial terms.

Safety & continuity

Risk and exception preparedness

Assess cargo, driver, vehicle, warehouse, security, claims, disruption and escalation controls relevant to the proposed service.
