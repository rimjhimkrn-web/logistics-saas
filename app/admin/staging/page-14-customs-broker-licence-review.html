Customs Broker Licence Review

Govern customs broker identity, licence evidence, issuing authority, jurisdiction, authorized scope, responsible personnel and continuing validity before a broker is represented as eligible for customs-related workflows.

◉ STAGING · READ-ONLY PREVIEW
Customs governance · Broker authorization

Confirm the authority, scope and validity behind every customs authorization.

A broker's licence is jurisdiction-specific and may be limited by activity, customs territory, entity, individual qualification or other conditions. Verification must be grounded in the competent authority's current records and must not imply that KRIM itself is licensed to perform customs brokerage.

01 · Identity

Broker and legal entity identity

Establish who holds the authorization and which legal entity is accountable for the service.

  • Legal name and entity registration details
  • Licence holder and authorized trading names
  • Registered office and customs jurisdiction
  • Relationship between broker, partner and personnel
02 · Authorization

Licence and issuing authority

Confirm the licence against the relevant competent customs authority or official register.

  • Licence number, type and issuing authority
  • Issue date, effective date and expiry date
  • Current status, conditions and restrictions
  • Official evidence reference and verification timestamp
03 · Scope

Permitted customs activities

Map precisely which customs activities and territories the authorization covers.

  • Import, export, transit or other authorized activities
  • Customs territory, ports and permitted locations
  • Goods, regimes or transaction-specific restrictions
  • Required qualified representatives or responsible officers

Core verification domains

Requirements differ by customs territory
Authority validation

Official status confirmation

Prefer direct authority registers or verifiable official communications. Record the source, date, method, reviewer and any limits of the verification.

Legal entity

Holder and business continuity

Confirm the authorization belongs to the contracted entity. Assess name changes, mergers, ownership changes, branch permissions and legal succession.

Personnel

Qualified responsible persons

Where required, verify individual licences, professional qualifications, responsible officer appointments and their current association with the broker.

Operational scope

Territory, port and activity limits

Record the exact customs territory, port, regime, goods category and service permissions. Never infer nationwide or cross-border scope from a narrower licence.

Integrity

Document authenticity and risk

Check source provenance, tampering indicators, duplicate licence numbers, inconsistent dates and unexplained differences between submitted and official details.

Ongoing oversight

Conditions and regulatory changes

Track licence conditions, authority notices, disciplinary restrictions, renewals and changes to the applicable customs regime.

Renewal and event monitoring

Maintain continuous eligibility controls
Expiry calendarTrack expiry and renewal deadlines for each licence and individual qualification separately.
Authority noticesRecord suspensions, revocations, conditions and material changes published by the authority.
Partner alertsNotify accountable broker contacts and KRIM reviewers, with delivery and escalation history.
ReverificationReassess after entity changes, personnel departures, scope changes or adverse events.

Approval and service safeguards

Licence review does not equal shipment clearance
Independent control

Maker / checker approval

Separate evidence submission from final approval. Prevent reviewers from approving their own submissions and retain a complete decision trail.

Service boundaries

Broker appointment and declaration authority

Confirm customer authorization, broker appointment, power of attorney or equivalent mandate where required before any declaration-related workflow.

Transaction controls

Shipment-specific checks

Validate goods, origin, destination, customs regime, documentation and restrictions for each shipment. A valid broker licence does not guarantee clearance or admissibility.

i
Do not present KRIM as the customs broker by implication

Customs brokerage must be performed by an appropriately authorized party in the relevant jurisdiction. KRIM should identify the actual service provider, preserve customer authorization and disclose the scope of its platform role. Country-specific requirements require qualified local review.
