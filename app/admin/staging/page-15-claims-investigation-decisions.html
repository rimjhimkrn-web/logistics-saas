Claims Investigation & Decisions

Provide a controlled, evidence-led process to register logistics claims, preserve records, investigate incidents, coordinate responsible parties and issue independently reviewed decisions with clear reasons and appeal paths.

◉ STAGING · READ-ONLY PREVIEW
Trust and resolution · Claims governance

Every claim deserves a fair, traceable and accountable decision.

Claims handling must distinguish customer reports, verified facts, contractual responsibility, insurance decisions and platform coordination. KRIM must not promise compensation, decide an insurer's liability or imply that a partner's obligations have been discharged without the applicable evidence and authority.

01 · Intake

Claim registration and triage

Capture a clear account of the reported issue and establish an accountable case owner.

  • Claim reference, reporting party and authorized contact
  • Shipment, order and service-provider references
  • Incident date, location, event type and description
  • Requested remedy and immediate safety concerns
02 · Evidence

Preservation and chain of custody

Preserve original records, provenance, access history and subsequent changes.

  • Photos, videos, invoices and delivery evidence
  • Pickup, handling, tracking and temperature records
  • Messages, inspection reports and witness statements
  • Secure access, retention and legal-hold controls
03 · Investigation

Facts, responsibility and coverage

Test the reported facts against contractual terms and evidence without assuming fault.

  • Event timeline and custody handoffs
  • Partner, carrier, warehouse and customer statements
  • Contractual obligations, exclusions and limitations
  • Insurance notification and independent expert review

Investigation workbench

Evidence-led assessment domains
Timeline reconstruction

Shipment event chronology

Reconcile booking, pickup, scans, custody transfers, warehouse events, exceptions, delivery and proof-of-delivery timestamps, accounting for time zones and clock discrepancies.

Damage and loss

Condition and quantity evidence

Compare declared goods, packaging, loading records, inspection findings, weights, photographs and delivery exceptions. Preserve uncertainty and conflicting accounts.

Service failure

Delay, missed service and disruption

Review agreed service scope, route events, operational notices, customer instructions, force majeure provisions and documented mitigation actions.

Financial review

Claim amount and supporting records

Reconcile invoices, declared value, repair or replacement evidence, salvage, deductibles and contractual limits. Flag unsupported or duplicated amounts.

Partner response

Statements and cooperation

Request responses from relevant service providers, record response deadlines and preserve the distinction between allegation, admission and independently verified fact.

Fraud and conflicts

Integrity and impartiality checks

Identify duplicate claims, altered records, inconsistent declarations, related-party conflicts and potential reviewer bias. Escalate suspected fraud through controlled channels.

Decision framework

Document reasons, authority and limits
SupportedEvidence and applicable contractual or policy basis support the claim in whole or in part.
Partially supportedOnly specified loss items or amounts are supported; excluded portions are explained.
Not supportedReasons, relevant terms, evidence gaps and review rights are stated clearly.
Pending / referredFurther evidence, insurer review, expert assessment or competent authority action is awaited.

Governance and customer safeguards

Fair process throughout the case
Independence

Maker / checker decision controls

Separate investigator and decision approver for consequential outcomes. A person must not approve their own investigation or a conflicted case.

Communication

Mandatory case updates

Send timely acknowledgements, evidence requests, status changes, decision notices and delay explanations through authorized channels. Track delivery and escalation.

Privacy

Restricted evidence access

Apply role- and case-based access, secure evidence links, retention schedules, redaction and lawful disclosure controls for sensitive customer and third-party information.

Review rights

Appeal and complaint route

Explain review deadlines, permitted grounds, independent reviewer assignment and external complaint or regulator options where applicable.

Financial boundary

Settlement authorization

Record who is legally responsible for any compensation or refund. Require separate financial authorization and proof of payment; a case decision alone does not move funds.

Auditability

Complete decision record

Retain evidence references, actions, communications, access logs, policy versions, reviewer identities, reasons and any later correction or appeal outcome.

i
Separate platform coordination from legal and insurance determinations

KRIM may coordinate information and case status within its agreed role. Contractual liability, insurance coverage, statutory rights and compensation must be determined by the responsible party or qualified authority under the applicable terms and law. Communicate verified facts, not assumptions.
