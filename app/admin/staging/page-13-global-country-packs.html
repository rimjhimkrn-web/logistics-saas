Global Country Packs

Build, review and govern the country-level operating packs that define what KRIM can safely support in each jurisdiction: legal requirements, service scope, partner evidence, operating controls, language, currency and local escalation paths.

Global market governance · Country readiness

One governed country pack. Clear evidence for every market decision.

Country packs are controlled, versioned operating baselines—not a claim that KRIM is licensed, active or operational in a country. Every market capability must be supported by current authoritative sources, qualified review and verified partner coverage before activation.

01 · Jurisdiction profile

Country and territory definition

Establish the precise legal and operating scope covered by the pack.

  • Country, territories and relevant subnational jurisdictions
  • Official country and jurisdiction identifiers
  • Local languages, time zones and date conventions
  • Currency, numeric formats and measurement units
02 · Regulatory baseline

Legal and regulatory requirements

Map requirements to official sources, responsible reviewers and evidence refresh dates.

  • Business, transport and intermediary requirements
  • Road, rail, air, ocean and warehouse rules as applicable
  • Customs, trade, sanctions and cargo restrictions
  • Privacy, consumer, tax and electronic record obligations
03 · Operating design

Local service and workflow rules

Define the workflows that may be offered only after the relevant capabilities are verified.

  • Permitted service categories and exclusions
  • Partner onboarding and evidence standards
  • Customer disclosures and notification requirements
  • Local support, incident and escalation ownership

Country pack modules

Evidence-backed and independently reviewable
Governance

Entity and regulatory position

Record applicable registrations, permits, responsible entities, licence dependencies and any restrictions on KRIM's role. Distinguish platform, broker, agent and carrier obligations.

Transport modes

Mode-specific requirements

Separate road, rail, air, ocean, warehouse and customs requirements. Do not enable a mode merely because a country pack exists.

Trade controls

Customs and cross-border rules

Capture import/export procedures, customs broker requirements, documentation, restricted goods and border-specific controls with authoritative references.

Commercial

Currency, taxes and payment rules

Define display currency, supported transaction currencies, tax treatment review, payment methods and settlement constraints. No rate or tax outcome is presumed.

Data and trust

Privacy and record handling

Map privacy notices, lawful processing, retention, data residency and cross-border transfer controls to applicable requirements and approved policies.

Customer operations

Localized service commitments

Set language coverage, local time windows, complaint handling, emergency escalation and mandatory shipment notifications for supported services.

Evidence and version governance

Trace every approval to its source
Official source registryReference the regulator or government source, publication date, URL and applicable jurisdiction.
Evidence ownerAssign an accountable reviewer, verification method and next review date to each requirement.
Version controlKeep immutable approval history, effective dates, change summaries and superseded versions.
Change impactIdentify dependent services, partners, corridors, documents and customer workflows for reassessment.

Market activation dependencies

Country pack approval is necessary, not sufficient
Partner network

Verified local supply

Require current partner identity, compliance, capability, service area, insurance and operational contact evidence for the exact service being offered.

Corridor readiness

Origin, destination and route

Confirm each corridor and border crossing, mode, restrictions, handoffs and responsible operating partners before presenting availability.

Operational readiness

People, systems and support

Verify localized workflows, customer communications, incident handling, time-zone coverage, finance controls and monitoring before activation.

i
Global-ready architecture does not mean global authorization

Country packs must be prepared and reviewed market by market. KRIM's initial market activation remains India; expansion to other countries requires independent legal, partner, corridor and operational readiness checks. No service, partner coverage or country availability should be advertised until confirmed.
