Capacity Resource Review

Review partner-declared transport and handling resources, verify evidence and scope, and govern whether a specific capacity record may be considered for a particular service, location, mode and time window.

◉ STAGING · READ-ONLY PREVIEW
Network resource governance · Capacity assurance

Make capacity visible only when its source, scope and validity are verified.

Capacity is not a permanent partner attribute or a platform promise. It is a time-bound, location-specific resource declaration that must be supported by current evidence and confirmed by the responsible partner before it is offered or assigned.

01 · Resource identity

What resource is being declared?

Maintain a distinct record for each resource or defined pool, with a traceable owner and evidence source.

  • Partner, legal entity and responsible contact
  • Resource type, quantity and unique identifiers
  • Vehicle, trailer, container and equipment details
  • Warehouse space, handling assets and facilities
02 · Operational scope

Where and how can it be used?

Scope resources to the exact service and operating conditions supported by verification.

  • Country, state, city, facility and service area
  • Transport mode and permitted cargo classes
  • Payload, dimensions and technical constraints
  • Temperature, hazardous goods or special handling
03 · Availability window

When is it potentially available?

Separate a partner's declaration from a live, binding confirmation for a specific job.

  • Effective from and until timestamps with time zone
  • Capacity units and measurement basis
  • Maintenance, blackout and operating windows
  • Last partner confirmation and freshness threshold

Resource categories and verification

Evidence and restrictions vary by resource class
Road transport

Vehicles and trailers

Verify registration, fitness, insurance, permits, vehicle class, payload, dimensions, axle configuration and any specialist equipment. A vehicle record does not establish driver availability.

Rail and intermodal

Wagons and containers

Review equipment identity, ownership or control, technical compatibility, permitted cargo, interchange requirements and terminal acceptance evidence.

Air and ocean

Booked or partner-declared capacity

Distinguish indicative allocations from carrier-confirmed space. Verify carrier, service, route, cut-off, equipment and validity directly with the authorized provider.

Warehousing

Facility and storage capacity

Check usable area or pallet positions, facility authorization, operating hours, cargo restrictions, temperature range, fire and safety evidence, and occupancy freshness.

Handling equipment

Specialist operational assets

Verify cranes, forklifts, reefer plugs, loading equipment and other assets against inspection records, safe working limits, operator requirements and service location.

People and teams

Driver and workforce readiness

Keep personnel qualification, identity, licence and duty-hour controls in their respective verification workflows. Do not count a resource as deployable solely because a partner profile lists staff.

Capacity integrity controls

Prevent stale, duplicated or overstated supply
Evidence provenanceRecord issuer, source, reviewer, capture date, document version and verification outcome.
Duplicate detectionFlag repeated vehicle identifiers, overlapping resource records and inconsistent partner ownership.
Unit normalizationUse explicit units, dimensions, time zones and conversion rules; never mix incompatible capacity measures.
Freshness policySet category-specific reconfirmation periods and automatically flag stale declarations.
Conflict controlsDetect overlapping allocations, maintenance windows, double booking and inconsistent availability claims.
Change historyPreserve prior values, approvals, evidence and reason codes when a resource is amended or withdrawn.
Independent reviewRequire maker/checker approval for consequential verification; a creator cannot approve their own work.
Alert escalationNotify responsible operations and partner contacts about expiry, discrepancy, suspension and reconfirmation needs.

Downstream use and release gates

Verified resource is not a confirmed booking
Discovery gate

Qualified network visibility

Display only verified resources within their permitted geography, mode, cargo class, service scope and validity period. Label indicative information accurately.

Quotation gate

Partner-specific confirmation

Before quoting or promising service, request a current partner confirmation for the requested origin, destination, cargo, quantity and time window.

Assignment gate

Prevent double allocation

Recheck resource status, competing allocations, driver and equipment readiness, operational restrictions and partner acceptance before assignment.

Governance

Audit and customer transparency

Record who verified and confirmed each claim. Trigger required customer updates when a confirmed service changes, is delayed or becomes unavailable.

i
Capacity declarations must never become automatic service promises

A verified resource record is evidence of a scoped declaration, not proof that it remains free or can fulfil a shipment. Availability, pricing, route feasibility and acceptance must be reconfirmed with the actual partner for each requested movement.
