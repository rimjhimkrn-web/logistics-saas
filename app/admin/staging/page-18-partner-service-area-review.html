Partner Service-area Review

Govern the exact geographic areas and service scopes each logistics partner is authorized and demonstrably able to serve. Review evidence at country, state, city, postal zone, facility and corridor level before coverage is presented to customers.

◉ STAGING · READ-ONLY PREVIEW
Network coverage governance · Geographic assurance

Show only the service areas a verified partner can actually serve.

Partner coverage is not a broad marketing claim or an assumed radius. It is a scoped, evidence-backed authorization that varies by mode, cargo, facility, operating hours, route and local regulatory requirements. Every published service area must have a responsible partner and current supporting evidence.

01 · Geographic definition

Precisely define the territory

Use structured geographic identifiers rather than ambiguous free-text coverage claims.

  • Country, state, province and district
  • City, locality, postal or PIN code
  • Named facilities, terminals and service points
  • Polygon, radius or route segment where appropriate
02 · Service scope

What can the partner do there?

Associate every area with the specific service, mode and conditions verified for that partner.

  • Road, rail, air, ocean and multimodal services
  • Pickup, delivery, linehaul and cross-dock
  • Warehouse, customs and specialist handling
  • Cargo classes, vehicle types and exclusions
03 · Evidence and ownership

Who supports the coverage claim?

Require accountable partner confirmation and evidence appropriate to the operating model.

  • Partner legal entity and authorized contact
  • Local permits, licences and facility evidence
  • Subcontractor and agent relationships
  • Source, reviewer, validity and renewal dates

Coverage review dimensions

Every territory has its own operating constraints
Pan-India and international

Country and administrative hierarchy

Validate country and subnational divisions using recognized identifiers. Do not infer nationwide coverage from a single office, registration or service location.

Local serviceability

City, district and postal zones

Review locality-level pickup and delivery limits, remote-area exclusions, postal code accuracy, operating hours and any last-mile restrictions.

Corridor-linked

Origin, destination and transit

Connect service areas to verified corridors and authorized route segments. A partner's presence at both endpoints does not prove the route between them.

Facility coverage

Warehouse and terminal catchments

Check facility identity, permitted activities, actual catchment, handling equipment, opening hours, cargo acceptance and current operating status.

Mode and cargo

Conditional service eligibility

Keep coverage distinct for each mode and cargo class, including temperature-controlled, oversized, hazardous or otherwise restricted consignments.

Partner network

Agents and subcontractors

Record whether service is direct or subcontracted, verify the responsible provider and authorization, and disclose material service-chain limitations.

Integrity, freshness and controls

Prevent overstatement and unsupported expansion
Boundary precisionStore authoritative geographic identifiers, boundary versions and explicit inclusion or exclusion rules.
Evidence provenancePreserve source documents, issuer, reviewer, capture date and partner attestation.
Expiry monitoringAlert on expiring licences, permits, facility approvals and overdue partner reconfirmations.
Overlap detectionIdentify conflicting territories, duplicate postal zones and inconsistent mode or service declarations.
Change impactReassess coverage after partner changes, regulatory updates, incidents or repeated failed service attempts.
Subcontractor traceabilityMaintain accountable parties and effective dates for each delegated local service arrangement.
Independent approvalRequire maker/checker review for material coverage changes; creators cannot approve their own submissions.
Audit historyKeep prior scope, evidence, decision rationale, approver and effective dates for every revision.

Publication and customer-facing release gates

Coverage is not capacity or a confirmed booking
Discovery gate

Accurate service-area display

Publish only approved territory-service combinations and make material exclusions, limitations and subcontracted delivery arrangements clear.

Enquiry gate

Validate the requested addresses

Check exact pickup and delivery points, cargo, mode, service type and requested date against the partner's current approved scope.

Booking gate

Obtain partner acceptance

Confirm actual route feasibility, capacity, pricing, local provider availability and acceptance before presenting a service as booked or guaranteed.

Customer operations

Manage exceptions transparently

Trigger required customer notifications if a partner withdraws coverage, a service becomes unavailable or an accepted movement is materially affected.

i
Never convert a partner's broad coverage statement into a KRIM service guarantee

Each customer request must be checked against exact addresses, mode, cargo, route, date and partner acceptance. Service-area approval alone does not establish available capacity, a rate, a confirmed route or an operational assignment.
