Warehouse Facility Review

Govern warehouse, fulfilment, cross-dock, cold-chain and specialist storage facilities before they are represented as eligible nodes in the KRIM network. Review facility identity, operator authority, premises evidence, permitted activities, safety, equipment and service limitations.

◉ STAGING · READ-ONLY PREVIEW
Physical network infrastructure · Facility assurance

Make every facility a verified, scoped and accountable network node.

A facility listing, address or partner declaration does not prove that a site is authorized, operational or suitable for a particular cargo. Approval must be tied to the legal operator, exact premises, permitted activities, equipment, safety controls, operating hours and evidence validity.

01 · Facility identity

Establish the real premises

Bind each facility record to an identifiable site and accountable legal operator.

  • Facility name, unique ID and facility type
  • Full address, geocode and postal identifier
  • Legal owner, operator and responsible contact
  • Lease, occupancy or premises-control evidence
02 · Legal authorization

Verify permitted activities

Review applicable registrations, licences and local permissions for the specific site and activity.

  • Business and premises registrations
  • Fire, building and safety approvals
  • Environmental and local operating permissions
  • Bonded, customs or regulated storage authority
03 · Operating capability

Validate actual facility capability

Confirm physical infrastructure and operating processes match the proposed service scope.

  • Storage capacity and usable area
  • Dock, handling and material equipment
  • Temperature, security and monitoring systems
  • Staffing, hours and emergency arrangements

Facility types and specialized controls

Different premises require different evidence
General storage

Warehousing and fulfilment

Review racking, floor loading, fire protection, access control, pest management, inventory controls, receiving and dispatch processes, and capacity evidence.

Cross-dock and transit

Transfer and consolidation hubs

Validate dock availability, vehicle access, staging space, segregation, scanning, custody transfer, operating windows and safe handling processes.

Temperature controlled

Cold-chain facilities

Check validated temperature zones, calibration, continuous monitoring, alarm escalation, backup power, excursion procedures and product-specific acceptance.

Customs and bonded

Regulated storage premises

Confirm site-specific customs or bonded authorization, access restrictions, stock segregation, customs recordkeeping and controlled release procedures.

Special cargo

Hazardous and high-value storage

Require applicable dangerous-goods permissions, segregation, containment, fire controls, secure access, surveillance and trained personnel.

Specialist handling

Project, oversized and sensitive cargo

Assess lifting equipment, safe working loads, clearances, floor strength, weather protection, specialist handling procedures and qualified operators.

Evidence, inspections and compliance controls

Traceable facility-level assurance

Premises consistencyEnsure certificates, address, operator and approved activities refer to the same physical site.
Inspection findingsRecord inspector, date, scope, evidence, deficiencies, severity and corrective-action deadlines.
Safety readinessReview emergency exits, fire systems, incident response, training and statutory inspections.
Equipment assuranceTrack equipment inventory, inspection, maintenance, calibration and safe working limits.
Environmental controlsReview waste, emissions, water, hazardous materials and other applicable environmental obligations.
Insurance and liabilityValidate coverage, insured entity, premises, activities, limits and policy validity as applicable.
Independent approvalEnforce maker/checker separation, reasoned decisions, version history and immutable audit events.

Capacity, availability and network integration

Facility approval does not guarantee available space
Capacity model

Separate nominal and usable capacity

Record units and measurement basis, storage type, occupied or blocked space, operational reserve and the date of the last verified capacity assessment.

Service eligibility

Scope by cargo and activity

Only approved handling, storage, packaging, fulfilment and value-added services may be shown. Apply exclusions for incompatible cargo or unsupported processes.

Network linkage

Connect to service areas and lanes

Associate the facility with validated geographic coverage and lane segments. Check access, operating hours, transfer capability and provider responsibility.

Operational release

Reconfirm before allocation

Check current operating status, actual space, labour, equipment, cargo acceptance and partner confirmation before promising or assigning facility capacity.

i
Facility approval is not a live inventory or capacity commitment

Before allocating a shipment, confirm current available space, cargo compatibility, handling resources, opening hours, site access and operator acceptance. Any expired licence, failed inspection, safety incident or material change must trigger reassessment and, where appropriate, immediate suspension.
