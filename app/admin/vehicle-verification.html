Country & vehicle document requirements

Configure the required evidence profile before any vehicle can be verified. For India road goods-carriage vehicles, review registration certificate, valid insurance, applicable goods-carriage permit, transport-vehicle fitness, valid PUC/emissions certificate and vehicle tax certificate/token. The exact permit and tax evidence must match the operating route and jurisdiction; driver licence evidence is reviewed separately. Use category code goods_carriage for India road freight. Hazardous/dangerous-goods vehicles require additional evidence under applicable Central Motor Vehicles Rules (including Rules 132–133); do not approve them under the ordinary goods-carriage baseline until a separate category-specific control profile is configured. Do not configure a document as optional merely to bypass a missing record.

Official references: Jharkhand traffic document list · Jharkhand vehicle fitness · Jharkhand goods-carriage permits · Central Road Regulations, Rule 38.
