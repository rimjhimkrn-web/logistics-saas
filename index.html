04 / Shipment lifecycle

From first details
to final handoff.

A disciplined operating sequence. Available actions depend on service readiness, shipment status and user authority.

STAGE 01

Define

Capture cargo, locations, mode, timing and handling requirements.

STAGE 02

Agree

Review serviceability, quote terms and applicable payment conditions.

STAGE 03

Assign

Confirm capacity, responsible partner and execution handoffs.

STAGE 04

Coordinate

Record movement, documents, milestones and exceptions.

STAGE 05

Close

Capture delivery evidence and complete required financial checks.

05 / Operating standards

Serious operations.
Evidence over assumptions.

Global ambition requires disciplined execution. Access, operational readiness, delivery proof and service limitations must remain explicit.

Capability verified

Confirm route, partner, capacity and service terms before accepting a shipment.

Access controlled

Keep customer, partner, field, finance and privileged access separated.

Events traceable

Record important state changes, exceptions and operational decisions.

Outcomes tested

Verify database writes, payment confirmation and delivery evidence end to end.
