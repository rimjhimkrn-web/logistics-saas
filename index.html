System architecture

A shared core. Separate responsibilities. Controlled handoffs.

KRIM is organised around a common operational foundation, with dedicated workspaces for customers, logistics providers, field teams and business functions. Access to sensitive actions and records must remain role-based and organisation-scoped.

A disciplined shipment lifecycle

Use the lifecycle as an operating framework; the actual next action depends on the shipment, service, market and authorised team.

Define the shipment

Capture cargo, locations, mode, timing and handling requirements.

Assign execution

Manage movement

STAGE 05

Close with evidence

Capture delivery evidence and complete applicable financial checks.

Operating principles

Designed for serious, long-term operations.

A global ambition requires clear controls, reliable workflows and honest visibility into what is—and is not—ready.

01

Global by architecture, activated by evidence

Design for different countries, currencies, modes and operating contexts. Enable actual services only after market, partner, legal and operational readiness is checked.

02

Customer needs before unnecessary complexity

Prioritise clear requests, transparent terms, dependable updates, service quality and useful support throughout the shipment journey.

03

Security and accountability by design

Protect organisational data, restrict privileged actions and keep operational decisions traceable to authorised users and workflows.

04

Verify before calling a workflow complete

Test real identity, database writes, partner assignment, payment confirmation, shipment updates and delivery evidence before relying on them in production.
