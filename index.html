Clarity matters

Questions, answered.

Can I book a shipment anywhere in the world?

Not automatically. KRIM's architecture is global-ready, but each route must be checked for partner availability, legal requirements and service readiness.

Does KRIM show live tracking?

Use the tracking page for a shipment reference. Live status is available only when the shipment record and tracking integrations provide verified updates.

How do I get a price?

Submit shipment details through the quote request page. A final price should be accepted only after the relevant service and partner costs are confirmed.

How can my logistics business join?

Register or contact KRIM to begin partner onboarding. Documents, service capability, route coverage and commercial terms need verification before activation.
