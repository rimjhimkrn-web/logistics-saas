Clear expectations

Before you get started.

Is every country and route live?

No. KRIM is designed for global expansion, but a route should be offered only after service, partner, compliance and payment readiness is verified.

Does a workspace link grant access?

No. The signed-in identity, active membership and backend permissions determine access.

Can I track a shipment?

Use the tracking page with your shipment reference. Only recorded live milestones can be shown as confirmed progress.

How do I join as a logistics partner?

Start partner registration and provide accurate business and service information for review.
