From request to delivery

A clear shipment journey.

The exact sequence varies by service and route. A request is not a confirmed booking; provider availability, quote, terms and charges must be accepted before a shipment commitment.

STEP 01

Share requirements

Provide origin, destination, cargo and timing details.

STEP 02

Review options

Review available service, provider, quote and terms.

STEP 03

Confirm arrangements

Agree responsibilities, capacity and applicable charges.

STEP 04

Coordinate movement

Follow assigned partner and available milestones.

STEP 05

Complete & reconcile

Record delivery evidence and financial close-out.
