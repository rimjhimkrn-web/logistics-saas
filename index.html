Frequently asked questions

Important things to know.

A straightforward view of KRIM's operating approach and current market activation.

Is KRIM only an India logistics platform?

No. KRIM is being designed as a global-ready operating system. India is the first market activation; additional markets will be activated as partner coverage, legal requirements and operations are ready.

Can I request a shipment anywhere in the world?

You can submit an enquiry, but submission does not mean every route is serviceable. KRIM must confirm the route, provider availability, applicable requirements, terms and price before accepting a shipment commitment.

Does KRIM operate its own trucks, aircraft or warehouses?

KRIM's model is to connect customers with logistics service providers and coordinate workflows through technology. Asset ownership and service responsibility depend on the contracted provider for the shipment.

How are logistics partners onboarded?

Prospective partners are subject to information collection, capability review, document and business verification as applicable, commercial agreement and operational approval before activation.

Are prices fixed on the website?

Freight pricing depends on route, cargo, mode, capacity, timing and provider terms. A price should only be treated as confirmed when a valid quote is issued and accepted through the applicable workflow.

How do I contact KRIM?

Use the contact page for business, customer or partner enquiries. Do not send passwords, payment credentials or other sensitive authentication information through public forms.
