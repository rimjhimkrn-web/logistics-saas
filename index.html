A connected operating journey

From first request to final handoff.

Every stage should have a clear owner, a recorded decision and a truthful status. The platform should never present a planned step as a completed real-world event.

01 / DEFINE

Describe the shipment

Capture origin, destination, cargo, timing, handling needs and required documents.

02 / CONFIRM

Validate quote & capacity

Review applicable rates, service availability, partner readiness and terms before acceptance.

03 / EXECUTE

Coordinate fulfilment

Connect authorized partner, driver, warehouse and customs workflows as needed.

04 / VERIFY

Record delivery evidence

Use actual milestones and delivery proof to close the loop and support finance review.

Clear expectations

Trust starts with transparency.

Is every country and route already live?

No. Global expansion is the design goal. Actual service must be confirmed route by route after partner, compliance, operational and payment checks.

Does a workspace link automatically grant access?

No. Your signed-in identity, active organization membership and backend permissions determine access.

Can I see live shipment tracking?

Use the tracking page with a valid shipment reference. Only recorded milestones should be shown as confirmed progress.

How can a logistics business join?

Start partner onboarding and provide accurate business, service-area and contact information for verification.

KRIM's global-ready design does not imply that every route, country, carrier, payment method or logistics service is currently available. Confirmed availability depends on verified operational readiness.
