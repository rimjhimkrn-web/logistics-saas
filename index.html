Clear expectations

Before you get started.

Is every country and route live?

No. The platform is designed for global expansion, but service is available only where partner coverage, compliance and operations have been verified.

Can I choose a role and get its permissions?

No. Selecting a workspace only helps route you to sign-in. Your real access depends on verified identity and backend permissions.

How do partners join?

Start with partner registration. Carrier details and eligibility may need review before assignments are enabled.

Where do I track a shipment?

Use Track Shipment and enter the requested tracking reference. Results depend on an available, authorized tracking record.
