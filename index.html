From request to delivery

Make every handoff easier to follow.

The exact steps vary by service, market and shipment. This is the operating sequence—not a promise that every route or service is currently available.

STEP 01

Enquire

Share origin, destination, cargo and timing requirements.

STEP 02

Review & quote

Review the available service, route, terms and quote.

STEP 03

Confirm booking

Proceed only after applicable details and charges are confirmed.

STEP 04

Coordinate execution

Use assigned partner and operations workflows for milestones.

STEP 05

Complete & reconcile

Capture delivery evidence and follow applicable finance workflows.
