Shipping & Service Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Our Logistics Service

Our platform connects customers with logistics partners to arrange transportation services for eligible shipments.

2. Shipment Booking

Customers must provide accurate pickup location, delivery location, goods information, weight, vehicle requirements and contact details when requesting a shipment.

3. Vehicle Assignment

A suitable logistics partner and vehicle may be assigned based on availability, route, shipment requirements and other operational factors.

4. Pickup

Pickup arrangements will be communicated to the customer where applicable. Customers should ensure that the shipment is properly packed and ready for collection at the agreed location.

5. Delivery

Shipments will be transported to the delivery location provided by the customer. The recipient may be required to provide identification or other reasonable confirmation of delivery.

6. Delivery Timelines

Estimated delivery times may vary depending on distance, traffic, weather, vehicle availability, road conditions, regulations and other operational circumstances.

7. Tracking

Customers may receive a tracking number that can be used to view shipment status through the platform.

8. Shipment Restrictions

Customers must not request transportation of illegal, prohibited, dangerous or restricted goods unless such transportation is legally permitted and properly declared.

9. Customer Responsibilities

  • Provide accurate shipment information.
  • Ensure goods are properly packed where applicable.
  • Make the shipment available at the agreed pickup time.
  • Provide accurate delivery information.
  • Disclose relevant information about the goods.

10. Delays

Delays may occur because of traffic, weather, vehicle breakdown, regulatory requirements, road conditions, operational issues or other circumstances beyond reasonable control.

11. Proof of Delivery

Where applicable, delivery may be confirmed through recipient details, delivery notes, electronic records or other proof of delivery.

12. Service Charges

Applicable charges will be communicated through the quotation or agreed service terms before the shipment is processed.

13. Cancellation

Cancellation requests are handled according to our Cancellation & Refund Policy and the stage of the shipment.

14. Contact

For questions regarding a shipment or our logistics services, please contact us using the contact information provided on our website.
